# Plant data sources

Plant data release 2026.1.

- Govaerts R (ed.). 2026. WCVP: World Checklist of Vascular Plants. Facilitated by the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew. [WWW document] URL https://doi.org/10.34885/egs6-cp24 [accessed 04 Jun 2026]. Govaerts, R., Nic Lughadha, E. et al. The World Checklist of Vascular Plants, a continuously updated resource for exploring global plant diversity. Sci Data 8, 215 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-021-00997-6. Licence: CC BY 3.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0). Grouped into countries and counted by Make Me Smarter; Kew does not endorse Make Me Smarter.
- Botanical region codes: TDWG World Geographical Scheme for Recording Plant Distributions, 2nd edition (Brummitt, 2001). CC BY 4.0. Matched to countries by Make Me Smarter; region names and outlines are not used.
- FAO. 2025. FAOSTAT: Crops and Livestock Products. Accessed on 24 October 2026. https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/QCL. Licence: CC BY 4.0. Grouped and rounded by Make Me Smarter.
- Wikidata (CC0): plant items, their Commons images and Wikipedia links.
- Wikimedia pageviews (CC0), used only to suggest well-known plants for the lists.
- Photos from Wikimedia Commons, each credited with its author and licence; resized, cropped square and converted to WebP by Make Me Smarter, each copy keeping the original's licence.
- Country shapes: Natural Earth (public domain), as in World geography.
- The globe's relief, drawn by Make Me Smarter as in World geography from: GMTED2010 courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey (Danielson and Gesch 2011, USGS Open-File Report 2011-1073); GEBCO Bathymetric Compilation Group 2026 (2026), The GEBCO_2026 Grid, NERC EDS British Oceanographic Data Centre NOC, doi:10.5285/4f68d5c7-45eb-f999-e063-7086abc036fa (ocean depths only); Friedl, M., Sulla-Menashe, D. (2022), MODIS/Terra+Aqua Land Cover Type Yearly L3 Global 500m SIN Grid V061, NASA EOSDIS Land Processes DAAC, doi:10.5067/MODIS/MCD12Q1.061.
- Plant lists, facts in our own words and Hindi name choices: Make Me Smarter (CC BY 4.0). For learning, not for identifying, eating or treating plants.
