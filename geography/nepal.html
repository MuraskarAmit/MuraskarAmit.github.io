Nepal: map and facts

Nepal is in Southern Asia. It has about 29.6 million people (2025), the 52nd largest population. It covers 147,181 km², the 93rd largest by area. That is about 201 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Everest, at 8,849 m. The longest river on its map is the Gandak, 630 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 29,618,118 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 147,181 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Kathmandu
  • Highest point: Mount Everest, 8,849 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 43.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Nepal, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Gandak: 630 km

Also on the map: Ghaghara and Kosi.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Everest: 8,849 m (its highest point)
  • Kanchenjunga: 8,586 m
  • Dhaulagiri: 8,167 m

Mountain ranges: Himalayas and Siwalik Hills.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): mixed forest 21.3%, wooded savanna 17.6%, grassland 16.7%, farmland 14.1%, savanna 10.5%, bare ground 8.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 47%, high mountains 34%, plains 12.6%, hills 6.3%.

Forests covered 43.6% of its land in 2024, up from 40.2% in 1990 (World Bank).
