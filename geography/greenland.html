Greenland: map and facts

Greenland is in Northern America. It has 56,831 people (2025). It covers 2,166,086 km². That is about 0.03 people per km². Its highest point is Gunnbjørn Fjeld, at 3,694 m.

Facts

  • Population: 56,831 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 2,166,086 km² (UN Demographic Yearbook)
  • Capital: Nuuk
  • Highest point: Gunnbjørn Fjeld, 3,694 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Gunnbjørn Fjeld: 3,694 m (its highest point)
  • Mont Forel: 3,383 m
  • Perserajoq: 2,259 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Knud Rasmussen Land, Crown Prince Christian Land, Jameson Land, King Christian IX Land, King Christian X Land, King Frederik VI Coast, King Frederik VIII Land, King Wilhelm Land, Nyeboe Land and Peary Land.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): snow and ice 86.4%, bare ground 6.2%, grassland 5.2%, water 1.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 62.9%, hills 18.9%, mountains 17.3%.

Forests covered 0% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).
