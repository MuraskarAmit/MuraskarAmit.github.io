Djibouti: map and facts

Djibouti is in Eastern Africa. It has about 1.18 million people (2025), the 156th largest population. It covers 23,200 km², the 146th largest by area. That is about 51 people per km². Its highest point is Moussa Ali, at 2,028 m.

Facts

  • Population: 1,184,076 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 23,200 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 21,900 km².
  • Capital: Djibouti
  • Highest point: Moussa Ali, 2,028 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Moussa Ali: 2,028 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Danakil Desert.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 52%, grassland 30.1%, open shrubland 16.2%, water 1.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 55.5%, mountains 31.7%, plains 7.2%, plateaus 5.6%.

Forests covered 0.3% of its land in 2024, up from 0.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Djibouti shares land borders with Eritrea, Ethiopia and Somalia on this map.
