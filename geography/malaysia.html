Malaysia: map and facts

Malaysia is in South-Eastern Asia. It has about 36 million people (2025), the 45th largest population. It covers 330,534 km², the 67th largest by area. That is about 109 people per km². Its highest point is Gunung Kinabalu, at 4,095 m. The longest river on its map is the Pahang, 459 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 35,977,838 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 330,534 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Kuala Lumpur
  • Highest point: Gunung Kinabalu, 4,095 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 57.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Malaysia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Pahang: 459 km
  • Baram: 400 km

Also on the map: Bajang and Igan.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Gunung Kinabalu: 4,095 m (its highest point)
  • Gunung Tahan: 2,187 m
  • Bukit Pagon: 1,850 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Malay Archipelago, Greater Sunda Islands, Malay Peninsula and Datu Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 72.8%, wooded savanna 18.2%, wetland 1.8%, towns and cities 1.6%, grassland 1.5%, savanna 1.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 36.5%, plains 32.1%, hills 31.3%.

Forests covered 57.7% of its land in 2024, down from 62.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #10 in tin among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Malaysia shares land borders with Brunei, Indonesia and Thailand on this map.
