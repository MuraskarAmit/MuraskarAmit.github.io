Madagascar: map and facts

Madagascar is in Eastern Africa. It has about 32.7 million people (2025), the 49th largest population. It covers 591,896 km², the 47th largest by area. That is about 55 people per km². Its highest point is Maromokotro, at 2,876 m. The longest river on its map is the Betsiboka, 600 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 32,740,678 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 591,896 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Antananarivo
  • Highest point: Maromokotro, 2,876 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 17.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Madagascar, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Betsiboka: 600 km
  • Mangoky: 564 km
  • Onilahy: 400 km
  • Maningory: 130 km

Also on the map: Mananantanana, Mania and Tsiribihina.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Maromokotro: 2,876 m (its highest point)
  • Pic Boby: 2,658 m

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 67.2%, savanna 10.4%, evergreen broadleaf forest 8.8%, wooded savanna 8.3%, open shrubland 1.9%, deciduous broadleaf forest 1.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 47.8%, plains 24.1%, mountains 20.6%, plateaus 7.5%.

Forests covered 17.3% of its land in 2024, down from 23.5% in 1990 (World Bank).
