Tanzania: map and facts

Tanzania is in Eastern Africa. It has about 70.5 million people (2025), the 21st largest population. It covers 945,500 km², the 30th largest by area. That is about 75 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Kilimanjaro, at 5,895 m. The longest river on its map is the Ruvuma, 800 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 70,545,865 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 945,500 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Dodoma
  • Highest point: Mount Kilimanjaro, 5,895 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 49.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Tanzania, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Ruvuma: 800 km
  • Rufiji: 600 km
  • Wami: 545 km
  • Great Ruaha: 480 km
  • Malagarasi: 475 km
  • Pangani: 420 km
  • Kagera: 400 km

Also on the map: Luwegu, Ugalla, Shama, Rungwa, Igombe and Gombe.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Kilimanjaro: 5,895 m (its highest point)
  • Loolmalasin: 3,682 m
  • Mtorwi: 2,961 m
  • Kimhandu: 2,653 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Great Rift Valley, Iwembere Steppe, Masai Steppe and Serengeti Plains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 40.8%, savanna 40.4%, water 6.3%, mixed forest 3.2%, deciduous broadleaf forest 2.9%, evergreen broadleaf forest 2.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 44.2%, hills 37.7%, mountains 10.2%, plains 7.6%.

Forests covered 49.6% of its land in 2024, down from 65.7% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #3 in graphite among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Tanzania shares land borders with Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Rwanda, Uganda and Zambia on this map.
