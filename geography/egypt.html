Egypt: map and facts

Egypt is in Northern Africa. It has about 118 million people (2025), the 13th largest population. It covers 1,001,450 km², the 29th largest by area. That is about 118 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Catherine, at 2,637 m. The longest river on its map is the Nile, 6,650 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 118,365,995 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,001,450 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Cairo
  • Highest point: Mount Catherine, 2,637 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Egypt, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Nile: 6,650 km
  • Damietta Branch: 240 km

Also on the map: Rosetta Branch.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Catherine: 2,637 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Sahara, Libyan Desert, Western Desert, Nile Delta, Eastern Desert, Gilf Kebir Plateau and Libyan Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 94.2%, farmland 3%, grassland 1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 51.4%, plateaus 28%, hills 17.9%, mountains 2.7%.

Forests covered 0% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #7 in phosphate among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Egypt shares land borders with Bir Tawil, Israel, Libya, Palestine and Sudan on this map.
