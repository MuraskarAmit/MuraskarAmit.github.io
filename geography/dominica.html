Dominica: map and facts

Dominica is in the Caribbean. It has 65,871 people (2025), the 185th largest population. It covers 751 km², the 173rd largest by area. That is about 88 people per km². Its highest point is Morne Diablotins, at 1,447 m.

Facts

  • Population: 65,871 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 751 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 732 km².
  • Capital: Roseau
  • Highest point: Morne Diablotins, 1,447 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 76.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Morne Diablotins: 1,447 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 90.7%, water 3.7%, grassland 2.9%, wooded savanna 1.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 98.4%, hills 1.6%.

Forests covered 76.4% of its land in 2024, down from 78.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Dominica has no land borders on this map.
