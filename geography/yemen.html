Yemen: map and facts

Yemen is in Western Asia. It has about 41.8 million people (2025), the 37th largest population. It covers 527,968 km², the 50th largest by area. That is about 79 people per km². Its highest point is Jabal an-Nabi Shu’aib, at 3,666 m.

Facts

  • Population: 41,773,878 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 527,968 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 455,000 km².
  • Capital: Aden, Sanaa
  • Highest point: Jabal an-Nabi Shu’aib, 3,666 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Jabal an-Nabi Shu’aib: 3,666 m (its highest point)
  • Jabal Jihaf: 3,277 m

Mountain ranges: Asir Mountains and Hadhramaut.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Arabian Peninsula, Rub' al Khali and Tihamah.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 80.6%, open shrubland 10.6%, grassland 6.9%, farmland 1.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 40.5%, hills 31.1%, mountains 21.7%, plains 5.7%, high mountains 1%.

Forests covered 1% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Yemen shares land borders with Oman and Saudi Arabia on this map.
