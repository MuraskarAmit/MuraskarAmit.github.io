Eritrea: map and facts

Eritrea is in Eastern Africa. It has about 3.61 million people (2025), the 129th largest population. It covers 121,630 km², the 97th largest by area. That is about 30 people per km². Its highest point is Soira, at 3,018 m. The longest river on its map is the Setit, 608 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 3,607,003 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 121,630 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Asmara
  • Highest point: Soira, 3,018 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 12% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Eritrea, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Setit: 608 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Soira: 3,018 m (its highest point)
  • Jabal Hamoyet: 2,726 m
  • Mount Ramlo: 2,248 m
  • Moussa Ali: 2,028 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Danakil Desert and Dahlak Archipelago.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 44.9%, grassland 40%, open shrubland 10.7%, farmland 3.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 38.2%, hills 37.6%, plains 12%, plateaus 12%.

Forests covered 12% of its land in 2024, down from 16.1% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Eritrea shares land borders with Djibouti, Ethiopia and Sudan on this map.
