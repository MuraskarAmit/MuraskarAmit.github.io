Heard Island and McDonald Islands: map and facts

Heard Island and McDonald Islands is in the Indian Ocean. It has no permanent population. It covers 393 km². Its highest point is Big Ben, at 2,745 m.

Facts

  • Population: none permanent
  • Area: 393 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Highest point: Big Ben, 2,745 m (Natural Earth)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Big Ben: 2,745 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 38.6%, water 22.4%, snow and ice 15.3%, wetland 14.2%, bare ground 7.3%, farmland 1.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 97.4%, hills 1.6%.

Neighbours

Heard Island and McDonald Islands has no land borders on this map.
