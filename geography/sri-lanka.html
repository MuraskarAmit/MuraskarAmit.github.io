Sri Lanka: map and facts

Sri Lanka is in Southern Asia. It has about 21.8 million people (2025), the 62nd largest population. It covers 65,610 km², the 120th largest by area. That is about 332 people per km². Its highest point is Pidurutalagala, at 2,524 m. The longest river on its map is the Mahaweli, 335 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 21,756,000 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 65,610 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Colombo, Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte
  • Highest point: Pidurutalagala, 2,524 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 34.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pidurutalagala: 2,524 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 31.9%, savanna 23.8%, evergreen broadleaf forest 18.4%, farmland with natural plants 9.9%, grassland 6.8%, farmland 5.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 64.8%, hills 19.5%, mountains 15.7%.

Forests covered 34.4% of its land in 2024, down from 37.8% in 1990 (World Bank).
