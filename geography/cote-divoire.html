Cote d'Ivoire: map and facts

Cote d'Ivoire is in Western Africa. It has about 32.7 million people (2025), the 50th largest population. It covers 322,460 km², the 68th largest by area. That is about 101 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Richard-Molard, at 1,752 m. The longest river on its map is the Black Volta, 1,352 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 32,711,547 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 322,460 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Yamoussoukro
  • Highest point: Mount Richard-Molard, 1,752 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 12.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Cote d'Ivoire, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Black Volta: 1,352 km
  • Komoé: 1,160 km
  • Bandama: 1,050 km
  • Sassandra: 650 km

Also on the map: Baoulé, Bandama Blanc, Bagoé and Cess.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Richard-Molard: 1,752 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Guinea Highlands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 71%, grassland 12.8%, wooded savanna 11.7%, evergreen broadleaf forest 2.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 58.7%, plateaus 30.5%, hills 9.9%.

Forests covered 12.1% of its land in 2024, down from 26.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Cote d'Ivoire shares land borders with Burkina Faso, Ghana, Guinea, Liberia and Mali on this map.
