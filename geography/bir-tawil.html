Bir Tawil: map and facts

Bir Tawil is in Northern Africa. It has no permanent population. It covers 2,023 km².

Facts

  • Population: none permanent
  • Area: 2,023 km² (area as drawn on this map)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 100%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 84.2%, hills 15.8%.

Neighbours

Bir Tawil shares land borders with Egypt and Sudan on this map.
