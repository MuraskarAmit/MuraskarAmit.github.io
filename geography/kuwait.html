Kuwait: map and facts

Kuwait is in Western Asia. It has about 4.87 million people (2025), the 125th largest population. It covers 17,820 km², the 152nd largest by area. That is about 273 people per km².

Facts

  • Population: 4,865,298 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 17,820 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Kuwait City
  • Forest: 0.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mutla Ridge: 140 m

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 93.6%, towns and cities 3.5%, grassland 1.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 0.4% of its land in 2024, up from 0.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #10 in oil among the countries EIA lists (2025)

Neighbours

Kuwait shares land borders with Iraq and Saudi Arabia on this map.
