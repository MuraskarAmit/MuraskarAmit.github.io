Colombia: map and facts

Colombia is in South America. It has about 53.4 million people (2025), the 28th largest population. It covers 1,140,619 km², the 25th largest by area. That is about 47 people per km². Its highest point is Pico Cristóbal Colón, at 5,775 m. The longest river on its map is the Japura, 2,820 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 53,425,635 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,140,619 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Bogota
  • Highest point: Pico Cristóbal Colón, 5,775 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 53.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Colombia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Japura: 2,820 km
  • Negro: 2,250 km
  • Orinoco: 2,140 km
  • Putumayo: 1,610 km
  • Magdalena: 1,528 km
  • Meta: 1,000 km
  • Cauca: 965 km
  • Ajaju: 770 km
  • Atrato: 644 km
  • Mira: 400 km

Also on the map: Amazonas, Caqueta, Guainía and Uaupés.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pico Cristóbal Colón: 5,775 m (its highest point)
  • Ritacuba Blanco: 5,410 m
  • Nevado del Ruiz: 5,389 m
  • Volcan Galeras: 4,276 m
  • Alto de Buey: 1,810 m
  • Cerro Maine Hanari: 860 m

Mountain ranges: Andes, Selvas, Cordillera Central, Cordillera Occidental and Cordillera Oriental.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Llanos and Guajira Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 53.3%, savanna 24.5%, wooded savanna 13.3%, grassland 6.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 62.8%, mountains 20.5%, hills 9.4%, high mountains 5.4%, plateaus 1.9%.

Forests covered 53.7% of its land in 2024, down from 58.7% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Colombia shares land borders with Brazil, Ecuador, Panama, Peru and Venezuela on this map.
