Croatia: map and facts

Croatia is in Southern Europe. It has about 3.88 million people (2025), the 128th largest population. It covers 54,901 km², the 124th largest by area. That is about 71 people per km². Its highest point is Dinara, at 1,831 m. The longest river on its map is the Danube, 2,850 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 3,876,200 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 54,901 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Zagreb
  • Highest point: Dinara, 1,831 m (CIA World Factbook (archived))
  • Forest: 34.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Croatia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Danube: 2,850 km
  • Sava: 945 km
  • Mur: 434 km
  • Kupa: 297 km
  • Neretva: 225 km
  • Una: 207 km

Also on the map: Drava.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Dinara: 1,831 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Dinaric Alps.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Dalmatia.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): deciduous broadleaf forest 31.3%, savanna 17.8%, farmland 15.7%, wooded savanna 14%, grassland 11%, mixed forest 3.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 36.8%, hills 33.7%, mountains 29.4%.

Forests covered 34.8% of its land in 2024, up from 33.4% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Croatia shares land borders with Bosnia and Herzegovina, Hungary, Montenegro, Serbia and Slovenia on this map.
