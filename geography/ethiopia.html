Ethiopia: map and facts

Ethiopia is in Eastern Africa. It has about 135 million people (2025), the 10th largest population. It covers 1,129,819 km², the 26th largest by area. That is about 120 people per km². Its highest point is Ras Dejen, at 4,533 m. The longest river on its map is the Abay, 1,783 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 135,472,051 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,129,819 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Addis Ababa
  • Highest point: Ras Dejen, 4,533 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 23.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Ethiopia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Abay: 1,783 km
  • Jubba: 1,658 km
  • Awash: 1,200 km
  • Omo: 760 km
  • Setit: 608 km
  • Akobo: 434 km
  • Sobat: 306 km

Also on the map: Genale and Shebele.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Ras Dejen: 4,533 m (its highest point)
  • Talo: 4,413 m
  • Mount Batu: 4,307 m
  • Amba Farit: 4,270 m
  • Gugu: 3,626 m
  • Argun: 3,418 m
  • Muleta: 3,405 m

Mountain ranges: Ethiopian Highlands and Ahmar Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Somali Peninsula, Great Rift Valley, Ogaden and Danakil Desert.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 46.8%, savanna 17.2%, farmland 13%, open shrubland 11.2%, bare ground 3.9%, evergreen broadleaf forest 2.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 36%, plateaus 29.2%, mountains 28.3%, high mountains 4.6%, plains 1.9%.

Forests covered 23.8% of its land in 2024, down from 28.2% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Ethiopia shares land borders with Djibouti, Eritrea, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan and Sudan on this map.
