Oman: map and facts

Oman is in Western Asia. It has about 5.49 million people (2025), the 119th largest population. It covers 309,500 km², the 70th largest by area. That is about 18 people per km². Its highest point is Jabal Shams, at 3,018 m.

Facts

  • Population: 5,494,691 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 309,500 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Muscat
  • Highest point: Jabal Shams, 3,018 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Jabal Shams: 3,018 m (its highest point)
  • Jabal Yibir: 1,527 m

Mountain ranges: Al Hajar Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Arabian Peninsula, Rub' al Khali, Musandam Peninsula and Wahiba Sands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 97%, grassland 1.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 67.8%, plateaus 13.1%, hills 10.1%, mountains 9%.

Forests covered 0% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #3 in gypsum among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Oman shares land borders with Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates and Yemen on this map.
