Saint Vincent and the Grenadines: map and facts

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines is in the Caribbean. It has 99,924 people (2025), the 182nd largest population. It covers 389 km², the 183rd largest by area. That is about 257 people per km². Its highest point is La Soufriere, at 1,220 m.

Facts

  • Population: 99,924 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 389 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 369 km².
  • Capital: Kingstown
  • Highest point: La Soufriere, 1,220 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 73.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • La Soufriere: 1,220 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 38%, wooded savanna 30.4%, grassland 12.1%, wetland 10.8%, water 4.6%, savanna 1.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 88.5%, plains 10.3%, hills 1.2%.

Forests covered 73.4% of its land in 2024, up from 73.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines has no land borders on this map.
