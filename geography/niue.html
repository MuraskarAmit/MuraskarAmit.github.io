Niue: map and facts

Niue is in Polynesia. It has 1,817 people (2023). It covers 260 km². That is about 7 people per km². Its highest point is unnamed point near Mutalau, at 68 m.

Facts

  • Population: 1,817 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 260 km² (UN Demographic Yearbook). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 221 km².
  • Capital: Alofi
  • Highest point: unnamed point near Mutalau, 68 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • unnamed point near Mutalau: 68 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 93.3%, water 4.1%, grassland 1.5%, wetland 1.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Neighbours

Niue has no land borders on this map.
