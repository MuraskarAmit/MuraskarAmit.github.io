Saint Helena: map and facts

Saint Helena is in Western Africa. It has 5,288 people (2023). It covers 367 km². That is about 14 people per km². Its highest point is Queen Mary's Peak, at 2,062 m.

Facts

  • Population: 5,288 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 367 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Highest point: Queen Mary's Peak, 2,062 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Queen Mary's Peak: 2,062 m (its highest point)
  • Diana's Peak: 818 m

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 26.8%, grassland 24.2%, water 19.7%, bare ground 13.3%, evergreen broadleaf forest 6.4%, wetland 3.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 94.4%, hills 5.6%.

Neighbours

Saint Helena has no land borders on this map.
