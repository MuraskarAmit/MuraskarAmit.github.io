Guam: map and facts

Guam is in Micronesia. It has 168,999 people (2025). It covers 541 km². That is about 312 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Lamlam, at 406 m.

Facts

  • Population: 168,999 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 541 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 566 km².
  • Capital: Hagatna
  • Highest point: Mount Lamlam, 406 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 52.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Lamlam: 406 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Mariana Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland with natural plants 42.1%, wooded savanna 16.9%, savanna 16.8%, water 7.8%, grassland 5%, farmland 4.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 94%, plains 6%.

Forests covered 52.3% of its land in 2024, up from 44.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Guam has no land borders on this map.
