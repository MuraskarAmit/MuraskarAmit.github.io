South Sudan: map and facts

South Sudan is in Eastern Africa. It has about 12.2 million people (2025), the 80th largest population. It covers 646,883 km², the 41st largest by area. That is about 19 people per km². Its highest point is Kinyeti, at 3,187 m. The longest river on its map is the Akobo, 434 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 12,188,788 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 646,883 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 630,000 km².
  • Capital: Juba
  • Highest point: Kinyeti, 3,187 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 11.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of South Sudan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Akobo: 434 km
  • Sobat: 354 km
  • Pibor: 320 km
  • Kangen: 300 km

Also on the map: White Nile and Bahr el Zeraf.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Kinyeti: 3,187 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Chaine des Mongos.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Sudd.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 57.8%, grassland 35.2%, deciduous broadleaf forest 2.5%, farmland 1.9%, wetland 1.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 92.5%, hills 5.7%, mountains 1.8%.

Forests covered 11.3% of its land in 2024, the same as in 2015 (World Bank).

Neighbours

South Sudan shares land borders with Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Kenya, Sudan and Uganda on this map.
