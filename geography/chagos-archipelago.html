Chagos Archipelago: map and facts

The Chagos Archipelago is in the Indian Ocean. It covers 49 km².

Facts

  • Area: 49 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital not shown: its status is disputed.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): water 56.7%, wetland 41.6%, grassland 1.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Neighbours

The Chagos Archipelago has no land borders on this map.
