Liberia: map and facts

Liberia is in Western Africa. It has about 5.73 million people (2025), the 115th largest population. It covers 111,370 km², the 102nd largest by area. That is about 51 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Wuteve, at 1,440 m.

Facts

  • Population: 5,731,206 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 111,370 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 95,700 km².
  • Capital: Monrovia
  • Highest point: Mount Wuteve, 1,440 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 66.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

Rivers on the map: St. Paul and Cess.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Wuteve: 1,440 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Guinea Highlands.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Grain Coast.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 57.1%, wooded savanna 40.3%, savanna 1.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 65.7%, hills 23.9%, plateaus 9.9%.

Forests covered 66.1% of its land in 2024, down from 83.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Liberia shares land borders with Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea and Sierra Leone on this map.
