Solomon Islands: map and facts

The Solomon Islands is in Melanesia. It has 838,645 people (2025), the 159th largest population. It covers 28,446 km², the 140th largest by area. That is about 29 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Popomanaseu, at 2,335 m.

Facts

  • Population: 838,645 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 28,446 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 27,300 km².
  • Capital: Honiara
  • Highest point: Mount Popomanaseu, 2,335 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 89.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Popomanaseu: 2,335 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Guadalcanal, Makira, Santa Isabel Island, Santa Cruz Islands and New Georgia Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 90%, water 6.5%, wetland 2.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 47.4%, hills 44.5%, plains 8.1%.

Forests covered 89.8% of its land in 2024, down from 90.7% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The Solomon Islands has no land borders on this map.
