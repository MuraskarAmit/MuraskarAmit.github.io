Hong Kong: map and facts

Hong Kong is in Eastern Asia. It has about 7.5 million people (2025). It covers 1,110 km². That is about 6,756 people per km². Its highest point is Tai Mo Shan, at 957 m.

Facts

  • Population: 7,498,900 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,110 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 1,040 km².
  • Highest point: Tai Mo Shan, 957 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Tai Mo Shan: 957 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 45.5%, towns and cities 20.6%, savanna 12.3%, wooded savanna 6.2%, water 5.6%, grassland 4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 67.7%, mountains 32%.
