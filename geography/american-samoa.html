American Samoa: map and facts

American Samoa is in Polynesia. It has 46,029 people (2025). It covers 199 km². That is about 231 people per km². Its highest point is Lata Mountain, at 963 m.

Facts

  • Population: 46,029 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 199 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 181 km².
  • Capital: Pago Pago
  • Highest point: Lata Mountain, 963 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 79.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Lata Mountain: 963 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 44.7%, wetland 27.5%, water 15.8%, grassland 8.8%, wooded savanna 2.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 77.2%, mountains 20.9%, plains 1.9%.

Forests covered 79.6% of its land in 2024, down from 90.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

American Samoa has no land borders on this map.
