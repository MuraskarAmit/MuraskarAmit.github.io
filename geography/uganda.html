Uganda: map and facts

Uganda is in Eastern Africa. It has about 51.4 million people (2025), the 31st largest population. It covers 241,551 km², the 79th largest by area. That is about 213 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Stanley, at 5,109 m. The longest river on its map is the Albert Nile, 2,620 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 51,384,894 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 241,551 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Kampala
  • Highest point: Mount Stanley, 5,109 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 11.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Uganda, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Albert Nile: 2,620 km
  • Kagera: 400 km
  • Semliki: 230 km

Also on the map: Victoria Nile.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Stanley: 5,109 m (its highest point)
  • Mount Elgon: 4,321 m

Mountain ranges: Ruwenzori Range.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Great Rift Valley.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland with natural plants 30.7%, savanna 22.7%, water 14.3%, grassland 13.5%, farmland 11%, evergreen broadleaf forest 3.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 68.5%, hills 23.8%, mountains 7%.

Forests covered 11.9% of its land in 2024, down from 22.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Uganda shares land borders with Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Rwanda, South Sudan and Tanzania on this map.
