Anguilla: map and facts

Anguilla is in the Caribbean. It has 14,410 people (2023). It covers 88 km². That is about 164 people per km². Its highest point is Crocus Hill, at 65 m.

Facts

  • Population: 14,410 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 88 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: The Valley
  • Highest point: Crocus Hill, 65 m (Wikidata)
