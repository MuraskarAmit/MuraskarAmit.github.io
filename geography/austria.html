Austria: map and facts

Austria is in Western Europe. It has about 9.21 million people (2025), the 97th largest population. It covers 83,879 km², the 113th largest by area. That is about 110 people per km². Its highest point is Grossglockner, at 3,798 m. The longest river on its map is the Danube, 2,850 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 9,208,163 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 83,879 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Vienna
  • Highest point: Grossglockner, 3,798 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 47.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Austria, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Danube: 2,850 km
  • Rhine: 1,233 km
  • Inn: 517 km
  • Mur: 434 km
  • Morava: 388 km
  • Enns: 254 km
  • Thaya: 235 km
  • Salzach: 225 km
  • Traun: 153 km

Also on the map: Drava and Raab.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Grossglockner: 3,798 m (its highest point)
  • Zugspitze: 2,962 m
  • Grauspitze: 2,599 m

Mountain ranges: Alps.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): mixed forest 30.6%, farmland 16.2%, grassland 15.1%, wooded savanna 14.3%, evergreen conifer forest 8.6%, savanna 6.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 59%, hills 25.6%, plains 7.5%, plateaus 5.3%, high mountains 2.6%.

Forests covered 47.3% of its land in 2024, up from 45.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #9 in tungsten among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Austria shares land borders with Czechia, Germany, Hungary, Italy, Liechtenstein, Slovakia, Slovenia and Switzerland on this map.
