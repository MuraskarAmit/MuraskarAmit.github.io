Suriname: map and facts

Suriname is in South America. It has 639,850 people (2025), the 163rd largest population. It covers 163,820 km², the 90th largest by area. That is about 3.9 people per km². Its highest point is Julianatop, at 1,280 m. The longest river on its map is the Courantyne, 724 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 639,850 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 163,820 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 146,000 km².
  • Capital: Paramaribo
  • Highest point: Julianatop, 1,280 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 91.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Suriname, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Courantyne: 724 km
  • Suriname: 480 km

Also on the map: Corantijn, Maroni, Litani and Kabalebo.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Julianatop: 1,280 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Tumuc-Humac Mountains and Wilhelmina Mountains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 94.9%, wooded savanna 1.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 71.8%, hills 23.5%, mountains 3%, plateaus 1.7%.

Forests covered 91.5% of its land in 2024, down from 92.6% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Suriname shares land borders with Brazil, France and Guyana on this map.
