Montenegro: map and facts

Montenegro is in Southern Europe. It has 623,129 people (2025), the 164th largest population. It covers 13,883 km², the 155th largest by area. That is about 45 people per km². Its highest point is Zla Kolata, at 2,534 m. The longest river on its map is the Drina, 346 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 623,129 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 13,883 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Podgorica
  • Highest point: Zla Kolata, 2,534 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 61.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Montenegro, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Drina: 346 km
  • Lim: 220 km
  • Moraca: 99 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Zla Kolata: 2,534 m (its highest point)
  • Bobotov Kuk: 2,523 m
  • Maglic: 2,386 m

Mountain ranges: Dinaric Alps.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 27.9%, wooded savanna 22.8%, grassland 21.8%, deciduous broadleaf forest 14.5%, mixed forest 7.1%, evergreen conifer forest 2.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 90.3%, hills 8.6%, plains 1.1%.

Forests covered 61.5% of its land in 2024, the same as in 2010 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Montenegro shares land borders with Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia and Serbia on this map.
