Papua New Guinea: map and facts

Papua New Guinea is in Melanesia. It has about 10.8 million people (2025), the 91st largest population. It covers 462,840 km², the 55th largest by area. That is about 23 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Wilhelm, at 4,509 m. The longest river on its map is the Sepik, 1,126 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 10,762,817 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 462,840 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Port Moresby
  • Highest point: Mount Wilhelm, 4,509 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 75.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Papua New Guinea, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Sepik: 1,126 km
  • Fly: 1,050 km
  • Ramu: 640 km
  • Purari: 470 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Wilhelm: 4,509 m (its highest point)
  • Mount Victoria: 4,038 m
  • Mount Balbi: 2,715 m
  • Ulawun: 2,334 m
  • Takuan: 2,210 m

Mountain ranges: New Guinea Highlands and Owen Stanley Range.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Melanesia, Bismarck Archipelago, Bougainville Island, New Britain, Solomon Isands, New Ireland, D'Entrecasteaux Islands and Louisiade Archipelago.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 86.1%, wooded savanna 5.9%, savanna 2.7%, wetland 2.3%, water 1.6%, grassland 1.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 44.4%, plains 34.3%, hills 17.5%, high mountains 3.7%.

Forests covered 75.2% of its land in 2024, down from 76.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #8 in cobalt among the countries USGS lists (2025)
