Vanuatu: map and facts

Vanuatu is in Melanesia. It has 335,169 people (2025), the 172nd largest population. It covers 12,189 km², the 157th largest by area. That is about 27 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Tabwemasana, at 1,879 m.

Facts

  • Population: 335,169 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 12,189 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Port Vila
  • Highest point: Mount Tabwemasana, 1,879 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 74.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Tabwemasana: 1,879 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Espiritu Santo and Malakula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 86.3%, water 9%, grassland 1.9%, wooded savanna 1.7%, wetland 1.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 57.1%, hills 39.8%, plains 3.1%.

Forests covered 74.6% of its land in 2024, down from 81.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Vanuatu has no land borders on this map.
