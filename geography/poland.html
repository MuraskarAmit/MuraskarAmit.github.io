Poland: map and facts

Poland is in Eastern Europe. It has about 36.4 million people (2025), the 44th largest population. It covers 313,940 km², the 69th largest by area. That is about 116 people per km². Its highest point is Rysy, at 2,501 m. The longest river on its map is the Vistula, 1,047 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 36,435,861 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 313,940 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Warsaw
  • Highest point: Rysy, 2,501 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 31% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Poland, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Vistula: 1,047 km
  • Oder: 854 km
  • Warta: 808 km
  • Bug: 772 km
  • Narew: 484 km
  • San: 444 km
  • Notec: 388 km
  • Wieprz: 328 km
  • Pilica: 319 km
  • Lyna: 289 km

Also on the map: Bóbr, Lusatian Neisse, Wkra, Dunajec, Drweca, Prosna, Nysa Klodzka, Bzura and Gwda.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Rysy: 2,501 m (its highest point)
  • Snežka: 1,603 m

Mountain ranges: Carpathian Mountains, Sudetes Mountains and Tatra Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: North European Plain.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 41.8%, mixed forest 15%, evergreen conifer forest 10.8%, savanna 9.8%, grassland 9.2%, wooded savanna 5.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 88.5%, hills 7.8%, mountains 2.1%, plateaus 1.6%.

Forests covered 31% of its land in 2024, up from 29% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #6 in silver among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #10 in coal among the countries EIA lists (2024)

Neighbours

Poland shares land borders with Belarus, Czechia, Germany, Lithuania, Russia, Slovakia and Ukraine on this map.
