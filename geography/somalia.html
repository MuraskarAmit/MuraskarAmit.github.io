Somalia: map and facts

Somalia is in Eastern Africa. It has about 19.7 million people (2025), the 66th largest population. It covers 637,657 km², the 43rd largest by area. That is about 31 people per km². Its highest point is Shimbiris, at 2,460 m. The longest river on its map is the Jubba, 875 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 19,654,739 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 637,657 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Mogadishu
  • Highest point: Shimbiris, 2,460 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 7.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Somalia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Jubba: 875 km

Also on the map: Shebelle.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Shimbiris: 2,460 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Ogo Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Somali Peninsula and Banaadir Coast.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 39.8%, open shrubland 31.2%, bare ground 26.4%, dense shrubland 1.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 45.3%, plateaus 36.6%, hills 13.9%, mountains 4.2%.

Forests covered 7.8% of its land in 2024, down from 13.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Somalia shares land borders with Djibouti, Ethiopia and Kenya on this map.
