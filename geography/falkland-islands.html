Falkland Islands: map and facts

The Falkland Islands is in South America. It has 3,477 people (2023). It covers 11,552 km². That is about 0.30 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Usborne, at 705 m.

Facts

  • Population: 3,477 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 11,552 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Stanley
  • Highest point: Mount Usborne, 705 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Usborne: 705 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 47.2%, grassland 44.4%, water 4.5%, wetland 3.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 48.8%, plains 41%, mountains 10.2%.

Neighbours

The Falkland Islands has no land borders on this map.
