Syria: map and facts

Syria is in Western Asia. It has about 25.6 million people (2025), the 57th largest population. It covers 185,180 km², the 87th largest by area. That is about 138 people per km². Its highest point is Jabal ash Shaykh, at 2,814 m. The longest river on its map is the Al Furat, 2,800 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 25,620,427 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 185,180 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Damascus
  • Highest point: Jabal ash Shaykh, 2,814 m (Natural Earth)
  • Forest: 2.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Syria, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Al Furat: 2,800 km
  • Dicle: 1,850 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Jabal ash Shaykh: 2,814 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Mesopotamia, Syrian Desert and Levant Coast.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 50.9%, farmland 22.1%, open shrubland 19.2%, grassland 4.8%, towns and cities 1.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 57%, hills 22.7%, plains 14%, mountains 6.3%.

Forests covered 2.9% of its land in 2024, up from 2.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Syria shares land borders with Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Lebanon and Turkey on this map.
