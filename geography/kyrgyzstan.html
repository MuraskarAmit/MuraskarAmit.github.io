Kyrgyzstan: map and facts

Kyrgyzstan is in Central Asia. It has about 7.34 million people (2025), the 105th largest population. It covers 199,950 km², the 85th largest by area. That is about 37 people per km². Its highest point is Pik Pobeda, at 7,439 m. The longest river on its map is the Naryn, 807 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 7,343,064 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 199,950 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Bishkek
  • Highest point: Pik Pobeda, 7,439 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 6.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Kyrgyzstan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Naryn: 807 km

Also on the map: Toxkan.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pik Pobeda: 7,439 m (its highest point)
  • Pik Karakol: 5,216 m
  • Gora Manas: 4,482 m

Mountain ranges: Tian Shan.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Fergana Valley.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 75.8%, bare ground 11%, farmland 5.6%, water 3.3%, snow and ice 2.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): high mountains 53.1%, mountains 30.3%, plateaus 8.5%, hills 8.1%.

Forests covered 6.5% of its land in 2024, up from 6% in 1995 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #9 in antimony among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Kyrgyzstan shares land borders with China, Kazakhstan, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan on this map.
