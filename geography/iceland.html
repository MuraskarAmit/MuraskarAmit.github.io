Iceland: map and facts

Iceland is in Northern Europe. It has 392,404 people (2025), the 171st largest population. It covers 103,000 km², the 106th largest by area. That is about 3.8 people per km². Its highest point is Hvannadalshnukur, at 2,110 m. The longest river on its map is the Hvíta, 185 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 392,404 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 103,000 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Reykjavík
  • Highest point: Hvannadalshnukur, 2,110 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Iceland, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Hvíta: 185 km
  • Skjalfandafljot: 178 km
  • Jökulsa a Bru: 150 km
  • Blanda: 125 km
  • Hvíta: 117 km
  • Jökulsa í Fljótsdal: 56 km
  • Héradsvötn: 40 km

Also on the map: Thjorsa and Kaldakvísl.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Hvannadalshnukur: 2,110 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Vatnajökull.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 55.2%, bare ground 25.2%, snow and ice 11.8%, savanna 3.8%, water 2.1%, wetland 1.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 46.1%, hills 44.9%, plains 5.2%, plateaus 3.8%.

Forests covered 0.6% of its land in 2024, up from 0.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Iceland has no land borders on this map.
