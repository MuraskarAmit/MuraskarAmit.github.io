China: map and facts

China is in Eastern Asia. It has about 1.41 billion people (2025), the 2nd largest population. It covers 9,562,910 km², the 4th largest by area. That is about 147 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Everest, at 8,849 m. The longest river on its map is the Yangtze, 6,300 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 1,406,585,000 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 9,562,910 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 9,340,000 km².
  • Capital: Beijing
  • Highest point: Mount Everest, 8,849 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 24% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Everest: 8,849 m (its highest point)
  • Namcha Barwa: 7,782 m
  • Kongkoerh: 7,649 m
  • Gangkar Punsum: 7,570 m
  • Gongga Shan: 7,556 m
  • Pik Pobeda: 7,439 m
  • Gyala Peri: 7,294 m
  • Liushi Shan: 7,167 m

Mountain ranges: Himalayas, Tian Shan, Altay Mountains, Greater Khingan Range, Karakoram Range, Kunlun Mountains, Pamirs, Altun Mountains, Dabie Mountains, Lesser Khingan Range, Nan Ling Mountains and Quilian Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Gobi Desert, North China Plain, Plateau of Tibet, Manchurian Plain, Mongolian Plateau, Shandong Peninsula, Sichuan Basin, Tarim Basin, Yungui Plateau, Hexi Corridor, Junggar Basin and Leizhou Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 30.1%, bare ground 23%, farmland 13%, wooded savanna 8.6%, savanna 7.4%, deciduous broadleaf forest 4.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 25.2%, hills 24.9%, high mountains 19%, mountains 18.7%, plains 12.2%.

Forests covered 24% of its land in 2024, up from 16.7% in 1990 (World Bank).
