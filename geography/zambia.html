Zambia: map and facts

Zambia is in Eastern Africa. It has about 21.9 million people (2025), the 61st largest population. It covers 752,612 km², the 38th largest by area. That is about 29 people per km². Its highest point is Mafinga Central, at 2,339 m. The longest river on its map is the Zambezi, 2,574 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 21,913,874 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 752,612 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Lusaka
  • Highest point: Mafinga Central, 2,339 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 60.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Zambia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Zambezi: 2,574 km
  • Kafue: 1,577 km
  • Luangwa: 770 km
  • Cuando: 731 km
  • Lungwebungu: 645 km
  • Luapula: 560 km
  • Chambeshi: 480 km
  • Kabompo: 410 km

Also on the map: Dongwe.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mafinga Central: 2,339 m (its highest point)
  • Sunzu: 2,067 m

Mountain ranges: Muchinga Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Katanga Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 44%, grassland 29.1%, wooded savanna 14.7%, deciduous broadleaf forest 4.7%, mixed forest 4.4%, water 1.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 76%, hills 20.9%, mountains 3.1%.

Forests covered 60.5% of its land in 2024, down from 63.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #7 in copper among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Zambia shares land borders with Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia, Tanzania and Zimbabwe on this map.
