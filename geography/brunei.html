Brunei: map and facts

Brunei is in South-Eastern Asia. It has 466,330 people (2025), the 168th largest population. It covers 5,770 km², the 163rd largest by area. That is about 81 people per km². Its highest point is Bukit Pagon, at 1,850 m. The longest river on its map is the Baram, 400 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 466,330 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 5,770 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Bandar Seri Begawan
  • Highest point: Bukit Pagon, 1,850 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 72.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

  • Baram: 400 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Bukit Pagon: 1,850 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 86.5%, towns and cities 3.7%, wooded savanna 2.7%, wetland 2.5%, savanna 2%, grassland 1.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 69.8%, hills 22.6%, mountains 7.6%.

Forests covered 72.1% of its land in 2024, down from 78.4% in 1990 (World Bank).
