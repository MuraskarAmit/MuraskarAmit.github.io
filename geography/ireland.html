Ireland: map and facts

Ireland is in Northern Europe. It has about 5.48 million people (2025), the 120th largest population. It covers 70,280 km², the 118th largest by area. That is about 78 people per km². Its highest point is Carrauntoohil, at 1,039 m. The longest river on its map is the Shannon, 368 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 5,484,367 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 70,280 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Dublin
  • Highest point: Carrauntoohil, 1,039 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 12% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Ireland, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Shannon: 368 km
  • Barrow: 192 km
  • Suir: 184 km

Also on the map: Annalee.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Carrauntoohil: 1,039 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: British Isles.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 46.3%, grassland 31.3%, mixed forest 6.3%, evergreen broadleaf forest 5.7%, farmland 2.9%, farmland with natural plants 2.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 46.7%, plains 43.6%, mountains 9.7%.

Forests covered 12% of its land in 2024, up from 6.7% in 1990 (World Bank).
