Kiribati: map and facts

Kiribati is in Micronesia. It has 136,488 people (2025), the 177th largest population. It covers 811 km², the 171st largest by area. That is about 168 people per km². Its highest point is Banaba Island, at 81 m.

Facts

  • Population: 136,488 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 811 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 955 km².
  • Capital: South Tarawa
  • Highest point: Banaba Island, 81 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 1.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Banaba Island: 81 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Kiritimati.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): water 51.1%, grassland 31.3%, wetland 12.7%, bare ground 4.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 1.3% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Kiribati has no land borders on this map.
