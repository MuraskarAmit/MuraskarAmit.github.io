Cook Islands: map and facts

The Cook Islands is in Polynesia. It has 14,222 people (2023). It covers 236 km². That is about 60 people per km². Its highest point is Te Manga, at 652 m.

Facts

  • Population: 14,222 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 236 km² (UN Demographic Yearbook). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 192 km².
  • Capital: Avarua
  • Highest point: Te Manga, 652 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Te Manga: 652 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Southern Cook Islands.

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 66.9%, hills 33.1%.

Neighbours

The Cook Islands has no land borders on this map.
