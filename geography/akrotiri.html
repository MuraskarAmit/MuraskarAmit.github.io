Akrotiri: map and facts

Akrotiri is in Western Asia. It covers 99 km².

Facts

  • Area: 99 km² (area as drawn on this map)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 43.8%, farmland 23.6%, bare ground 9%, water 9%, towns and cities 8.3%, savanna 6.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 55.6%, hills 44.4%.

Neighbours

Akrotiri shares land borders with Cyprus on this map.
