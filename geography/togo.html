Togo: map and facts

Togo is in Western Africa. It has about 8.59 million people (2025), the 101st largest population. It covers 56,785 km², the 123rd largest by area. That is about 151 people per km². Its highest point is Mont Agou, at 986 m. The longest river on its map is the Oti, 900 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 8,591,626 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 56,785 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Lomé
  • Highest point: Mont Agou, 986 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 22.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Togo, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Oti: 900 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mont Agou: 986 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Slave Coast.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 56.1%, savanna 37.2%, farmland 3.2%, wooded savanna 1.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 58.4%, hills 26.2%, plateaus 10.9%, mountains 4.5%.

Forests covered 22.5% of its land in 2024, down from 23.9% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Togo shares land borders with Benin, Burkina Faso and Ghana on this map.
