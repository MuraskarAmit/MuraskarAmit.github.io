Sudan: map and facts

Sudan is in Northern Africa. It has about 51.7 million people (2025), the 30th largest population. It covers 1,878,000 km², the 15th largest by area. That is about 28 people per km². Its highest point is Jabal Marrah, at 3,042 m. The longest river on its map is the Nile, 6,650 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 51,662,147 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,878,000 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Khartoum
  • Highest point: Jabal Marrah, 3,042 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 11.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Sudan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Nile: 6,650 km
  • El Bahr el Abyad: 2,620 km
  • El Bahr el Azraq: 1,783 km
  • Atbara: 1,120 km
  • Setit: 608 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Jabal Marrah: 3,042 m (its highest point)
  • Jabal Hamoyet: 2,726 m
  • Jabal Oda: 2,260 m
  • Jabal Teljo: 1,954 m
  • Jabal al Uwaynt: 1,934 m

Mountain ranges: Chaine des Mongos and Marrah Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Sahara, Sahel, Libyan Desert, Nubian Desert, Gezira and Jabal Abyad Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 57.9%, grassland 37.4%, farmland 3.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 84%, hills 9.7%, plains 4.8%, mountains 1.5%.

Forests covered 11.9% of its land in 2024, down from 12.7% in 2015 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Sudan shares land borders with Bir Tawil, Central African Republic, Chad, Egypt, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Libya and South Sudan on this map.
