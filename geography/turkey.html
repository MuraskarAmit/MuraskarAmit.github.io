Turkey: map and facts

Turkey is in Western Asia. It has about 85.9 million people (2025), the 18th largest population. It covers 785,350 km², the 36th largest by area. That is about 109 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Ararat, at 5,137 m. The longest river on its map is the Euphrates, 2,800 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 85,878,556 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 785,350 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Ankara
  • Highest point: Mount Ararat, 5,137 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 29.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Turkey, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Euphrates: 2,800 km
  • Tigris: 1,850 km
  • Kura: 1,360 km
  • Kizilirmak: 1,355 km
  • Aras: 1,072 km
  • Sakarya: 824 km
  • Murat: 722 km
  • Seyhan: 560 km
  • Buyuk Menderes: 548 km
  • Maritsa: 490 km

Also on the map: Ceyhan, Coruh, Delice, Gediz, Tundzha, Cekerek, Filyos, Kelkit, Simav and Carsamba.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Ararat: 5,137 m (its highest point)
  • Kackar Dagi: 3,932 m
  • Erciyes Dagi: 3,917 m
  • Kizilkaya: 3,767 m
  • Demirkazik: 3,756 m
  • Kizlar Sivrisi: 3,070 m
  • Uludag Tepe: 2,542 m

Mountain ranges: Pontic Mountains and Taurus Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Anatolia and Levant Coast.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 47%, farmland 25.8%, savanna 8.6%, wooded savanna 5.5%, evergreen conifer forest 2.9%, deciduous broadleaf forest 2.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 55.4%, hills 32.6%, plateaus 6.6%, plains 2.8%, high mountains 2.6%.

Forests covered 29.5% of its land in 2024, up from 25.7% in 1990 (World Bank).
