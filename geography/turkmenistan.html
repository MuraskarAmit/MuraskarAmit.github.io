Turkmenistan: map and facts

Turkmenistan is in Central Asia. It has about 7.62 million people (2025), the 103rd largest population. It covers 491,209 km², the 53rd largest by area. That is about 16 people per km². Its highest point is Ayrybaba, at 3,139 m. The longest river on its map is the Amu Darya, 2,620 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 7,618,847 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 491,209 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 470,000 km².
  • Capital: Ashgabat
  • Highest point: Ayrybaba, 3,139 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Turkmenistan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Amu Darya: 2,620 km
  • Atrek: 669 km

Also on the map: Harirud.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Ayrybaba: 3,139 m (its highest point)
  • Kuh-e Ruzeh: 2,941 m
  • Gora Arlan: 1,880 m

Mountain ranges: Kopet Dag Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Turan Lowland and Garagum Desert.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 63.1%, open shrubland 20%, grassland 12.7%, farmland 2.6%, water 1.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 78.2%, hills 12.1%, plateaus 6.6%, mountains 3.1%.

Forests covered 5% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Turkmenistan shares land borders with Afghanistan, Iran, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan on this map.
