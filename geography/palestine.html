Palestine: map and facts

Palestine is in Western Asia. It has about 5.41 million people (2025). It covers 6,025 km². That is about 899 people per km². The longest river on its map is the Jordan, 252 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 5,413,596 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 6,025 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital not shown: its status is disputed.
  • Forest: 1.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Palestine, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Jordan: 252 km

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 34.3%, grassland 26.5%, open shrubland 18.7%, bare ground 12.6%, towns and cities 5.2%, water 2.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 55.9%, mountains 37.1%, plains 7%.

Forests covered 1.8% of its land in 2024, up from 1.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Palestine shares land borders with Egypt, Israel and Jordan on this map.
