Norway: map and facts

Norway is in Northern Europe. It has about 5.61 million people (2025), the 117th largest population. It covers 379,542 km², the 61st largest by area. That is about 15 people per km². Its highest point is Galdhøpiggen, at 2,469 m. The longest river on its map is the Glomma, 604 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 5,610,870 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 379,542 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Oslo
  • Highest point: Galdhøpiggen, 2,469 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 33.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Norway, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Glomma: 604 km
  • Lagen: 358 km
  • Faxalven: 340 km
  • Vasterdal: 315 km
  • Altaelva: 229 km
  • Namsen: 229 km
  • Dalalven: 220 km
  • Hallingdalselva: 220 km
  • Begna: 213 km
  • Tana River: 166 km

Also on the map: Rena, Otta, Barduelva, Vorma, Klarlven, Patsoyoki, Mane and Verkselv.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Galdhøpiggen: 2,469 m (its highest point)
  • Beerenberg: 2,277 m
  • Oksskolten: 1,916 m
  • Newtontoppen: 1,713 m
  • Haltiatunturi: 1,361 m
  • Olavtoppen: 780 m

Mountain ranges: Kjølen Mountains and Hardangervidda.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Scandinavia, Svalbard and Finnmarksvidda.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 30.7%, evergreen conifer forest 12.8%, wooded savanna 12.8%, open shrubland 12.3%, snow and ice 9.9%, savanna 7.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 57.6%, hills 38.3%, plains 2.3%, plateaus 1.8%.

Forests covered 33.2% of its land in 2024, down from 33.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in titanium among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #8 in natural gas among the countries EIA lists (2024)
  • World #10 in graphite among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Norway shares land borders with Finland, Russia and Sweden on this map.
