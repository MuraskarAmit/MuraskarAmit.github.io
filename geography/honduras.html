Honduras: map and facts

Honduras is in Central America. It has about 11 million people (2025), the 86th largest population. It covers 112,492 km², the 101st largest by area. That is about 98 people per km². Its highest point is Cerro Las Minas, at 2,870 m. The longest river on its map is the Coco, 780 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 11,005,850 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 112,492 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Tegucigalpa
  • Highest point: Cerro Las Minas, 2,870 m (CIA World Factbook (archived))
  • Forest: 52.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Honduras, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Coco: 780 km
  • Patuca: 500 km
  • Lempa: 422 km
  • Choluteca: 349 km
  • Aguan: 240 km

Also on the map: Ulua, Humuya, Higuito and Jicatuyo.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Cerro Las Minas: 2,870 m (its highest point)
  • Cerro El Pital: 2,730 m
  • Pico Mogotón: 2,107 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Central America and Mosquito Coast.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 44.6%, evergreen broadleaf forest 27.7%, savanna 18.4%, grassland 4.3%, farmland 1.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 66.7%, hills 19.3%, plains 13.8%.

Forests covered 52.8% of its land in 2024, down from 68.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Honduras shares land borders with El Salvador, Guatemala and Nicaragua on this map.
