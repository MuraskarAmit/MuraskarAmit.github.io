Cabo Verde: map and facts

Cabo Verde is in Western Africa. It has 527,326 people (2025), the 167th largest population. It covers 4,030 km², the 165th largest by area. That is about 131 people per km². Its highest point is Pico do Fogo, at 2,829 m.

Facts

  • Population: 527,326 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 4,030 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 3,940 km².
  • Capital: Praia
  • Highest point: Pico do Fogo, 2,829 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 12.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pico de Cano: 2,829 m
  • Pico do Fogo: 2,829 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Ilhas de Cabo Verde.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 64.3%, bare ground 15%, open shrubland 7.7%, farmland 6.2%, water 6.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 60%, hills 30%, plains 10%.

Forests covered 12.5% of its land in 2024, up from 8.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Cabo Verde has no land borders on this map.
