Cyprus: map and facts

Cyprus is in Western Asia. It has about 1.37 million people (2025), the 151st largest population. It covers 9,250 km², the 162nd largest by area. That is about 148 people per km². Its highest point is Olympus, at 1,952 m.

Facts

  • Population: 1,370,754 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 9,250 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 9,060 km².
  • Capital: Nicosia
  • Highest point: Olympus, 1,952 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 18.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Olympus: 1,952 m (its highest point)
  • Selvili Tepe: 1,024 m

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 49.2%, farmland 27.3%, savanna 10.4%, wooded savanna 4.5%, evergreen conifer forest 3.2%, towns and cities 3.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 45.9%, hills 37.7%, plains 16.4%.

Forests covered 18.6% of its land in 2024, up from 17.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Cyprus shares land borders with Akrotiri and Dhekelia on this map.
