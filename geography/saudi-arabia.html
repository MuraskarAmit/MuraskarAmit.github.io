Saudi Arabia: map and facts

Saudi Arabia is in Western Asia. It has about 37 million people (2025), the 43rd largest population. It covers 2,149,690 km², the 12th largest by area. That is about 17 people per km². Its highest point is Jabal Sawda, at 2,999 m.

Facts

  • Population: 36,973,555 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 2,149,690 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 1,930,000 km².
  • Capital: Riyadh
  • Highest point: Jabal Sawda, 2,999 m (Gilbertson & Gilbertson's GPS surveys of national high points)
  • Forest: 1.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Jabal Sawda: 2,999 m (its highest point)
  • Jabal al Lawz: 2,580 m
  • Jebel Jar: 2,300 m

Mountain ranges: Asir Mountains, Hejaz Mountains and Tuwayq Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Arabian Peninsula, Rub' al Khali, Syrian Desert, An Nafud Desert, Hasa Plain and Tihamah.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 95.9%, open shrubland 2.5%, grassland 1.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 58.4%, plains 23.1%, hills 14.4%, mountains 4.1%.

Forests covered 1.3% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #3 in oil among the countries EIA lists (2025)
  • World #4 in sulfur among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #6 in phosphate among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #8 in bauxite among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #9 in natural gas among the countries EIA lists (2024)

Neighbours

Saudi Arabia shares land borders with Iraq, Jordan, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, United Arab Emirates and Yemen on this map.
