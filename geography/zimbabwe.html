Zimbabwe: map and facts

Zimbabwe is in Eastern Africa. It has about 17 million people (2025), the 73rd largest population. It covers 390,760 km², the 60th largest by area. That is about 43 people per km². Its highest point is Inyangani, at 2,592 m. The longest river on its map is the Zambezi, 2,574 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 16,950,795 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 390,760 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Harare
  • Highest point: Inyangani, 2,592 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 35.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Zimbabwe, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Zambezi: 2,574 km
  • Limpopo: 1,750 km
  • Save: 400 km

Also on the map: Mazoe, Shangani, Umzingwani and Gwayi.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Inyangani: 2,592 m (its highest point)
  • Monte Binga: 2,436 m

Mountain ranges: Inyanga Mountains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 82.9%, savanna 7.5%, farmland 3.9%, dense shrubland 2.3%, open shrubland 1.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 58.2%, hills 37.1%, mountains 4.4%.

Forests covered 35.7% of its land in 2024, down from 43.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #3 in platinum-group metals among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #4 in lithium among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #5 in chromium among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Zimbabwe shares land borders with Botswana, Mozambique, South Africa and Zambia on this map.
