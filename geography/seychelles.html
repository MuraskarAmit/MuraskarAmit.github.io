Seychelles: map and facts

The Seychelles is in Eastern Africa. It has 122,730 people (2025), the 178th largest population. It covers 457 km², the 180th largest by area. That is about 269 people per km². Its highest point is Morne Seychellois, at 905 m.

Facts

  • Population: 122,730 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 457 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 437 km².
  • Capital: Victoria
  • Highest point: Morne Seychellois, 905 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 58.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Morne Seychellois: 905 m (its highest point)

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 42.9%, hills 29.4%, mountains 27.7%.

Forests covered 58.5% of its land in 2024, down from 74.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The Seychelles has no land borders on this map.
