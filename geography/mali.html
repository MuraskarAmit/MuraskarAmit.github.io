Mali: map and facts

Mali is in Western Africa. It has about 25.2 million people (2025), the 58th largest population. It covers 1,241,238 km², the 23rd largest by area. That is about 20 people per km². Its highest point is Hombori Tondo, at 1,153 m. The longest river on its map is the Niger, 4,180 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 25,198,821 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,241,238 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Bamako
  • Highest point: Hombori Tondo, 1,153 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 8.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Mali, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Niger: 4,180 km
  • Komoé: 900 km
  • Bafing: 500 km
  • Baoulé: 500 km
  • Bani: 430 km
  • Bakoy: 400 km
  • Bagoé: 350 km

Also on the map: Sénégal and Baoulé.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Hombori Tondo: 1,153 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Sahel, El Djouf, Erg Chech and Aoukar.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 57.7%, grassland 40.7%, farmland 1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 50.7%, plains 44.3%, hills 4.9%.

Forests covered 8.7% of its land in 2024, down from 11.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #7 in lithium among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Mali shares land borders with Algeria, Burkina Faso, Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea, Mauritania, Niger and Senegal on this map.
