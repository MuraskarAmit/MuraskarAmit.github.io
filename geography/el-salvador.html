El Salvador: map and facts

El Salvador is in Central America. It has about 6.37 million people (2025), the 111th largest population. It covers 21,040 km², the 149th largest by area. That is about 303 people per km². Its highest point is Cerro El Pital, at 2,730 m. The longest river on its map is the Lempa, 422 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 6,365,503 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 21,040 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 20,600 km².
  • Capital: San Salvador
  • Highest point: Cerro El Pital, 2,730 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 33% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of El Salvador, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Lempa: 422 km
  • Paz: 134 km

Also on the map: San Miguel.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Cerro El Pital: 2,730 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 33.4%, savanna 32.6%, farmland with natural plants 8%, evergreen broadleaf forest 7.2%, farmland 6.6%, deciduous broadleaf forest 6.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 52.6%, hills 39.3%, plains 8.1%.

Forests covered 33% of its land in 2024, down from 35.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

El Salvador shares land borders with Guatemala and Honduras on this map.
