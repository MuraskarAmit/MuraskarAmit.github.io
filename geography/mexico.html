Mexico: map and facts

Mexico is in Central America. It has about 132 million people (2025), the 11th largest population. It covers 1,957,194 km², the 13th largest by area. That is about 67 people per km². Its highest point is Pico de Orizaba, at 5,636 m. The longest river on its map is the Rio Grande, 3,051 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 131,946,900 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,957,194 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Mexico City
  • Highest point: Pico de Orizaba, 5,636 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 34.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Mexico, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Rio Grande: 3,051 km
  • Colorado: 2,334 km

  • Yaqui: 680 km
  • Usumacinta: 560 km
  • Grijalva: 480 km

Also on the map: Rio Grande de Santiago, Verde, Panuco, Conchos, Cuilco, Rio Salinas, San Pedro y San Pablo, Moctezuma, Atengo and San Juan.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pico de Orizaba: 5,636 m (its highest point)
  • Volcan Popocatépetl: 5,420 m
  • Volcan Tacana: 4,064 m
  • Volcan de Colima: 3,850 m
  • Cerro Nube Flan: 3,720 m
  • Cerro San Rafael: 3,710 m
  • Sierra El Viejo: 3,500 m
  • Cerro El Jabaln: 3,440 m

Mountain ranges: Sierra Madre del Sur, Sierra Madre Occidental, Sierra Madre Oriental and Sierra Chiapas.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Central America, Alti-Planicie Mexicana, Baja California Peninsula, Istmo de Tehuantepec, Yucatan Peninsula, Chihuahuan Desert, Sonoran Desert and Revillagigedo Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): open shrubland 24%, grassland 22.9%, savanna 15.8%, wooded savanna 11%, evergreen broadleaf forest 8.3%, farmland 6.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 36.8%, hills 32%, plains 20.1%, plateaus 8.9%, high mountains 2.2%.

Forests covered 34.2% of its land in 2024, down from 36.9% in 1990 (World Bank).
