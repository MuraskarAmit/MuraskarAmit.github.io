Myanmar: map and facts

Myanmar is in South-Eastern Asia. It has about 54.9 million people (2025), the 27th largest population. It covers 676,578 km², the 39th largest by area. That is about 81 people per km². Its highest point is Hkakabo Razi, at 5,881 m. The longest river on its map is the Mekong, 4,350 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 54,850,648 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 676,578 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Naypyidaw
  • Highest point: Hkakabo Razi, 5,881 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 42% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Myanmar, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Mekong: 4,350 km
  • Salween: 2,815 km
  • Chindwin: 1,207 km

Also on the map: Irrawaddy, Nmai, Sittaung, Myitnge, Kaladan and Myittha.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Hkakabo Razi: 5,881 m (its highest point)
  • Saramati: 3,826 m
  • Nat Ma Taung: 3,053 m
  • Kennedy Peak: 2,703 m
  • Loi Leng: 2,651 m
  • Myinmoletkat Taung: 2,072 m
  • Mowdok Mual: 1,054 m

Mountain ranges: Arakan Yoma, Chin Hills and Naga Hills.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Indochina Peninsula, Kra Isthmus, Myeik Archipelago, Shan Plateau and Irrawaddy Delta.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 32.7%, farmland 20.4%, wooded savanna 15.3%, mixed forest 10.4%, deciduous broadleaf forest 7.6%, grassland 6.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 45.8%, hills 27%, plains 24.6%, high mountains 1.5%, plateaus 1.1%.

Forests covered 42% of its land in 2024, down from 60% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #4 in rare earths among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #5 in antimony among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #7 in tin among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Myanmar shares land borders with Bangladesh, China, India, Laos and Thailand on this map.
