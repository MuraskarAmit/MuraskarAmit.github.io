Belarus: map and facts

Belarus is in Eastern Europe. It has about 9.09 million people (2025), the 99th largest population. It covers 207,629 km², the 84th largest by area. That is about 44 people per km². Its highest point is Dzyarzhynskaya, at 346 m. The longest river on its map is the Dnipro, 2,285 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 9,085,991 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 207,629 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Minsk
  • Highest point: Dzyarzhynskaya, 346 m (Natural Earth)
  • Forest: 44.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Belarus, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Dnipro: 2,285 km
  • Daugava: 1,020 km
  • Pripyat: 775 km
  • Bug: 772 km
  • Haryn: 659 km
  • Sozh: 648 km
  • Byarezina: 613 km
  • Neris: 510 km
  • Styr: 494 km
  • Narew: 484 km

Also on the map: Iputs, Shchara, Ubort, Yasyelda, Drysa, Dysna, Pronya, Ula, Usha and Mukhavyets.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Dzyarzhynskaya: 346 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): mixed forest 32.8%, farmland 24.3%, wooded savanna 15.8%, grassland 9.9%, savanna 8.5%, evergreen conifer forest 3.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 99.8%.

Forests covered 44.1% of its land in 2024, up from 39.6% in 1995 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #4 in potash among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Belarus shares land borders with Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Russia and Ukraine on this map.
