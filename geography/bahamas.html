Bahamas: map and facts

The Bahamas is in the Caribbean. It has 403,033 people (2025), the 170th largest population. It covers 13,878 km², the 156th largest by area. That is about 29 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Alvernia, at 63 m.

Facts

  • Population: 403,033 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 13,878 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 12,600 km².
  • Capital: Nassau
  • Highest point: Mount Alvernia, 63 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 50.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Alvernia: 63 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: West Indies and Bahama Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wetland 23.5%, grassland 22.3%, water 15.4%, savanna 14.9%, evergreen broadleaf forest 13.1%, wooded savanna 8.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 50.9% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The Bahamas has no land borders on this map.
