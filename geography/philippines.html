Philippines: map and facts

The Philippines is in South-Eastern Asia. It has about 117 million people (2025), the 14th largest population. It covers 300,000 km², the 72nd largest by area. That is about 389 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Apo, at 2,954 m. The longest river on its map is the Cagayan, 505 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 116,786,962 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 300,000 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Manila
  • Highest point: Mount Apo, 2,954 m (Natural Earth)
  • Forest: 24.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of the Philippines, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Cagayan: 505 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Apo: 2,954 m (its highest point)
  • Pulog: 2,934 m
  • Mount Halcon: 2,582 m
  • Canlaon Peak: 2,430 m
  • Mount Mantalingajan: 2,085 m
  • Mount Pinatubo: 1,486 m
  • Taal Volcano: 400 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Malay Archipelago, Sulu Archipelago, Babuyan Islands and Calamian Group.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 34.9%, wooded savanna 27.2%, savanna 12.1%, farmland with natural plants 7.6%, farmland 7.3%, grassland 4.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 46.5%, hills 37.4%, plains 16%.

Forests covered 24.8% of its land in 2024, down from 26.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #2 in nickel among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #5 in cobalt among the countries USGS lists (2025)
