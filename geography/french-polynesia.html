French Polynesia: map and facts

French Polynesia is in Polynesia. It has 282,465 people (2025). It covers 3,521 km². That is about 80 people per km². Its highest point is Mont Orohena, at 2,241 m.

Facts

  • Population: 282,465 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 3,521 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 3,350 km².
  • Capital: Papeete
  • Highest point: Mont Orohena, 2,241 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 42.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mont Orohena: 2,241 m (its highest point)
  • Mont Tekao: 1,224 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Polynesia, Tahiti, Hiva-Oa, Nuku Hiva, Raiatea, Society Islands, Gambier Islands, Marquesas Islands and Tubuai Islands.

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 64.2%, plains 21%, hills 14.8%.

Forests covered 42.4% of its land in 2024, up from 39.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

French Polynesia has no land borders on this map.
