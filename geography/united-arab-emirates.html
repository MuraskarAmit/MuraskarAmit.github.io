United Arab Emirates: map and facts

The United Arab Emirates is in Western Asia. It has about 11.5 million people (2025), the 85th largest population. It covers 71,249 km², the 117th largest by area. That is about 162 people per km². Its highest point is Jebel Jais, at 1,911 m.

Facts

  • Population: 11,513,149 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 71,249 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Abu Dhabi
  • Highest point: Jebel Jais, 1,911 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 4.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Jebel Jais: 1,911 m (its highest point)
  • Jabal Yibir: 1,527 m

Mountain ranges: Al Hajar Mountains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 96.8%, open shrubland 1.3%, towns and cities 1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 93.1%, mountains 3.2%, hills 3.1%.

Forests covered 4.6% of its land in 2024, up from 4.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in sulfur among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #8 in oil among the countries EIA lists (2025)

Neighbours

The United Arab Emirates shares land borders with Oman and Saudi Arabia on this map.
