Thailand: map and facts

Thailand is in South-Eastern Asia. It has about 71.6 million people (2025), the 20th largest population. It covers 513,115 km², the 50th largest by area. That is about 140 people per km². Its highest point is Doi Inthanon, at 2,565 m. The longest river on its map is the Mekong, 4,350 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 71,619,863 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 513,115 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Bangkok
  • Highest point: Doi Inthanon, 2,565 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 38.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Thailand, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Mekong: 4,350 km
  • Salween: 2,815 km
  • Chi: 765 km
  • Yom: 700 km
  • Mun: 673 km
  • Ping: 658 km
  • Chao Phraya: 372 km

Also on the map: Mae Klong.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Doi Inthanon: 2,565 m (its highest point)
  • Phu Soi Dao: 2,120 m
  • Khao Luang: 1,780 m

Mountain ranges: Dangrek Range.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Indochina Peninsula, Malay Peninsula, Kra Isthmus and Khorat Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 38.6%, evergreen broadleaf forest 18.9%, wooded savanna 16.1%, savanna 10.7%, grassland 5.6%, deciduous broadleaf forest 4.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 44.5%, hills 29.1%, mountains 25.7%.

Forests covered 38.7% of its land in 2024, down from 39.6% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in rare earths among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #7 in gypsum among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #9 in fluorspar among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Thailand shares land borders with Cambodia, Laos, Malaysia and Myanmar on this map.
