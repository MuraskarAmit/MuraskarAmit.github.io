Vietnam: map and facts

Vietnam is in South-Eastern Asia. It has about 102 million people (2025), the 16th largest population. It covers 331,338 km², the 66th largest by area. That is about 307 people per km². Its highest point is Fan Si Pan, at 3,147 m. The longest river on its map is the Mekong, 4,350 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 101,598,527 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 331,338 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Hanoi
  • Highest point: Fan Si Pan, 3,147 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 47.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Vietnam, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Mekong: 4,350 km
  • Hong: 1,149 km

Also on the map: Kong, Krong, Da, Ca, Zuo and Ba.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Fan Si Pan: 3,147 m (its highest point)
  • Ngoc Linh: 2,598 m
  • Chuo Yang Sin: 2,420 m

Mountain ranges: Chaine Annamitique and Central Highlands.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Indochina Peninsula, Ca Mau Point and Mekong Delta.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 30.4%, wooded savanna 19.3%, savanna 18.8%, farmland 12.5%, farmland with natural plants 7.6%, grassland 4.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 45.4%, plains 27.2%, hills 26.3%, plateaus 1.1%.

Forests covered 47.1% of its land in 2024, up from 28.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #2 in tungsten among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #5 in fluorspar among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #9 in tin among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Vietnam shares land borders with Cambodia, China and Laos on this map.
