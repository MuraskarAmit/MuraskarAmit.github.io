Libya: map and facts

Libya is in Northern Africa. It has about 7.46 million people (2025), the 104th largest population. It covers 1,759,540 km², the 16th largest by area. That is about 4.2 people per km². Its highest point is Pic Bette, at 2,267 m.

Facts

  • Population: 7,458,555 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,759,540 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 1,630,000 km².
  • Capital: Tripoli
  • Highest point: Pic Bette, 2,267 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pic Bette: 2,267 m (its highest point)
  • Jabal al Uwaynt: 1,934 m

Mountain ranges: Tibesti Mountains, Al Jabal Al Akhdar and Jabal Bin Ghunaymah.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Sahara, Libyan Desert, Idhan Murzuq, Sarir Kalanshiyu ar Ramli al Kabi, Hamada de Tinrhert, Jabal Nafusah, Libyan Plateau, Al Haruj al Aswad and Jabal as Sawda’.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 98.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 60.7%, plains 32.5%, hills 6.5%.

Forests covered 0.1% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Libya shares land borders with Algeria, Chad, Egypt, Niger, Sudan and Tunisia on this map.
