Puerto Rico: map and facts

Puerto Rico is in the Caribbean. It has about 3.18 million people (2025). It covers 8,870 km². That is about 359 people per km². Its highest point is Cerro de Punta, at 1,338 m.

Facts

  • Population: 3,184,835 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 8,870 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: San Juan
  • Highest point: Cerro de Punta, 1,338 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 49.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Cerro de Punta: 1,338 m (its highest point)
  • Pico El Yunque: 1,080 m

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 39.4%, evergreen broadleaf forest 26.7%, towns and cities 13.9%, farmland 5.9%, grassland 5.6%, savanna 3.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 54.2%, mountains 33.7%, plains 12.1%.

Forests covered 49.7% of its land in 2024, up from 34.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Puerto Rico has no land borders on this map.
