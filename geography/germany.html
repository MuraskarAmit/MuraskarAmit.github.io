Germany: map and facts

Germany is in Western Europe. It has about 83.5 million people (2025), the 19th largest population. It covers 357,680 km², the 63rd largest by area. That is about 233 people per km². Its highest point is Zugspitze, at 2,962 m. The longest river on its map is the Donau, 2,850 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 83,491,249 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 357,680 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Berlin
  • Highest point: Zugspitze, 2,962 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 32.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Germany, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Donau: 2,850 km
  • Rhine: 1,233 km
  • Elbe: 1,094 km
  • Oder: 854 km
  • Mosel: 544 km
  • Main: 524 km
  • Inn: 517 km
  • Weser: 451 km
  • Saale: 413 km
  • Spree: 382 km

Also on the map: Ems, Neckar, Isar, Aller, Sarre, Sure, Berkel, Waldnaab, Regnitz and Warme Steinach.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Zugspitze: 2,962 m (its highest point)
  • Feldberg: 1,494 m
  • Brocken: 1,141 m
  • Hohe Acht: 746 m
  • Vaalserberg: 322 m

Mountain ranges: Böhmerwald, Erzgebirge, Harz, Sudetes Mountains and Vosges Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Jutland and Frisian Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 33%, mixed forest 17.6%, savanna 16.7%, grassland 8.6%, evergreen conifer forest 7.3%, towns and cities 6.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 46.3%, hills 39.9%, plateaus 10.4%, mountains 3.4%.

Forests covered 32.8% of its land in 2024, up from 32.4% in 1990 (World Bank).
