Sint Maarten: map and facts

Sint Maarten is in the Caribbean. It has 43,923 people (2025). It covers 34 km². That is about 1,292 people per km².

Facts

  • Population: 43,923 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 34 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 24 km².
  • Capital: Philipsburg
  • Forest: 8.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): towns and cities 46.4%, grassland 17.9%, water 14.3%, evergreen broadleaf forest 7.1%, savanna 7.1%, farmland 3.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 100%.

Forests covered 8.8% of its land in 2024, the same as in 2015 (World Bank).
