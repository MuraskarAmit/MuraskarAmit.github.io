Brazil: map and facts

Brazil is in South America. It has about 213 million people (2025), the 7th largest population. It covers 8,510,420 km², the 5th largest by area. That is about 25 people per km². Its highest point is Pico da Neblina, at 2,994 m. The longest river on its map is the Japura, 2,820 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 212,812,405 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 8,510,420 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Brasília
  • Highest point: Pico da Neblina, 2,994 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 58.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Brazil, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Japura: 2,820 km

Also on the map: Jequitinhonha, Juruena, Paranaíba, Tapajós, Culuene, Indaia, Amazon, Javaés, Tocantins and Purus.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pico da Neblina: 2,994 m (its highest point)
  • Pico da Bandeira: 2,891 m
  • Pico da Pedra da Mina: 2,798 m
  • Pico do Barbado: 2,033 m
  • Pico Parana: 1,877 m
  • Morro da Boa Vista: 1,827 m
  • Pouso Alto: 1,691 m
  • Pico do Jabre: 1,197 m

Mountain ranges: Selvas, Guiana Highlands, Serra da Mantiqueira, Serra do Mar, Serra Geral, Pakaraima Mountains, Serra do Cachimbo, Serra do Piaui, Serra dos Gradaus, Serra Dourada, Acarai Mountains and Serra da Ibiapaba.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Amazon Basin, Brazilian Highlands, Caatingas, Mato Grosso Plateau, Amazon Delta, Guiana Shield, Pantanal, Planalto Central, Chapada do Araripe and Borborema Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 42.1%, savanna 25%, grassland 20.7%, farmland 4.2%, wooded savanna 3.9%, deciduous broadleaf forest 1.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 49.4%, hills 27.7%, plateaus 19.9%, mountains 3%.

Forests covered 58.5% of its land in 2024, down from 74% in 1990 (World Bank).
