Estonia: map and facts

Estonia is in Northern Europe. It has about 1.37 million people (2025), the 153rd largest population. It covers 45,340 km², the 129th largest by area. That is about 30 people per km². Its highest point is Suur Munamagi, at 317 m. The longest river on its map is the Gauja, 453 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 1,366,475 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 45,340 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Tallinn
  • Highest point: Suur Munamagi, 317 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 57.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Estonia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Gauja: 453 km
  • Parnu: 145 km
  • Emajogi: 100 km
  • Narva: 74 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Suur Munamagi: 317 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Baltic Plains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): mixed forest 42.2%, wooded savanna 25.5%, grassland 7.7%, farmland 6.6%, water 5.3%, savanna 5.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 99.7%.

Forests covered 57.2% of its land in 2024, up from 52.4% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Estonia shares land borders with Latvia and Russia on this map.
