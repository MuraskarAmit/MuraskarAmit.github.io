Mozambique: map and facts

Mozambique is in Eastern Africa. It has about 35.6 million people (2025), the 46th largest population. It covers 799,380 km², the 34th largest by area. That is about 45 people per km². Its highest point is Monte Binga, at 2,436 m. The longest river on its map is the Zambezi, 2,574 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 35,631,653 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 799,380 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Maputo
  • Highest point: Monte Binga, 2,436 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 41.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Mozambique, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Zambezi: 2,574 km
  • Limpopo: 1,750 km
  • Ruvuma: 800 km
  • Luangwa: 770 km
  • Save: 640 km
  • Shire: 402 km
  • Maputo: 300 km

Also on the map: Lurio, Mazowe, Ligonha, Licungo and Pungwe.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Monte Binga: 2,436 m (its highest point)
  • Serra Namuli: 2,419 m

Mountain ranges: Eastern Highlands and Lebombo Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Zambezi Delta.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 54.6%, grassland 24.4%, deciduous broadleaf forest 8%, wooded savanna 7.2%, mixed forest 1.7%, water 1.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 44.9%, hills 26.9%, plateaus 23.2%, mountains 5%.

Forests covered 41.3% of its land in 2024, down from 52.9% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #2 in titanium among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #3 in zircon among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #5 in graphite among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Mozambique shares land borders with Eswatini, Malawi, South Africa, Tanzania, Zambia and Zimbabwe on this map.
