Belize: map and facts

Belize is in Central America. It has 422,924 people (2025), the 169th largest population. It covers 22,966 km², the 147th largest by area. That is about 18 people per km². Its highest point is Doyle's Delight, at 1,124 m. The longest river on its map is the Hondo, 150 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 422,924 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 22,966 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 22,400 km².
  • Capital: Belmopan
  • Highest point: Doyle's Delight, 1,124 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 58% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Belize, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Hondo: 150 km
  • Sarstun: 111 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Doyle's Delight: 1,124 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Maya Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Yucatan Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 67.4%, grassland 10.1%, wooded savanna 9.8%, savanna 6.8%, wetland 3.3%, water 2.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 58.6%, hills 31.2%, mountains 10.2%.

Forests covered 58% of its land in 2024, down from 69.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Belize shares land borders with Guatemala and Mexico on this map.
