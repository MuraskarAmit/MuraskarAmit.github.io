Israel: map and facts

Israel is in Western Asia. It has about 10.1 million people (2025), the 95th largest population. It covers 22,070 km², the 148th largest by area. That is about 459 people per km². Its highest point is Har Meron, at 1,208 m. The longest river on its map is the Jordan, 252 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 10,122,800 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 22,070 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 20,900 km².
  • Capital not shown: its status is disputed.
  • Highest point: Har Meron, 1,208 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 6.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Israel, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Jordan: 252 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Har Meron: 1,208 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Levant Coast and Negev Desert.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 54%, farmland 18.7%, grassland 14.3%, towns and cities 6%, open shrubland 3.4%, water 1.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 56.8%, plains 23.8%, mountains 14.5%, plateaus 4.9%.

Forests covered 6.8% of its land in 2024, up from 6.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #7 in potash among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Israel shares land borders with Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Palestine and Syria on this map.
