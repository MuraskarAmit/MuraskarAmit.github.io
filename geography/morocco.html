Morocco: map and facts

Morocco is in Northern Africa. It has about 38.4 million people (2025), the 41st largest population. It covers 446,550 km², the 57th largest by area. That is about 86 people per km². Its highest point is Jebel Toubkal, at 4,167 m. The longest river on its map is the Moulouya, 520 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 38,430,770 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 446,550 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 683,000 km².
  • Capital: Rabat
  • Highest point: Jebel Toubkal, 4,167 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 12.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Morocco, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Moulouya: 520 km
  • Sous: 190 km

Also on the map: Oued Sebou and Oum Er Rbia.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Jebel Toubkal: 4,167 m (its highest point)
  • Adrar Bou Nasser: 3,340 m
  • Jebel Tidirhine: 2,456 m
  • Jebel Lekst: 2,359 m

Mountain ranges: Atlas Mountains, Haut Atlas, Anti-Atlas, Atlas Tellien, Rif and Moyen Atlas.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Hamada du Draa.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 65.5%, open shrubland 18.1%, farmland 8.4%, grassland 6.3%, savanna 1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 32.8%, hills 27%, plateaus 22.9%, mountains 16.4%.

Forests covered 12.8% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #2 in phosphate among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #3 in barite among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Morocco shares land borders with Algeria, Mauritania and Spain on this map.
