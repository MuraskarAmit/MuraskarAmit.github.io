Afghanistan: map and facts

Afghanistan is in Southern Asia. It has about 43.8 million people (2025), the 36th largest population. It covers 652,870 km², the 40th largest by area. That is about 67 people per km². Its highest point is Nowshak, at 7,492 m. The longest river on its map is the Amu Darya, 2,620 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 43,844,111 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 652,870 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Kabul
  • Highest point: Nowshak, 7,492 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 1.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Afghanistan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Amu Darya: 2,620 km
  • Helmand: 1,150 km
  • Panj: 921 km

Also on the map: Pamir and Harirud.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Nowshak: 7,492 m (its highest point)
  • Shah Fuladi: 5,048 m
  • Sikaram: 4,755 m
  • Kuh-e Jang Qal'eh: 4,171 m

Mountain ranges: Hindu Kush, Paropamisus Range and Sulaiman Range.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Baluchistan, Margo Desert and Rigestan.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 47.5%, grassland 37.5%, open shrubland 11.6%, farmland 1.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 26.5%, plateaus 25.4%, high mountains 24.9%, hills 21.7%, plains 1.5%.

Forests covered 1.9% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Afghanistan shares land borders with China, India, Iran, Pakistan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan on this map.
