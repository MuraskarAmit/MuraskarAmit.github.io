British Virgin Islands: map and facts

The British Virgin Islands is in the Caribbean. It has 39,732 people (2025). It covers 151 km². That is about 263 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Sage, at 523 m.

Facts

  • Population: 39,732 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 151 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 145 km².
  • Capital: Road Town
  • Highest point: Mount Sage, 523 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 66% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Sage: 523 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Virgin Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wetland 32.6%, evergreen broadleaf forest 16%, water 16%, grassland 14.4%, wooded savanna 13.3%, savanna 5.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 69.1%, plains 30.9%.

Forests covered 66% of its land in 2024, down from 66.7% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The British Virgin Islands has no land borders on this map.
