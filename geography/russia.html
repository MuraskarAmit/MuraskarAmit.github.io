Russia: map and facts

Russia is in Eastern Europe. It has about 144 million people (2025), the 9th largest population. It covers 17,125,190 km², the largest by area. That is about 8.4 people per km². Its highest point is Gora Elbrus, at 5,642 m. The longest river on its map is the Lena, 4,294 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 143,513,328 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 17,125,190 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Moscow
  • Highest point: Gora Elbrus, 5,642 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 50.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Gora Elbrus: 5,642 m (its highest point)
  • Gora Shkhara: 5,193 m
  • Klyuchevskaya Sopka: 4,754 m
  • Belukha: 4,506 m
  • Gora Tebulosmta: 4,493 m
  • Bazarduzu: 4,466 m
  • Huiten Peak: 4,374 m
  • Dombai-Ulgen: 4,046 m

Mountain ranges: Caucasus Mountains, Ural Mountains, Altai Mountains, Stanovoy Range, Eastern Sayan Mountains, Sikhote-Alin, Verkhoyansk Range, Yablonovyy Range, Abakan Range, Cherskiy Range, Kolyma Range and Koryak Range.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Kazakh Steppe, Siberia, Bolshezemelskaya Tundra, Central Siberian Plateau, Kamchatka Peninsula, Pontic Steppe, Taymyr Peninsula, Yamal Peninsula, Aldan Upland, Caspian Depression, Central Russian Upland and Franz Josef Land.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): open shrubland 20.7%, wooded savanna 16.3%, mixed forest 15.1%, savanna 13.8%, grassland 12.7%, farmland 6.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 48.2%, hills 29.3%, mountains 16.9%, plateaus 5.4%.

Forests covered 50.8% of its land in 2024, up from 49.6% in 1995 (World Bank).
