Guernsey: map and facts

Guernsey is in Northern Europe. It has 64,017 people (2023). It covers 78 km². That is about 821 people per km². Its highest point is Le Moulin, at 114 m.

Facts

  • Population: 64,017 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 78 km² (CIA World Factbook (archived)). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 74 km².
  • Capital: Saint Peter Port
  • Highest point: Le Moulin, 114 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Le Moulin: 114 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 51.1%, wetland 16.8%, towns and cities 13.7%, farmland with natural plants 11.5%, water 4.6%, evergreen broadleaf forest 1.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Neighbours

Guernsey has no land borders on this map.
