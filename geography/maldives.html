Maldives: map and facts

The Maldives is in Southern Asia. It has 529,676 people (2025), the 166th largest population. It covers 300 km², the 186th largest by area. That is about 1,766 people per km². Its highest point is Villingili, at 5 m.

Facts

  • Population: 529,676 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 300 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 103 km².
  • Capital: Malé
  • Highest point: Villingili, 5 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 12.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Villingili: 5 m (its highest point)
  • Addu City: 2 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Maldive Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): water 88.7%, bare ground 7.3%, towns and cities 2.4%, wetland 1.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 12.5% of its land in 2024, up from 12.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The Maldives has no land borders on this map.
