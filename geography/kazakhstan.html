Kazakhstan: map and facts

Kazakhstan is in Central Asia. It has about 20.8 million people (2025), the 64th largest population. It covers 2,724,902 km², the 9th largest by area. That is about 7.6 people per km². Its highest point is Khan Tengri, at 7,010 m. The longest river on its map is the Irtysh, 4,248 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 20,843,754 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 2,724,902 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Astana
  • Highest point: Khan Tengri, 7,010 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 1.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Kazakhstan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Ishim: 2,450 km
  • Ural: 2,428 km
  • Syr Darya: 2,212 km
  • Tobol: 1,591 km
  • Emba: 712 km

Also on the map: Ili.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Khan Tengri: 7,010 m (its highest point)
  • Pik Talghar: 4,979 m
  • Semenov-Tyan-Shansky Peak: 4,622 m
  • Belukha: 4,506 m
  • Gora Tastau: 2,992 m

Mountain ranges: Altai Mountains, Tarbagatay Range, Dzungarian Alatau and Karatau Range.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Kazakh Steppe, Betpaqdala Desert, Turan Lowland, Caspian Depression, Kyzylkum Desert, Kazakh Upland and Ustyurt Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 84.8%, bare ground 6.8%, farmland 4.2%, water 2.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 55.2%, plateaus 31%, hills 9.2%, mountains 3.8%.

Forests covered 1.3% of its land in 2024, up from 1.2% in 1995 (World Bank).
