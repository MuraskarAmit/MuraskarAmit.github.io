Saint Lucia: map and facts

Saint Lucia is in the Caribbean. It has 180,149 people (2025), the 176th largest population. It covers 616 km², the 177th largest by area. That is about 292 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Gimie, at 952 m.

Facts

  • Population: 180,149 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 616 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Castries
  • Highest point: Mount Gimie, 952 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 53.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Gimie: 952 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 50.8%, wooded savanna 25.1%, grassland 16.1%, towns and cities 2.9%, water 2.5%, wetland 1.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 66.9%, mountains 32.7%.

Forests covered 53.8% of its land in 2024, up from 44.4% in 1990 (World Bank).
