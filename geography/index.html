World geography: country maps and facts

A map and the facts for each of 247 countries and territories: population, area, capital, highest point, rivers, mountains, land cover and minerals, each with its year and source. Each map shows the country and what lies around it, and its caption says what is marked on it.

Open the world map

In the game you can zoom into any country’s rivers, mountains, minerals, terrain and forests. Free, with no sign-up.
