Guyana: map and facts

Guyana is in South America. It has 835,986 people (2025), the 160th largest population. It covers 214,970 km², the 83rd largest by area. That is about 3.9 people per km². The longest river on its map is the Essequibo, 1,014 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 835,986 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 214,970 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Georgetown
  • Forest: 87.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Guyana, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Essequibo: 1,014 km
  • Courantyne: 724 km
  • Cuyuni: 618 km

Also on the map: Corantijn.

Mountains and landscapes

Mountain ranges: Guiana Highlands and Serra Acaraí.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Guiana Shield.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 87.3%, grassland 6.1%, wooded savanna 3.3%, savanna 1.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 67.8%, hills 21.2%, mountains 10%, plateaus 1%.

Forests covered 87.1% of its land in 2024, down from 94.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Guyana shares land borders with Brazil, Suriname and Venezuela on this map.
