Dhekelia: map and facts

Dhekelia is in Western Asia. It covers 127 km².

Facts

  • Area: 127 km² (area as drawn on this map)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 66.3%, grassland 21%, towns and cities 7.7%, water 5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 77.3%, hills 22.7%.

Neighbours

Dhekelia shares land borders with Cyprus on this map.
