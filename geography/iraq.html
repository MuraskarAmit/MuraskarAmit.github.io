Iraq: map and facts

Iraq is in Western Asia. It has about 47 million people (2025), the 34th largest population. It covers 435,050 km², the 58th largest by area. That is about 108 people per km². Its highest point is Cheekha Dar, at 3,611 m. The longest river on its map is the Euphrates, 2,800 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 47,020,774 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 435,050 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Baghdad
  • Highest point: Cheekha Dar, 3,611 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 1.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Iraq, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Euphrates: 2,800 km
  • Tigris: 1,850 km
  • Karkheh: 870 km

Also on the map: Shatt al Arab, Gharraf Canal, Shatt al Hillah, Hindiyah Channel and Atshan.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Cheekha Dar: 3,611 m (its highest point)
  • Kuh-e Haji Ebrahim: 3,587 m

Mountain ranges: Zagros Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Mesopotamia and Syrian Desert.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 66.4%, grassland 14.4%, farmland 10.7%, open shrubland 6.5%, water 1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 59.2%, plateaus 29.1%, mountains 6.1%, hills 5.5%.

Forests covered 1.6% of its land in 2024, down from 1.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in oil among the countries EIA lists (2025)

Neighbours

Iraq shares land borders with Iran, Jordan, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Syria and Turkey on this map.
