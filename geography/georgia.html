Georgia: map and facts

Georgia is in Western Asia. It has about 3.94 million people (2025), the 127th largest population. It covers 69,700 km², the 119th largest by area. That is about 56 people per km². Its highest point is Gora Shkhara, at 5,193 m. The longest river on its map is the Kura, 1,360 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 3,935,766 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 69,700 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Tbilisi
  • Highest point: Gora Shkhara, 5,193 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 44.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Georgia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Kura: 1,360 km
  • Terek: 623 km
  • Coruh: 438 km
  • Alazani: 351 km
  • Rioni: 327 km
  • Iori: 320 km
  • Khrami: 201 km
  • Sulak: 169 km
  • Pambak: 86 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Gora Shkhara: 5,193 m (its highest point)
  • Gora Tebulosmta: 4,493 m
  • Dombai-Ulgen: 4,046 m

Mountain ranges: Caucasus Mountains and Lesser Caucasus.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 34.7%, deciduous broadleaf forest 30.9%, wooded savanna 13.7%, savanna 7.1%, mixed forest 6.2%, farmland 3.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 66.7%, hills 19%, high mountains 7.9%, plains 5.9%.

Forests covered 44.6% of its land in 2024, up from 43.7% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Georgia shares land borders with Armenia, Azerbaijan, Russia and Turkey on this map.
