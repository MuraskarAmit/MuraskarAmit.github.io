Ghana: map and facts

Ghana is in Western Africa. It has about 35.1 million people (2025), the 47th largest population. It covers 238,539 km², the 80th largest by area. That is about 147 people per km². The longest river on its map is the Black Volta, 1,352 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 35,064,272 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 238,539 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Accra
  • Forest: 30.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Ghana, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Black Volta: 1,352 km
  • Oti: 900 km
  • Volta: 467 km

Also on the map: White Volta.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Afadja: 587 m

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 43.2%, savanna 27.5%, wooded savanna 11.1%, farmland 6.6%, farmland with natural plants 3.6%, evergreen broadleaf forest 3.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 77.8%, hills 15.9%, plateaus 5.2%, mountains 1.1%.

Forests covered 30.9% of its land in 2024, up from 25.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #3 in manganese among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #6 in gold among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Ghana shares land borders with Burkina Faso, Cote d'Ivoire and Togo on this map.
