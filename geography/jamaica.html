Jamaica: map and facts

Jamaica is in the Caribbean. It has about 2.84 million people (2025), the 138th largest population. It covers 10,991 km², the 160th largest by area. That is about 258 people per km². Its highest point is Blue Mountain Peak, at 2,256 m.

Facts

  • Population: 2,837,077 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 10,991 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Kingston
  • Highest point: Blue Mountain Peak, 2,256 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 56.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Blue Mountain Peak: 2,256 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 41.8%, wooded savanna 38.5%, savanna 4.3%, farmland 3.4%, grassland 3.4%, towns and cities 3.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 47.1%, hills 47%, plains 5.9%.

Forests covered 56.5% of its land in 2024, up from 48.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #7 in bauxite among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Jamaica has no land borders on this map.
