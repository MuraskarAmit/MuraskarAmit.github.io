Tonga: map and facts

Tonga is in Polynesia. It has 103,742 people (2025), the 181st largest population. It covers 749 km², the 174th largest by area. That is about 139 people per km². Its highest point is Kao, at 1,030 m.

Facts

  • Population: 103,742 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 749 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 604 km².
  • Capital: Nuku'alofa
  • Highest point: Kao, 1,030 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 12.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Kao: 1,030 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Tonga Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 25%, evergreen broadleaf forest 21.7%, water 19.4%, grassland 13.6%, wetland 13.1%, savanna 3.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 68.9%, hills 31.1%.

Forests covered 12.1% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Tonga has no land borders on this map.
