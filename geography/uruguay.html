Uruguay: map and facts

Uruguay is in South America. It has about 3.38 million people (2025), the 131st largest population. It covers 176,215 km², the 89th largest by area. That is about 19 people per km². Its highest point is Cerro Catedral, at 514 m. The longest river on its map is the Uruguay, 1,790 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 3,384,688 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 176,215 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Montevideo
  • Highest point: Cerro Catedral, 514 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 11.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

  • Uruguay: 1,790 km

Also on the map: Negro.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Cerro Catedral: 514 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Cuchilla Grande.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 47%, savanna 40.1%, evergreen broadleaf forest 5.5%, farmland 4.2%, water 1.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 91.8%, hills 7.8%.

Forests covered 11.5% of its land in 2024, up from 5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Uruguay shares land borders with Argentina, Brazil and Brazilian Island on this map.
