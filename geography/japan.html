Japan: map and facts

Japan is in Eastern Asia. It has about 123 million people (2025), the 12th largest population. It covers 377,975 km², the 62nd largest by area. That is about 326 people per km². Its highest point is Fuji, at 3,777 m. The longest river on its map is the Tone, 322 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 123,366,734 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 377,975 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Tokyo
  • Highest point: Fuji, 3,777 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 68.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Japan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Tone: 322 km
  • Ishikari: 268 km
  • Mogami: 229 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Fuji: 3,777 m (its highest point)
  • Tate-yama: 3,015 m
  • Asahi-dake: 2,291 m
  • Iwate-san: 2,038 m
  • Ishizuchi-san: 1,982 m
  • Kuju-san: 1,787 m
  • Unzen-dake: 1,486 m
  • Sakurajima: 1,117 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Ryukyu Islands, Amami Islands, Okinawa Island, Izu Islands, Noto Peninsula, Oki Islands, Daitō Islands, Osumi Islands and Sakishima Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): mixed forest 28%, deciduous broadleaf forest 24.4%, towns and cities 9.1%, evergreen conifer forest 8.9%, evergreen broadleaf forest 7%, farmland 6.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 52.7%, hills 35.8%, plains 11.4%.

Forests covered 68.3% of its land in 2024, down from 68.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Japan has no land borders on this map.
