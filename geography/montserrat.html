Montserrat: map and facts

Montserrat is in the Caribbean. It has 4,420 people (2023). It covers 100 km². That is about 44 people per km². Its highest point is Soufriere Hills Volcano, at 1,050 m.

Facts

  • Population: 4,420 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 100 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Brades, Plymouth
  • Highest point: Soufriere Hills Volcano, 1,050 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Soufriere Hills Volcano: 1,050 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 30.1%, evergreen broadleaf forest 23.6%, wooded savanna 19.5%, savanna 7.3%, wetland 7.3%, towns and cities 5.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 96.7%, hills 3.3%.

Neighbours

Montserrat has no land borders on this map.
