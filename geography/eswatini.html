Eswatini: map and facts

Eswatini is in Southern Africa. It has about 1.26 million people (2025), the 154th largest population. It covers 17,364 km², the 153rd largest by area. That is about 72 people per km². Its highest point is Emlembe, at 1,862 m.

Facts

  • Population: 1,256,174 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 17,364 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Lobamba, Mbabane
  • Highest point: Emlembe, 1,862 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 25.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Emlembe: 1,862 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Lebombo Mountains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 47.7%, savanna 30.3%, deciduous broadleaf forest 6.4%, evergreen broadleaf forest 5.6%, wooded savanna 4.1%, farmland 3.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 55.4%, mountains 31.7%, plains 10%, plateaus 2.9%.

Forests covered 25.3% of its land in 2024, down from 28.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Eswatini shares land borders with Mozambique and South Africa on this map.
