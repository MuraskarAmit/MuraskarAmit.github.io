Serbia: map and facts

Serbia is in Southern Europe. It has about 6.55 million people (2025), the 108th largest population. It covers 88,490 km², the 111th largest by area. That is about 74 people per km². Its highest point is Velika Rudoka, at 2,658 m. The longest river on its map is the Danube, 2,850 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 6,549,143 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 88,490 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Belgrade
  • Highest point: Velika Rudoka, 2,658 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 39.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Serbia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Danube: 2,850 km
  • Tisza: 966 km
  • Sava: 945 km
  • Timis: 359 km
  • Drina: 346 km
  • Lim: 220 km
  • Nišava: 218 km
  • Timok: 189 km
  • West Morava: 184 km
  • White Drin: 175 km

Also on the map: Sitnica and Morava.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Velika Rudoka: 2,658 m (its highest point)
  • Gjeravica: 2,656 m
  • Midzor: 2,169 m

Mountain ranges: Dinaric Alps.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Balkan Peninsula and Great Hungarian Plain.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 30.7%, deciduous broadleaf forest 22.2%, wooded savanna 18%, savanna 11.1%, farmland with natural plants 9.6%, grassland 4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 37%, hills 33.3%, plains 29%.

Forests covered 39.2% of its land in 2024, up from 32.9% in 2010 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Serbia shares land borders with Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Hungary, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Romania on this map.
