Turks and Caicos Islands: map and facts

The Turks and Caicos Islands is in the Caribbean. It has 46,855 people (2025). It covers 948 km². That is about 49 people per km². Its highest point is Blue Hills, at 49 m.

Facts

  • Population: 46,855 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 948 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 447 km².
  • Capital: Cockburn Town
  • Highest point: Blue Hills, 49 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 11.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Blue Hills: 49 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 32.9%, wetland 19.5%, water 17.5%, evergreen broadleaf forest 14.3%, savanna 7.3%, wooded savanna 3.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 11.1% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The Turks and Caicos Islands has no land borders on this map.
