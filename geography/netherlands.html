Netherlands: map and facts

The Netherlands is in Western Europe. It has about 18.1 million people (2025), the 71st largest population. It covers 41,540 km², the 131st largest by area. That is about 435 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Scenery, at 870 m. The longest river on its map is the Rhine, 1,233 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 18,087,633 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 41,540 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 37,300 km².
  • Capital: Amsterdam
  • Highest point: Mount Scenery, 870 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 11% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of the Netherlands, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Rhine: 1,233 km
  • Maas: 950 km
  • Vecht: 167 km
  • IJssel: 123 km
  • Berkel: 115 km
  • Waal: 82 km
  • Lek: 62 km
  • Nederrijn: 50 km
  • Vecht: 40 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Scenery: 870 m (its highest point)
  • Vaalserberg: 322 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Frisian Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 32.6%, farmland 17.2%, grassland 12.4%, towns and cities 12.1%, farmland with natural plants 10%, water 6.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 99.6%.

Forests covered 11% of its land in 2024, up from 10.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #3 in silica sand among the countries USGS lists (2025)
