Spain: map and facts

Spain is in Southern Europe. It has about 49.4 million people (2025), the 32nd largest population. It covers 505,981 km², the 51st largest by area. That is about 98 people per km². Its highest point is Pico del Teide, at 3,715 m. The longest river on its map is the Tagus, 1,007 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 49,355,143 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 505,981 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Madrid
  • Highest point: Pico del Teide, 3,715 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 38.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Spain, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Tagus: 1,007 km
  • Ebro: 910 km
  • Douro: 897 km
  • Guadiana: 778 km
  • Guadalquivir: 657 km
  • Garonne: 575 km
  • Jucar: 509 km
  • Segura: 325 km
  • Minho: 315 km
  • Esla: 285 km

Also on the map: Pisuerga, Segre, Sil, Aragón, Tiétar, Henares, Mijares, Jiloca, Zancara and Mino.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pico del Teide: 3,715 m (its highest point)
  • Mulhacén: 3,479 m
  • Pico de Aneto: 3,404 m
  • Pic de Coma Pedrosa: 2,942 m
  • Torre de Cerredo: 2,648 m
  • Pico de Almanzor: 2,591 m
  • Moncayo: 2,313 m
  • Mount San Lorenzo: 2,271 m

Mountain ranges: Pyrenees, Cantabrian Mountains, Sierra Nevada and Sierra Morena.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Iberian Peninsula, Balearic Islands and Canary Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 24.5%, grassland 23.1%, savanna 16%, wooded savanna 13.2%, open shrubland 11.3%, evergreen conifer forest 4.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 50.2%, mountains 29.7%, plateaus 14.4%, plains 5.6%.

Forests covered 38.2% of its land in 2024, up from 27.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in gypsum among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #6 in fluorspar among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #10 in tungsten among the countries USGS lists (2025)
