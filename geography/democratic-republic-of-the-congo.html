Democratic Republic of the Congo: map and facts

The Democratic Republic of the Congo is in Middle Africa. It has about 113 million people (2025), the 15th largest population. It covers 2,345,410 km², the 11th largest by area. That is about 48 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Stanley, at 5,109 m. The longest river on its map is the Congo, 4,700 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 112,832,473 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 2,345,410 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Kinshasa
  • Highest point: Mount Stanley, 5,109 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 61.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Congo: 4,700 km
  • Kasai: 2,272 km
  • Kwango: 1,770 km
  • Aruwimi: 1,300 km
  • Uele: 1,210 km
  • Sankuru: 1,200 km
  • Ubangi: 1,060 km
  • Tshuapa: 1,000 km
  • Kwilu: 965 km
  • Luapula: 560 km

Also on the map: Busira, Semliki, Rusizi, Ruki, Lualaba, Kibali, Lomami, Bomu, Ituri and Lukenie.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Stanley: 5,109 m (its highest point)
  • Volcan Karisimbi: 4,507 m
  • Mont Mohi: 3,475 m
  • Kabobo: 2,725 m

Mountain ranges: Mitumba Mountains and Ruwenzori Range.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Congo Basin and Katanga Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 51.1%, savanna 26.2%, wooded savanna 15.6%, grassland 2.5%, deciduous broadleaf forest 1.7%, mixed forest 1.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 73.8%, hills 22.2%, mountains 3.2%.

Forests covered 61.5% of its land in 2024, down from 70.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #1 in cobalt among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #2 in copper among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #3 in niobium among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #5 in tin among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

The Democratic Republic of the Congo shares land borders with Angola, Burundi, Central African Republic, Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, South Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda and Zambia on this map.
