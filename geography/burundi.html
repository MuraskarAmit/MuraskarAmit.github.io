Burundi: map and facts

Burundi is in Eastern Africa. It has about 14.4 million people (2025), the 77th largest population. It covers 27,834 km², the 142nd largest by area. That is about 517 people per km². Its highest point is Mont Heha, at 2,684 m. The longest river on its map is the Kagera, 400 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 14,390,003 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 27,834 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 27,200 km².
  • Capital: Gitega
  • Highest point: Mont Heha, 2,684 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 10.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Burundi, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Kagera: 400 km
  • Rusizi: 117 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mont Heha: 2,684 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 27.8%, farmland with natural plants 23.1%, savanna 20.8%, grassland 17.3%, water 7.7%, evergreen broadleaf forest 1.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 60.6%, mountains 28.6%, plateaus 10.7%.

Forests covered 10.9% of its land in 2024, up from 10.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Burundi shares land borders with Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda and Tanzania on this map.
