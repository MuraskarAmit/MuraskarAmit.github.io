Saint Martin: map and facts

Saint Martin is in the Caribbean. It has 24,941 people (2025). It covers 53 km². That is about 471 people per km². Its highest point is Pic du Paradis, at 424 m.

Facts

  • Population: 24,941 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 53 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 69 km².
  • Capital: Marigot
  • Highest point: Pic du Paradis, 424 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 23.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pic du Paradis: 424 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 32.9%, towns and cities 16.5%, wetland 16.5%, water 14.1%, savanna 8.2%, wooded savanna 4.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 92.9%, plains 7.1%.

Forests covered 23.4% of its land in 2024, down from 24.8% in 2015 (World Bank).
