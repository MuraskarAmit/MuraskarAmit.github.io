Singapore: map and facts

Singapore is in South-Eastern Asia. It has about 6.11 million people (2025), the 112th largest population. It covers 736 km², the 175th largest by area. That is about 8,303 people per km². Its highest point is Bukit Timah, at 164 m.

Facts

  • Population: 6,111,175 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 736 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 513 km².
  • Capital: Singapore
  • Highest point: Bukit Timah, 164 m (CIA World Factbook (archived))
  • Forest: 21.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Bukit Timah: 164 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): towns and cities 72.6%, evergreen broadleaf forest 9%, wooded savanna 8%, savanna 3.7%, grassland 3.5%, water 2.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 21.8% of its land in 2024, down from 22.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Singapore has no land borders on this map.
