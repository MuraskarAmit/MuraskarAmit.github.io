Antigua and Barbuda: map and facts

Antigua and Barbuda is in the Caribbean. It has 94,209 people (2025), the 182nd largest population. It covers 440 km², the 181st largest by area. That is about 214 people per km². Its highest point is Boggy Peak, at 402 m.

Facts

  • Population: 94,209 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 440 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 453 km².
  • Capital: Saint John's
  • Highest point: Boggy Peak, 402 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 17.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Boggy Peak: 402 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 35.6%, wooded savanna 19.6%, grassland 18%, water 13.3%, evergreen broadleaf forest 6.6%, wetland 4.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 68.1%, hills 31.9%.

Forests covered 17.9% of its land in 2024, down from 23% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Antigua and Barbuda has no land borders on this map.
