Spratly Islands: map and facts

The Spratly Islands is in South-Eastern Asia. It covers 12 km².

Facts

  • Area: 12 km² (area as drawn on this map)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): water 100%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Neighbours

The Spratly Islands has no land borders on this map.
