Malawi: map and facts

Malawi is in Eastern Africa. It has about 22.2 million people (2025), the 60th largest population. It covers 118,484 km², the 99th largest by area. That is about 188 people per km². Its highest point is Mulanje Massif, at 3,002 m. The longest river on its map is the Shire, 402 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 22,216,120 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 118,484 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Lilongwe
  • Highest point: Mulanje Massif, 3,002 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 22% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Malawi, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Shire: 402 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mulanje Massif: 3,002 m (its highest point)
  • Sapitwa Peak: 3,002 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Great Rift Valley.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 34.2%, water 19.4%, farmland 19.2%, savanna 18.7%, wooded savanna 2.4%, mixed forest 2.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 42%, hills 36.4%, mountains 19.1%, plains 2.5%.

Forests covered 22% of its land in 2024, down from 37.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Malawi shares land borders with Mozambique, Tanzania and Zambia on this map.
