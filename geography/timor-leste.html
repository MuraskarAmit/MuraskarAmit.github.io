Timor-Leste: map and facts

Timor-Leste is in South-Eastern Asia. It has about 1.42 million people (2025), the 150th largest population. It covers 14,870 km², the 154th largest by area. That is about 95 people per km². Its highest point is Tata Mailau, at 2,963 m.

Facts

  • Population: 1,418,517 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 14,870 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Dili
  • Highest point: Tata Mailau, 2,963 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 71% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Tata Mailau: 2,963 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Lesser Sunda Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 38%, wooded savanna 31.3%, evergreen broadleaf forest 17.1%, grassland 5.3%, deciduous broadleaf forest 3.2%, farmland 2.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 71.2%, hills 26.2%, plains 2.1%.

Forests covered 71% of its land in 2024, down from 74% in 1990 (World Bank).
