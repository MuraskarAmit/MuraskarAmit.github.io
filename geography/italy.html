Italy: map and facts

Italy is in Southern Europe. It has about 58.9 million people (2025), the 25th largest population. It covers 302,070 km², the 71st largest by area. That is about 195 people per km². Its highest point is Mont Blanc, at 4,806 m. The longest river on its map is the Po, 652 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 58,915,656 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 302,070 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Rome
  • Highest point: Mont Blanc, 4,806 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 31.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Italy, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Po: 652 km
  • Adige: 410 km
  • Tevere: 405 km
  • Adda: 313 km
  • Oglio: 280 km
  • Tanaro: 276 km
  • Ticino: 248 km
  • Arno: 241 km
  • Piave: 220 km
  • Reno: 211 km

Also on the map: Volturno, Ofanto, Dora Baltea, Simeto, Varaita, Sacco, Mincio and Salto.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mont Blanc: 4,806 m (its highest point)
  • Monte Rosa: 4,634 m
  • Matterhorn: 4,478 m
  • Monte Etna: 3,322 m
  • Corno Grande: 2,912 m
  • Monte Viglio: 2,156 m
  • Punta la Marmora: 1,834 m
  • Vesuvio: 1,281 m

Mountain ranges: Alps, Appennini and Calabria.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Riviera.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 33.5%, savanna 15.7%, wooded savanna 11.6%, deciduous broadleaf forest 11.3%, grassland 9.9%, towns and cities 4.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 51.2%, hills 28.4%, plains 18.5%, high mountains 1.4%.

Forests covered 31.8% of its land in 2024, up from 25.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in silica sand among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Italy shares land borders with Austria, France, San Marino, Slovenia, Switzerland and Vatican on this map.
