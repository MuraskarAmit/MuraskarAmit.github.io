United States Virgin Islands: map and facts

The United States Virgin Islands is in the Caribbean. It has 84,138 people (2025). It covers 348 km². That is about 242 people per km². Its highest point is Crown Mountain, at 473 m.

Facts

  • Population: 84,138 (2025, UN World Population Prospects 2024)
  • Area: 348 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 356 km².
  • Capital: Charlotte Amalie
  • Highest point: Crown Mountain, 473 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 58.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Crown Mountain: 473 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Virgin Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 18.8%, wooded savanna 18.8%, evergreen broadleaf forest 17.9%, water 10.6%, savanna 9.2%, towns and cities 9.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 98.2%, plains 1.8%.

Forests covered 58.9% of its land in 2024, up from 58.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The United States Virgin Islands has no land borders on this map.
