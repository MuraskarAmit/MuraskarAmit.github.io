Nigeria: map and facts

Nigeria is in Western Africa. It has about 238 million people (2025), the 6th largest population. It covers 923,768 km², the 31st largest by area. That is about 257 people per km². Its highest point is Chappal Waddi, at 2,419 m. The longest river on its map is the Niger, 4,180 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 237,527,782 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 923,768 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Abuja
  • Highest point: Chappal Waddi, 2,419 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 18.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Nigeria, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Niger: 4,180 km
  • Benue: 1,370 km
  • Sokoto: 600 km
  • Kaduna: 550 km
  • Gongola: 531 km
  • Komadugu Yobe: 467 km
  • Ogun: 300 km

Also on the map: Mariga, Jama'are and Hadejia.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Chappal Waddi: 2,419 m (its highest point)
  • Mount Dimlang: 1,687 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Niger Delta, Adamawa Plateau and Jos Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 39.7%, farmland 36.4%, savanna 11.3%, farmland with natural plants 5.6%, wooded savanna 2.3%, evergreen broadleaf forest 2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 41.7%, plateaus 39%, hills 15.6%, mountains 3.7%.

Forests covered 18.9% of its land in 2024, down from 23.7% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #10 in rare earths among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Nigeria shares land borders with Benin, Cameroon, Chad and Niger on this map.
