Trinidad and Tobago: map and facts

Trinidad and Tobago is in the Caribbean. It has about 1.37 million people (2025), the 152nd largest population. It covers 5,128 km², the 164th largest by area. That is about 267 people per km². Its highest point is Aripo Peak, at 940 m.

Facts

  • Population: 1,367,764 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 5,128 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Port of Spain
  • Highest point: Aripo Peak, 940 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 44.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Aripo Peak: 940 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Lesser Antilles.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 52.9%, wooded savanna 16.8%, grassland 11.5%, savanna 7.6%, towns and cities 7%, wetland 1.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 59.2%, hills 27.4%, mountains 13.4%.

Forests covered 44.2% of its land in 2024, down from 47% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Trinidad and Tobago has no land borders on this map.
