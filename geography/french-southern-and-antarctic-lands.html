French Southern and Antarctic Lands: map and facts

The French Southern and Antarctic Lands is in the Indian Ocean. It covers 7,668 km². Its highest point is Grand Ross, at 1,850 m.

Facts

  • Area: 7,668 km² (CIA World Factbook (archived)). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 7,260 km².
  • Capital not shown: its status is disputed.
  • Highest point: Grand Ross, 1,850 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Grand Ross: 1,850 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Kerguelen Islands and Crozet Islands.

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 68%, hills 23.1%, plains 8.9%.

Neighbours

The French Southern and Antarctic Lands has no land borders on this map.
