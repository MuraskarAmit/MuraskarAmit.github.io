Republic of Korea: map and facts

The Republic of Korea is in Eastern Asia. It has about 51.7 million people (2025), the 29th largest population. It covers 100,460 km², the 107th largest by area. That is about 514 people per km². Its highest point is Halla-san, at 1,947 m. The longest river on its map is the Han, 514 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 51,684,564 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 100,460 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Seoul
  • Highest point: Halla-san, 1,947 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 64.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of the Republic of Korea, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Han: 514 km
  • Nakdong: 506 km
  • Namhan: 394 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Halla-san: 1,947 m (its highest point)
  • Chiri-san: 1,915 m
  • Sorak-san: 1,708 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Korean Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): deciduous broadleaf forest 36.7%, wooded savanna 18.9%, farmland 18.1%, mixed forest 11.5%, towns and cities 5.8%, grassland 2.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 48.5%, mountains 43.7%, plains 7.8%.

Forests covered 64.4% of its land in 2024, down from 67.9% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in mica among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #9 in sulfur among the countries USGS lists (2025)
