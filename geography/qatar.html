Qatar: map and facts

Qatar is in Western Asia. It has about 2.97 million people (2025), the 136th largest population. It covers 11,637 km², the 158th largest by area. That is about 255 people per km². Its highest point is Qurayn Abu al Bawl, at 103 m.

Facts

  • Population: 2,972,215 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 11,637 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 11,200 km².
  • Capital: Doha
  • Highest point: Qurayn Abu al Bawl, 103 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Qurayn Abu al Bawl: 103 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 94.8%, towns and cities 3.5%, water 1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 0.1% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #6 in natural gas among the countries EIA lists (2024)
  • World #9 in sulfur among the countries USGS lists (2025)
