India: map and facts

India is in Southern Asia. It has about 1.46 billion people (2025), the largest population. It covers 3,287,263 km², the 7th largest by area. That is about 445 people per km². Its highest point is K2, at 8,611 m. The longest river on its map is the Indus, 3,180 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 1,463,865,525 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 3,287,263 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: New Delhi
  • Highest point: K2, 8,611 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 24.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of India, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Indus: 3,180 km
  • Brahmaputra: 2,900 km
  • Ganges: 2,525 km
  • Sutlej: 1,500 km
  • Krishna: 1,400 km
  • Yamuna: 1,376 km
  • Narmada: 1,315 km
  • Chenab: 974 km
  • Chambal: 960 km

Also on the map: Brahmani, Jhelum, Cauvery, Pennar, Mahi, Godavari, Mahanadi, Ghaghara, Kosi and Tapi.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • K2: 8,611 m (its highest point)
  • Kanchenjunga: 8,586 m
  • Nanga Parbat: 8,126 m
  • Nanda Devi: 7,817 m
  • Batura Sar: 7,795 m
  • Nun: 7,135 m
  • Kangto: 7,090 m
  • Shahi Kangri: 6,934 m

Mountain ranges: Himalayas, Hindu Kush, Karakoram Range, Siwalik Hills, Eastern Ghats, Vindhya Range, Western Ghats, Chin Hills, Khasi Hills, Naga Hills, Satpura Range and Aravalli Range.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Plateau of Tibet, Andaman Islands, Deccan Plateau, Ganges Plain, Nicobar Islands, Punjab, Thar Desert, Coromandel Coast, Ganges Delta, Kathiawar Peninsula, Malabar Coast and Chota Nagpur Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 60.3%, grassland 7.6%, bare ground 5.8%, savanna 5.6%, evergreen broadleaf forest 4.3%, wooded savanna 3.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 40.5%, plateaus 21.9%, hills 19.8%, mountains 10%, high mountains 7.8%.

Forests covered 24.4% of its land in 2024, up from 21.5% in 1990 (World Bank).
