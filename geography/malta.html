Malta: map and facts

Malta is in Southern Europe. It has 579,704 people (2025), the 165th largest population. It covers 315 km², the 185th largest by area. That is about 1,840 people per km². Its highest point is Ta' Dmejrek, at 253 m.

Facts

  • Population: 579,704 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 315 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 326 km².
  • Capital: Valletta
  • Highest point: Ta' Dmejrek, 253 m (Natural Earth)
  • Forest: 1.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Ta' Dmejrek: 253 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 42.3%, towns and cities 36.8%, grassland 14.8%, water 5.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 53.5%, hills 46.5%.

Forests covered 1.5% of its land in 2024, up from 1.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Malta has no land borders on this map.
