Namibia: map and facts

Namibia is in Southern Africa. It has about 3.09 million people (2025), the 133rd largest population. It covers 824,292 km², the 33rd largest by area. That is about 3.8 people per km². Its highest point is Brandberg, at 2,573 m. The longest river on its map is the Zambezi, 2,574 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 3,092,816 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 824,292 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Windhoek
  • Highest point: Brandberg, 2,573 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 9.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Namibia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Zambezi: 2,574 km
  • Orange: 2,200 km
  • Okavango: 1,600 km
  • Cunene: 1,207 km
  • Cuando: 731 km
  • Auob: 400 km

Also on the map: Chobe.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Brandberg: 2,573 m (its highest point)
  • Hunsberge: 1,655 m

Mountain ranges: Groot Karasberge.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Kalahari Desert, Namib Desert, Skeleton Coast, Etosha Pan, Kaokoland and Khomas Highland.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): open shrubland 48.8%, bare ground 23.7%, grassland 23.1%, dense shrubland 4.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 63.9%, hills 29.7%, mountains 4.7%, plains 1.7%.

Forests covered 9.8% of its land in 2024, down from 10.7% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Namibia shares land borders with Angola, Botswana, South Africa and Zambia on this map.
