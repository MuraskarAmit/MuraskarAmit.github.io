Albania: map and facts

Albania is in Southern Europe. It has about 2.35 million people (2025), the 143rd largest population. It covers 28,750 km², the 139th largest by area. That is about 82 people per km². Its highest point is Korab, at 2,764 m. The longest river on its map is the Drin, 280 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 2,349,580 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 28,750 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Tirana
  • Highest point: Korab, 2,764 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 34.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Albania, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Drin: 280 km
  • Vjosa: 280 km
  • Devoll: 196 km
  • Buna: 40 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Korab: 2,764 m (its highest point)
  • Maja e Jezerce: 2,694 m
  • Gjeravica: 2,656 m

Mountain ranges: Pindus Mountains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 44.9%, grassland 25.9%, farmland 8.4%, wooded savanna 7.6%, deciduous broadleaf forest 5.8%, farmland with natural plants 1.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 79%, hills 15.6%, plains 5.4%.

Forests covered 34.3% of its land in 2024, up from 28.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Albania shares land borders with Greece, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia on this map.
