Finland: map and facts

Finland is in Northern Europe. It has about 5.65 million people (2025), the 116th largest population. It covers 338,485 km², the 65th largest by area. That is about 17 people per km². Its highest point is Haltiatunturi, at 1,361 m. The longest river on its map is the Kemijoki, 550 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 5,646,436 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 338,485 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 331,000 km².
  • Capital: Helsinki
  • Highest point: Haltiatunturi, 1,361 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 74.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Finland, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Kemijoki: 550 km
  • Tornelven: 470 km
  • Iijoki: 370 km
  • Ounasjoki: 300 km
  • Lotta: 235 km
  • Kitinen: 235 km
  • Muonio: 230 km
  • Kymijoki: 204 km
  • Tana River: 166 km
  • Siika: 160 km

Also on the map: Kyrönjoki, Vuoksi, Ahtavanjoki, Kokemenjoki, Luirojoki, Patsoyoki, Oulu, Yanisyoki, Kokemaen and Hossanj.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Haltiatunturi: 1,361 m (its highest point)
  • Ukko Koli: 347 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Lapland, Finnish Lake Region and Finnmarksvidda.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 44.1%, evergreen conifer forest 21.3%, mixed forest 13.3%, savanna 9.3%, water 5%, open shrubland 3.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 84.4%, hills 13.2%, plateaus 2.4%.

Forests covered 74.2% of its land in 2024, up from 71.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #3 in mica among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Finland shares land borders with Norway, Russia and Sweden on this map.
