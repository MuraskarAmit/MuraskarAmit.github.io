Portugal: map and facts

Portugal is in Southern Europe. It has about 10.8 million people (2025), the 89th largest population. It covers 92,230 km², the 109th largest by area. That is about 117 people per km². Its highest point is Pico Alto, at 2,351 m. The longest river on its map is the Tagus, 1,007 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 10,804,871 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 92,230 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Lisbon
  • Highest point: Pico Alto, 2,351 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 36.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Portugal, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Tagus: 1,007 km
  • Douro: 897 km
  • Guadiana: 778 km
  • Minho: 315 km
  • Zezere: 248 km
  • Mondego: 234 km
  • Sado: 180 km
  • Sorraia: 155 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pico Alto: 2,351 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Iberian Peninsula and Azores.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 30.3%, grassland 22.9%, wooded savanna 18%, farmland 15.1%, evergreen conifer forest 6.6%, evergreen broadleaf forest 2.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 47%, plains 29.5%, mountains 21.9%, plateaus 1.6%.

Forests covered 36.6% of its land in 2024, down from 37.6% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #10 in lithium among the countries USGS lists (2025)
