Ecuador: map and facts

Ecuador is in South America. It has about 18.3 million people (2025), the 70th largest population. It covers 256,370 km², the 76th largest by area. That is about 71 people per km². Its highest point is Chimborazo, at 6,263 m. The longest river on its map is the Putumayo, 1,610 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 18,289,896 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 256,370 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Quito
  • Highest point: Chimborazo, 6,263 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 49.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Ecuador, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Putumayo: 1,610 km
  • Napo: 1,130 km
  • Mira: 400 km
  • Tumbes: 200 km

Also on the map: Santiago.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Chimborazo: 6,263 m (its highest point)
  • Volcan Wolf: 1,707 m

Mountain ranges: Andes, Cordillera Central and Cordillera Occidental.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Isabela Island, Santa Cruz Island and Galapagos Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 49.4%, savanna 24.1%, wooded savanna 13.6%, grassland 5.1%, farmland with natural plants 1.6%, farmland 1.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 29.7%, mountains 27.4%, hills 18.7%, high mountains 16.8%, plateaus 7.4%.

Forests covered 49.7% of its land in 2024, down from 52.9% in 1990 (World Bank).

Ecuador shares land borders with Colombia and Peru on this map.
