Lithuania: map and facts

Lithuania is in Northern Europe. It has about 2.89 million people (2025), the 137th largest population. It covers 65,290 km², the 121st largest by area. That is about 44 people per km². Its highest point is Aukštojas Hill, at 294 m. The longest river on its map is the Neman, 937 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 2,888,774 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 65,290 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Vilnius
  • Highest point: Aukštojas Hill, 294 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 35.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Lithuania, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Neman: 937 km
  • Neris: 510 km
  • Venta: 346 km
  • Šventoji: 246 km
  • Merkys: 206 km
  • Nemunelis: 191 km
  • Dysna: 178 km
  • Muša: 164 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Aukštojas Hill: 294 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Baltic Plains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 26.6%, mixed forest 21.8%, grassland 19.9%, savanna 15.1%, wooded savanna 7.7%, evergreen conifer forest 6.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 99.8%.

Forests covered 35.4% of its land in 2024, up from 31.6% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Lithuania shares land borders with Belarus, Latvia, Poland and Russia on this map.
