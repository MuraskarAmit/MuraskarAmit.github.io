United States: map and facts

The United States is in Northern America. It has about 342 million people (2025), the 3rd largest population. It covers 9,831,510 km², the 3rd largest by area. That is about 35 people per km². Its highest point is Denali, at 6,190 m. The longest river on its map is the Mississippi, 3,766 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 341,784,857 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 9,831,510 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 9,450,000 km².
  • Capital: Washington, D.C.
  • Highest point: Denali, 6,190 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 33.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Denali: 6,190 m (its highest point)
  • Mount Blackburn: 4,996 m
  • Mount Fairweather: 4,671 m
  • Mount Whitney: 4,421 m
  • Mount Elbert: 4,401 m
  • Mount Rainier: 4,389 m
  • Uncompahgre Peak: 4,361 m
  • Longs Peak: 4,345 m

Mountain ranges: Rocky Mountains, Alaska Range, Appalachian Mountains, Brooks Range, Cascade Range, Coast Mountains, Chugach Mountains, Coast Ranges, Piedmont, Sierra Nevada, Columbia Mountains and Adirondack Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Alaska, Great Plains, Hawaii, Hawaiian Islands, Aleutian Islands, Central Lowland, Atlantic and Gulf Coastal Plain, Great Basin, Alaska Peninsula, Allegheny Plateau, Colorado Plateau and Columbia Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 29.8%, farmland 14.3%, wooded savanna 13.5%, open shrubland 10.6%, deciduous broadleaf forest 8.2%, savanna 7.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 30.8%, plateaus 24.8%, hills 23.5%, mountains 19%, high mountains 1.9%.

Forests covered 33.8% of its land in 2024, up from 33% in 1990 (World Bank).
