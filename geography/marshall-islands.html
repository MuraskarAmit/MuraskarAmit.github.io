Marshall Islands: map and facts

The Marshall Islands is in Micronesia. It has 36,282 people (2025), the 189th largest population. It covers 181 km², the 188th largest by area. That is about 200 people per km².

Facts

  • Population: 36,282 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 181 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 166 km².
  • Capital: Majuro
  • Forest: 53.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Ratak Chain.

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 53.6% of its land in 2024, up from 52.2% in 1995 (World Bank).
