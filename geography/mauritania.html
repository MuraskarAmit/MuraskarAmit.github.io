Mauritania: map and facts

Mauritania is in Western Africa. It has about 5.32 million people (2025), the 123rd largest population. It covers 1,030,700 km², the 28th largest by area. That is about 5.2 people per km². Its highest point is Kediet ej Jill, at 915 m.

Facts

  • Population: 5,315,065 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,030,700 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Nouakchott
  • Highest point: Kediet ej Jill, 915 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

Rivers on the map: Sénégal.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Kdeyyat ej Joul: 915 m
  • Kediet ej Jill: 915 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Sahel, El Djouf, Akchar, Aoukar, Tagant Region and Trarza.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 83.8%, grassland 16%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 49.8%, plateaus 45.8%, hills 4.4%.

Forests covered 1% of its land in 2024, up from 0.9% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Mauritania shares land borders with Algeria, Mali, Morocco and Senegal on this map.
