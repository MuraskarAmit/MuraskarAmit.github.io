Macao: map and facts

Macao is in Eastern Asia. It has 685,900 people (2025). It covers 33 km². That is about 20,785 people per km². Its highest point is Alto Coloane, at 176 m.

Facts

  • Population: 685,900 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 33 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 30 km².
  • Highest point: Alto Coloane, 176 m (Natural Earth)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Alto Coloane: 176 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): towns and cities 64.1%, savanna 17.9%, water 7.7%, wetland 7.7%, grassland 2.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 100%.
