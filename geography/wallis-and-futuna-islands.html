Wallis and Futuna Islands: map and facts

The Wallis and Futuna Islands is in Polynesia. It has 11,370 people (2023). It covers 140 km². That is about 81 people per km². Its highest point is Mont Puke, at 524 m.

Facts

  • Population: 11,370 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 140 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Mata-Utu
  • Highest point: Mont Puke, 524 m (CIA World Factbook (archived))

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mont Puke: 524 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Wallis.

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 54.9%, hills 45.1%.

Neighbours

The Wallis and Futuna Islands has no land borders on this map.
