Nauru: map and facts

Nauru is in Micronesia. It has 12,025 people (2025), the 192nd largest population. It covers 21 km², the 192nd largest by area. That is about 573 people per km². Its highest point is Command Ridge, at 70 m.

Facts

  • Population: 12,025 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 21 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 29 km².
  • Capital: Yaren District
  • Highest point: Command Ridge, 70 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0% of the land (2023, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Command Ridge: 70 m (its highest point)

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.
