Saint Pierre and Miquelon: map and facts

Saint Pierre and Miquelon is in Northern America. It has 5,681 people (2023). It covers 242 km². That is about 23 people per km². Its highest point is Morne de la Grande Montagne, at 240 m.

Facts

  • Population: 5,681 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 242 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Saint-Pierre
  • Highest point: Morne de la Grande Montagne, 240 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Morne de la Grande Montagne: 240 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 61.4%, water 11.2%, wooded savanna 10.2%, wetland 7.5%, evergreen conifer forest 3.6%, grassland 2.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 89.3%, plains 10.7%.

Neighbours

Saint Pierre and Miquelon has no land borders on this map.
