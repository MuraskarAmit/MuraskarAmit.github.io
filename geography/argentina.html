Argentina: map and facts

Argentina is in South America. It has about 45.9 million people (2025), the 35th largest population. It covers 2,780,400 km², the 8th largest by area. That is about 16 people per km². Its highest point is Cerro Aconcagua, at 6,962 m. The longest river on its map is the Parana, 4,880 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 45,851,378 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 2,780,400 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Buenos Aires
  • Highest point: Cerro Aconcagua, 6,962 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 17.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Argentina, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Parana: 4,880 km
  • Uruguay: 1,790 km
  • Desaguadero: 1,515 km
  • Colorado: 1,200 km
  • Salado: 1,150 km
  • Pilcomayo: 1,100 km
  • Bermejo: 1,060 km
  • Chubut: 810 km
  • Dulce: 650 km
  • Salado: 640 km

Also on the map: Deseado, San Juan, Limay, Neuquén, Santa Cruz, Tercero, Chico, Negro, Mendoza and Chico.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Cerro Aconcagua: 6,962 m (its highest point)
  • Nevado Ojos del Salado: 6,893 m
  • Volcan Llullaillaco: 6,723 m
  • Cerro Tupungato: 6,635 m
  • Volcan Domuyo: 4,709 m
  • Monte Tronador: 3,491 m
  • Monte Fitz Roy: 3,406 m
  • Cerro Champaquí: 2,790 m

Mountain ranges: Andes.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Gran Chaco, Pampas, Patagonia, Altiplano, Chaco Austral, Valdes Peninsula and Entre Ríos.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 29.5%, open shrubland 26.1%, savanna 14.5%, farmland 12.6%, bare ground 8%, wooded savanna 3.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 49.4%, hills 17.4%, plateaus 16.1%, mountains 9.9%, high mountains 7.2%.

Forests covered 17.1% of its land in 2024, down from 20.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in lithium among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #10 in silver among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Argentina shares land borders with Bolivia, Brazil, Brazilian Island, Chile, Paraguay and Uruguay on this map.
