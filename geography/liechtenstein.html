Liechtenstein: map and facts

Liechtenstein is in Western Europe. It has 41,024 people (2025), the 187th largest population. It covers 160 km², the 189th largest by area. That is about 256 people per km². Its highest point is Grauspitze, at 2,599 m. The longest river on its map is the Rhein, 1,233 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 41,024 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 160 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 137 km².
  • Capital: Vaduz
  • Highest point: Grauspitze, 2,599 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 36.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Liechtenstein, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Rhein: 1,233 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Grauspitze: 2,599 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): mixed forest 27.4%, grassland 25.8%, savanna 21.5%, wooded savanna 16.9%, towns and cities 5.9%, evergreen conifer forest 2.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 100%.

Forests covered 36.8% of its land in 2024, up from 32.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Liechtenstein shares land borders with Austria and Switzerland on this map.
