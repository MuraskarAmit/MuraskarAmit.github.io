Republic of the Congo: map and facts

The Republic of the Congo is in Middle Africa. It has about 6.48 million people (2025), the 109th largest population. It covers 342,000 km², the 64th largest by area. That is about 19 people per km². Its highest point is Mont Nabeba, at 1,020 m. The longest river on its map is the Congo, 4,700 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 6,484,437 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 342,000 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Brazzaville
  • Highest point: Mont Nabeba, 1,020 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 64% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of the Republic of the Congo, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Congo: 4,700 km
  • Ogooué: 1,200 km
  • Ubangi: 1,060 km
  • Sangha: 785 km
  • Dja: 720 km
  • Alima: 500 km

Also on the map: Aina, Niari, Ngounie and Kouilou.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mont Nabeba: 1,020 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Crystal Mountains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 63.9%, savanna 17.3%, grassland 10.1%, wooded savanna 7.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 64.3%, hills 29.2%, plains 6.2%.

Forests covered 64% of its land in 2024, down from 65.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The Republic of the Congo shares land borders with Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Gabon on this map.
