Czechia: map and facts

Czechia is in Eastern Europe. It has about 10.9 million people (2025), the 88th largest population. It covers 78,871 km², the 114th largest by area. That is about 138 people per km². Its highest point is Snežka, at 1,603 m. The longest river on its map is the Elbe, 1,094 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 10,886,878 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 78,871 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Prague
  • Highest point: Snežka, 1,603 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 38.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Czechia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Elbe: 1,094 km
  • Oder: 854 km
  • Vltava: 430 km
  • Morava: 388 km
  • Luzicka Nisa: 254 km
  • Eger: 247 km
  • Dyje: 235 km
  • Sazava: 226 km
  • Svratka: 174 km
  • Uhlava: 104 km

Also on the map: Svitava.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Snežka: 1,603 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Böhmerwald, Erzgebirge and Sudetes Mountains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 43.1%, mixed forest 16.9%, savanna 8.3%, evergreen conifer forest 8.2%, deciduous broadleaf forest 6.5%, wooded savanna 5.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 70.5%, plains 12.9%, mountains 8.6%, plateaus 8%.

Forests covered 38.4% of its land in 2024, up from 36.9% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Czechia shares land borders with Austria, Germany, Poland and Slovakia on this map.
