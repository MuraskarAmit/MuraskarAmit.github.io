Rwanda: map and facts

Rwanda is in Eastern Africa. It has about 14.6 million people (2025), the 76th largest population. It covers 26,340 km², the 144th largest by area. That is about 553 people per km². Its highest point is Volcan Karisimbi, at 4,507 m. The longest river on its map is the Kagera, 400 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 14,569,341 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 26,340 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 25,400 km².
  • Capital: Kigali
  • Highest point: Volcan Karisimbi, 4,507 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 26.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Rwanda, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Kagera: 400 km
  • Rusizi: 117 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Volcan Karisimbi: 4,507 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 24.4%, farmland 24%, farmland with natural plants 22.3%, savanna 15%, evergreen broadleaf forest 5.5%, water 4.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 58.5%, mountains 31.2%, plateaus 7.9%, high mountains 2.4%.

Forests covered 26.1% of its land in 2024, up from 17.7% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in niobium and tantalum among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #7 in tungsten among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Rwanda shares land borders with Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Tanzania and Uganda on this map.
