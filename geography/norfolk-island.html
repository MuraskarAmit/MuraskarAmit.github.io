Norfolk Island: map and facts

Norfolk Island is in Oceania. It has 2,188 people (2021). It covers 36 km². That is about 61 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Bates, at 319 m.

Facts

  • Population: 2,188 (2021, Australian Bureau of Statistics)
  • Area: 36 km² (UN Demographic Yearbook). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 41 km².
  • Capital: Kingston
  • Highest point: Mount Bates, 319 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Bates: 319 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 32.7%, wetland 27.3%, evergreen broadleaf forest 21.8%, water 14.6%, grassland 3.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 100%.

Neighbours

Norfolk Island has no land borders on this map.
