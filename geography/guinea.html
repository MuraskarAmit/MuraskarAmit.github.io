Guinea: map and facts

Guinea is in Western Africa. It has about 15.1 million people (2025), the 74th largest population. It covers 245,857 km², the 77th largest by area. That is about 61 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Richard-Molard, at 1,752 m. The longest river on its map is the Niger, 4,180 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 15,099,727 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 245,857 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Conakry
  • Highest point: Mount Richard-Molard, 1,752 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 20% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Guinea, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Niger: 4,180 km
  • Gambia: 1,130 km
  • Bafing: 500 km

Also on the map: Geba and St. Paul.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Richard-Molard: 1,752 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Guinea Highlands and Loma Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Fouta Djallon.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 73.9%, grassland 11.7%, wooded savanna 6.2%, farmland 3%, farmland with natural plants 2.7%, wetland 1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 44.2%, plateaus 37.7%, plains 9.1%, mountains 9%.

Forests covered 20% of its land in 2024, down from 29.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #1 in bauxite among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Guinea shares land borders with Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea-Bissau, Liberia, Mali, Senegal and Sierra Leone on this map.
