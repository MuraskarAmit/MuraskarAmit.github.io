Saint Barthelemy: map and facts

Saint Barthelemy is in the Caribbean. It has 11,085 people (2023). It covers 24 km². That is about 462 people per km². Its highest point is Morne du Vitet, at 286 m.

Facts

  • Population: 11,085 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 24 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Gustavia
  • Highest point: Morne du Vitet, 286 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes
