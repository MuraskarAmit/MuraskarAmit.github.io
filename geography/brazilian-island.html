Brazilian Island: map and facts

Brazilian Island is in South America. It has no permanent population. It covers 3 km².

Facts

  • Population: none permanent
  • Area: 3 km² (area as drawn on this map)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wetland 100%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Neighbours

Brazilian Island shares land borders with Argentina, Brazil and Uruguay on this map.
