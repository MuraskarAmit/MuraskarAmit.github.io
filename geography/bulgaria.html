Bulgaria: map and facts

Bulgaria is in Eastern Europe. It has about 6.43 million people (2025), the 110th largest population. It covers 111,000 km², the 103rd largest by area. That is about 58 people per km². Its highest point is Musala, at 2,925 m. The longest river on its map is the Danube, 2,850 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 6,433,302 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 111,000 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Sofia
  • Highest point: Musala, 2,925 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 36.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Bulgaria, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Danube: 2,850 km
  • Evros: 490 km
  • Struma: 415 km
  • Levi Iskar: 368 km
  • Arda: 290 km
  • Yantra: 286 km
  • Tundzha: 283 km
  • Kamchiya: 244 km
  • Mesta: 230 km
  • Nišava: 218 km

Also on the map: Ogosta.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Musala: 2,925 m (its highest point)
  • Botev: 2,376 m
  • Midzor: 2,169 m

Mountain ranges: Balkan Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Balkan Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 35.9%, deciduous broadleaf forest 19.8%, grassland 16.6%, wooded savanna 11.1%, savanna 7.2%, evergreen conifer forest 4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 43.8%, mountains 31.7%, plains 23.6%.

Forests covered 36.4% of its land in 2024, up from 30.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #9 in silica sand among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Bulgaria shares land borders with Greece, North Macedonia, Romania, Serbia and Turkey on this map.
