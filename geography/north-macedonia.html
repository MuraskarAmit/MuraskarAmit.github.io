North Macedonia: map and facts

North Macedonia is in Southern Europe. It has about 1.82 million people (2025), the 148th largest population. It covers 25,713 km², the 145th largest by area. That is about 71 people per km². Its highest point is Korab, at 2,764 m. The longest river on its map is the Drin, 280 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 1,820,909 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 25,713 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Skopje
  • Highest point: Korab, 2,764 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 40.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of North Macedonia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Drin: 280 km
  • Bregalnica: 225 km
  • Crna: 207 km
  • Treska: 132 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Korab: 2,764 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 37.2%, deciduous broadleaf forest 18.8%, wooded savanna 16.2%, savanna 12.8%, farmland 9.9%, water 1.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 78.5%, hills 19.8%, plateaus 1.6%.

Forests covered 40.8% of its land in 2024, up from 36.8% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

North Macedonia shares land borders with Albania, Bulgaria, Greece and Serbia on this map.
