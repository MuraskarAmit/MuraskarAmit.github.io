Bhutan: map and facts

Bhutan is in Southern Asia. It has 796,682 people (2025), the 161st largest population. It covers 38,390 km², the 133rd largest by area. That is about 21 people per km². Its highest point is Gangkar Punsum, at 7,570 m.

Facts

  • Population: 796,682 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 38,390 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 40,500 km².
  • Capital: Thimphu
  • Highest point: Gangkar Punsum, 7,570 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 70.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

Rivers on the map: Manas.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Gangkar Punsum: 7,570 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Himalayas.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 24.8%, mixed forest 24%, evergreen conifer forest 21.7%, grassland 16.1%, bare ground 5.6%, wooded savanna 3.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): high mountains 56.7%, mountains 43%.

Forests covered 70.4% of its land in 2024, up from 53.7% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Bhutan shares land borders with China and India on this map.
