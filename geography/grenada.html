Grenada: map and facts

Grenada is in the Caribbean. It has 117,303 people (2025), the 179th largest population. It covers 344 km², the 184th largest by area. That is about 341 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Saint Catherine, at 840 m.

Facts

  • Population: 117,303 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 344 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: St. George's
  • Highest point: Mount Saint Catherine, 840 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 51.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Saint Catherine: 840 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 70.8%, evergreen broadleaf forest 9.3%, grassland 7.2%, wetland 5.3%, water 4.5%, towns and cities 1.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 80.6%, hills 16.8%, plains 2.6%.

Forests covered 51.5% of its land in 2024, down from 52.1% in 1990 (World Bank).

Grenada has no land borders on this map.
