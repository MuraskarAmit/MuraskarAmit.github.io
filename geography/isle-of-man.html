Isle of Man: map and facts

The Isle of Man is in Northern Europe. It has 84,118 people (2025). It covers 572 km². That is about 147 people per km². Its highest point is Snaefell, at 621 m.

Facts

  • Population: 84,118 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 572 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Douglas
  • Highest point: Snaefell, 621 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 6.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Snaefell: 621 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 54%, grassland 19.6%, farmland with natural plants 7.4%, wooded savanna 4.7%, deciduous broadleaf forest 3.1%, mixed forest 2.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 74.3%, mountains 16.3%, plains 9.4%.

Forests covered 6.4% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

The Isle of Man has no land borders on this map.
