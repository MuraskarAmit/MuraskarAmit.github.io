Romania: map and facts

Romania is in Eastern Europe. It has about 19 million people (2025), the 67th largest population. It covers 238,397 km², the 81st largest by area. That is about 80 people per km². Its highest point is Moldoveanu, at 2,544 m. The longest river on its map is the Danube, 2,850 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 19,020,271 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 238,397 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Bucharest
  • Highest point: Moldoveanu, 2,544 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 30.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Romania, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Danube: 2,850 km
  • Prut: 953 km
  • Siret: 740 km
  • Olt: 615 km
  • Timis: 359 km
  • Arges: 350 km
  • Jiu: 331 km
  • Buzau: 325 km
  • Bistrita: 272 km
  • Crisul Alb: 236 km

Also on the map: Vedea, Moldova, Trotus, Somesul Cald, Chilia branch, Borcea, Sfantu Gheorghe branch, Sulina branch, Mures and Ialomita.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Moldoveanu: 2,544 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Carpathian Mountains and Transylvanian Alps.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Danube Delta and Great Hungarian Plain.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 42.4%, deciduous broadleaf forest 21.8%, savanna 10.5%, grassland 7.2%, wooded savanna 6.6%, mixed forest 5.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 36.9%, plains 36.5%, mountains 25.5%, plateaus 1.1%.

Forests covered 30.2% of its land in 2024, up from 27.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Romania shares land borders with Bulgaria, Hungary, Moldova, Serbia and Ukraine on this map.
