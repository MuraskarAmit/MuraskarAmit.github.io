Greece: map and facts

Greece is in Southern Europe. It has about 10.4 million people (2025), the 93rd largest population. It covers 131,960 km², the 95th largest by area. That is about 79 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Olympus, at 2,918 m. The longest river on its map is the Evros, 490 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 10,413,962 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 131,960 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Athens
  • Highest point: Mount Olympus, 2,918 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 36.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Greece, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Evros: 490 km
  • Struma: 415 km
  • Haliacmon: 297 km
  • Arda: 290 km
  • Aoos: 280 km
  • Mesta: 230 km
  • Acheloos: 217 km
  • Enipefs: 216 km
  • Treska: 132 km
  • Loudias: 50 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Olympus: 2,918 m (its highest point)
  • Psiloritis: 2,456 m

Mountain ranges: Pindus Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Balkan Peninsula, Cyclades and Ionian Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 24%, farmland 20.1%, grassland 19.5%, wooded savanna 14.6%, evergreen conifer forest 8.8%, deciduous broadleaf forest 4.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 69.4%, hills 24.3%, plains 6.2%.

Forests covered 36.9% of its land in 2024, up from 28.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Greece shares land borders with Albania, Bulgaria, North Macedonia and Turkey on this map.
