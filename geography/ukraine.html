Ukraine: map and facts

Ukraine is in Eastern Europe. It has about 39 million people (2025), the 40th largest population. It covers 603,550 km², the 45th largest by area. That is about 65 people per km². Its highest point is Hoverla, at 2,061 m. The longest river on its map is the Danube, 2,850 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 38,980,376 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 603,550 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Kyiv
  • Highest point: Hoverla, 2,061 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 17.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Ukraine, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Danube: 2,850 km
  • Dnieper: 2,285 km
  • Dniester: 1,352 km
  • Desna: 1,130 km
  • Donets: 1,053 km
  • Prut: 953 km
  • Southern Bug: 806 km
  • Pripyat: 775 km
  • Bug: 772 km
  • Siret: 740 km

Also on the map: Seym, Horyn, Sozh, Styr, San, Teteriv, Snov, Synyukha, Chilia branch and Western Bug.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Hoverla: 2,061 m (its highest point)
  • Roman-Kosh: 1,545 m

Mountain ranges: Carpathian Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Pontic Steppe, Central Russian Upland, Crimea, Azov Upland and Dnieper Upland.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 64.7%, grassland 7.2%, deciduous broadleaf forest 6.3%, savanna 6%, mixed forest 5.4%, wooded savanna 3.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 87%, hills 5.6%, plateaus 4.3%, mountains 3.1%.

Forests covered 17.3% of its land in 2024, up from 16.2% in 1995 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #9 in iron ore among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #10 in titanium among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Ukraine shares land borders with Belarus, Hungary, Moldova, Poland, Romania, Russia and Slovakia on this map.
