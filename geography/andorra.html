Andorra: map and facts

Andorra is in Southern Europe. It has 82,904 people (2025), the 183rd largest population. It covers 468 km², the 178th largest by area. That is about 177 people per km². Its highest point is Pic de Coma Pedrosa, at 2,942 m.

Facts

  • Population: 82,904 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 468 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 452 km².
  • Capital: Andorra la Vella
  • Highest point: Pic de Coma Pedrosa, 2,942 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 38.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pic de Coma Pedrosa: 2,942 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Pyrenees.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen conifer forest 30.2%, grassland 29.2%, wooded savanna 18.9%, savanna 15%, mixed forest 3.4%, towns and cities 2.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 88.6%, high mountains 11.4%.

Forests covered 38.9% of its land in 2024, up from 37.6% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Andorra shares land borders with France and Spain on this map.
