Venezuela: map and facts

Venezuela is in South America. It has about 28.5 million people (2025), the 53rd largest population. It covers 912,050 km², the 32nd largest by area. That is about 31 people per km². Its highest point is Pico Bolívar, at 4,978 m. The longest river on its map is the Negro, 2,250 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 28,516,896 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 912,050 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Caracas
  • Highest point: Pico Bolívar, 4,978 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 53.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Venezuela, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Negro: 2,250 km
  • Orinoco: 2,140 km
  • Apure: 1,580 km
  • Meta: 1,000 km
  • Caroní: 765 km
  • Cuyuni: 618 km
  • Guarico: 525 km
  • Ventuari: 520 km
  • Casiquiare: 326 km

Also on the map: Cojedes.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pico Bolívar: 4,978 m (its highest point)
  • Pico da Neblina: 2,994 m
  • Cerro Marahuaca: 2,832 m
  • Mount Roraima: 2,810 m
  • Pico Naiguata: 2,765 m
  • Cero Raya: 2,070 m
  • Cerro El Copey: 957 m

Mountain ranges: Cordillera Oriental, Guiana Highlands, Cordillera de Mérida, Pakaraima Mountains and Serra de Curupira.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Llanos, Guiana Shield and Lesser Antilles.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 46.2%, savanna 20.9%, grassland 19%, wooded savanna 9%, deciduous broadleaf forest 1.5%, water 1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 52.2%, hills 22.9%, mountains 20.4%, plateaus 3.8%.

Forests covered 53.4% of its land in 2024, down from 57.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Venezuela shares land borders with Brazil, Colombia and Guyana on this map.
