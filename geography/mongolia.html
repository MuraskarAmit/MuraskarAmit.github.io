Mongolia: map and facts

Mongolia is in Eastern Asia. It has about 3.57 million people (2025), the 130th largest population. It covers 1,564,116 km², the 18th largest by area. That is about 2.3 people per km². Its highest point is Huiten Peak, at 4,374 m. The longest river on its map is the Onon, 1,032 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 3,568,978 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,564,116 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Ulaanbaatar
  • Highest point: Huiten Peak, 4,374 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 9.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Mongolia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Onon: 1,032 km
  • Selenga: 1,024 km

Also on the map: Ider, Shishhid Gol, Orkhon and Kherlen.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Huiten Peak: 4,374 m (its highest point)
  • Munkh Khairkhan: 4,231 m
  • Otgon Tenger: 4,008 m
  • Tergun Bogd: 3,957 m
  • Gora Munku-Sardyk: 3,491 m
  • Dund Saikhan: 2,825 m

Mountain ranges: Altai Mountains, Khangai Mountains, Tannu-Ola Range and Khentii Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Gobi Desert and Mongolian Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 64.2%, bare ground 30.1%, wooded savanna 1.9%, savanna 1.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 42.1%, plateaus 34.6%, mountains 17.8%, high mountains 5.5%.

Forests covered 9.1% of its land in 2024, down from 9.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #2 in fluorspar among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #8 in molybdenum among the countries USGS lists (2025)
