Samoa: map and facts

Samoa is in Polynesia. It has 219,306 people (2025), the 175th largest population. It covers 2,840 km², the 166th largest by area. That is about 77 people per km². Its highest point is Mauga Silisili, at 1,858 m.

Facts

  • Population: 219,306 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 2,840 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Apia
  • Highest point: Mauga Silisili, 1,858 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 57.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mauga Silisili: 1,858 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Savai'i and Samoa Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 93.1%, water 2.9%, grassland 1.9%, wetland 1.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 76%, hills 22.9%, plains 1.1%.

Forests covered 57.6% of its land in 2024, down from 62.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Samoa has no land borders on this map.
