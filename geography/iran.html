Iran: map and facts

Iran is in Southern Asia. It has about 92.4 million people (2025), the 17th largest population. It covers 1,745,150 km², the 17th largest by area. That is about 53 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Damavand, at 5,628 m. The longest river on its map is the Helmand, 1,150 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 92,417,681 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,745,150 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 1,620,000 km².
  • Capital: Tehran
  • Highest point: Mount Damavand, 5,628 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 6.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Iran, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Helmand: 1,150 km
  • Aras: 1,072 km
  • Karkheh: 870 km
  • Sefid: 670 km
  • Atrek: 669 km
  • Zarriné-Rud: 302 km
  • Qotur/Ghotour Chai: 170 km

Also on the map: Shatt al Arab, Harirud, Talkeh and Qezel Owzan.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Damavand: 5,628 m (its highest point)
  • Sabalon Kuh: 4,814 m
  • Kuh-e Hezar: 4,500 m
  • Kuh-e Dinar: 4,409 m
  • Zard Kuh: 4,221 m
  • Kuh-e Taftan: 3,941 m
  • Kuh-e Garin: 3,630 m
  • Cheekha Dar: 3,611 m

Mountain ranges: Zagros Mountains, Elburz Mountains, Kuh Rud Mountains, Central Makran Range and Kopet Dag Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Baluchistan, Lut Desert and Kavir Desert.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 59.4%, grassland 20.9%, open shrubland 13.3%, farmland 4.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 36.5%, mountains 36.1%, plateaus 18.3%, plains 5.6%, high mountains 3.5%.

Forests covered 6.6% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).
