Sao Tome and Principe: map and facts

Sao Tome and Principe is in Middle Africa. It has 240,254 people (2025), the 174th largest population. It covers 960 km², the 170th largest by area. That is about 250 people per km². Its highest point is Pico de São Tomé, at 2,024 m.

Facts

  • Population: 240,254 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 960 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 1,040 km².
  • Capital: São Tomé
  • Highest point: Pico de São Tomé, 2,024 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 57.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pico de São Tomé: 2,024 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 78.3%, wooded savanna 12.6%, water 5.9%, wetland 2.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 71.8%, hills 28.1%.

Forests covered 57.6% of its land in 2024, down from 61.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Sao Tome and Principe has no land borders on this map.
