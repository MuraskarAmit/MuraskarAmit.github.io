Sierra Leone: map and facts

Sierra Leone is in Western Africa. It has about 8.82 million people (2025), the 100th largest population. It covers 72,300 km², the 116th largest by area. That is about 122 people per km². Its highest point is Loma Mansa, at 1,945 m. The longest river on its map is the Rokel, 386 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 8,819,794 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 72,300 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Freetown
  • Highest point: Loma Mansa, 1,945 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 34% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

  • Rokel: 386 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Loma Mansa: 1,945 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Loma Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Grain Coast.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 51.6%, savanna 33.3%, farmland with natural plants 7%, evergreen broadleaf forest 3.6%, wetland 2.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 52.7%, hills 32.9%, plateaus 11.1%, mountains 3.3%.

Forests covered 34% of its land in 2024, down from 43.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Sierra Leone shares land borders with Guinea and Liberia on this map.
