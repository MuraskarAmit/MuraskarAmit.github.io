Mauritius: map and facts

Mauritius is in Eastern Africa. It has about 1.24 million people (2025), the 155th largest population. It covers 2,010 km², the 168th largest by area. That is about 619 people per km². Its highest point is Petite Riviere Noire Peak, at 828 m.

Facts

  • Population: 1,243,741 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 2,010 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Port Louis
  • Highest point: Petite Riviere Noire Peak, 828 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 19.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Petite Riviere Noire Peak: 828 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Mascarene Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 31.6%, evergreen broadleaf forest 20.5%, savanna 19.2%, farmland with natural plants 11.9%, grassland 6.9%, water 4.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 66%, mountains 24%, plains 10%.

Forests covered 19.1% of its land in 2024, down from 20.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Mauritius has no land borders on this map.
