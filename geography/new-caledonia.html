New Caledonia: map and facts

New Caledonia is in Melanesia. It has 295,333 people (2025). It covers 18,576 km². That is about 16 people per km². Its highest point is Mont Panié, at 1,629 m.

Facts

  • Population: 295,333 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 18,576 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Nouméa
  • Highest point: Mont Panié, 1,629 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 48.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mont Panié: 1,629 m (its highest point)
  • Mont Humboldt: 1,618 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Nouvelle-Calédonie and Iles Loyauté.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 53.7%, wooded savanna 21.5%, savanna 16.3%, water 2.8%, wetland 2.6%, grassland 1.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 64.1%, hills 21.5%, plains 14.4%.

Forests covered 48.1% of its land in 2024, up from 45.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #4 in nickel among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

New Caledonia has no land borders on this map.
