Cambodia: map and facts

Cambodia is in South-Eastern Asia. It has about 17.8 million people (2025), the 72nd largest population. It covers 181,035 km², the 88th largest by area. That is about 99 people per km². Its highest point is Phnom Aural, at 1,813 m. The longest river on its map is the Mekong, 4,350 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 17,847,982 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 181,035 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Phnom Penh
  • Highest point: Phnom Aural, 1,813 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 37.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Cambodia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Mekong: 4,350 km
  • Tonle Sap: 112 km

Also on the map: Kong, Krong, Srepok and Sreng.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Phnom Aural: 1,813 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Central Highlands and Dangrek Range.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 26.4%, savanna 22.1%, evergreen broadleaf forest 18.6%, grassland 17.2%, wooded savanna 7%, deciduous broadleaf forest 4.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 71.6%, hills 19.7%, mountains 8.3%.

Forests covered 37.6% of its land in 2024, down from 62.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Cambodia shares land borders with Laos, Thailand and Vietnam on this map.
