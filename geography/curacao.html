Curacao: map and facts

Curacao is in the Caribbean. It has 156,263 people (2025). It covers 444 km². That is about 352 people per km².

Facts

  • Population: 156,263 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 444 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 466 km².
  • Capital: Willemstad
  • Forest: 0.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 54.6%, towns and cities 33.8%, water 10%, savanna 1.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 80.5%, hills 19.5%.

Forests covered 0.2% of its land in 2024, the same as in 2015 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Curacao has no land borders on this map.
