Pitcairn Islands: map and facts

The Pitcairn Islands is in Polynesia. It covers 43 km².

Facts

  • Area: 43 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Adamstown

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wetland 88.9%, water 11.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Neighbours

The Pitcairn Islands has no land borders on this map.
