Micronesia: map and facts

Micronesia is in Micronesia. It has 113,683 people (2025), the 180th largest population. It covers 701 km², the 176th largest by area. That is about 162 people per km². Its highest point is Ngihneni, at 791 m.

Facts

  • Population: 113,683 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 701 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 636 km².
  • Capital: Palikir
  • Highest point: Ngihneni, 791 m (Natural Earth)
  • Forest: 92.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Ngihneni: 791 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Truk and Yap Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 61%, water 21.2%, wetland 15.7%, wooded savanna 2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 39.2%, hills 34.8%, plains 26%.

Forests covered 92.1% of its land in 2024, up from 91% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Micronesia has no land borders on this map.
