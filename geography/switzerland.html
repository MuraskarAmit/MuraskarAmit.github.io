Switzerland: map and facts

Switzerland is in Western Europe. It has about 9.09 million people (2025), the 98th largest population. It covers 41,291 km², the 132nd largest by area. That is about 220 people per km². Its highest point is Monte Rosa, at 4,634 m. The longest river on its map is the Rhin, 1,233 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 9,092,436 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 41,291 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Bern
  • Highest point: Monte Rosa, 4,634 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 32% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Switzerland, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Rhin: 1,233 km
  • Rhone: 812 km
  • Inn: 517 km
  • Doubs: 453 km
  • Aare: 295 km
  • Ticino: 248 km
  • Reuss: 159 km
  • Limmat: 140 km
  • Thielle: 18 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Monte Rosa: 4,634 m (its highest point)
  • Matterhorn: 4,478 m
  • Finsteraarhorn: 4,274 m
  • Grauspitze: 2,599 m

Mountain ranges: Alps and Jura Mountains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 29.6%, mixed forest 18.5%, savanna 16.1%, wooded savanna 12.9%, farmland 5.5%, bare ground 4.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 63.7%, hills 25.2%, high mountains 10.1%, plateaus 1%.

Forests covered 32% of its land in 2024, up from 29.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Switzerland shares land borders with Austria, France, Germany, Italy and Liechtenstein on this map.
