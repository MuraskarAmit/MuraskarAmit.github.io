Denmark: map and facts

Denmark is in Northern Europe. It has about 6.01 million people (2025), the 113th largest population. It covers 42,920 km², the 130th largest by area. That is about 140 people per km². Its highest point is Møllehøj, at 171 m.

Facts

  • Population: 6,009,169 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 42,920 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Copenhagen
  • Highest point: Møllehøj, 171 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 16% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Møllehøj: 171 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: North European Plain and Jutland.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 58.4%, savanna 14.2%, farmland with natural plants 8%, mixed forest 6.5%, grassland 5.1%, towns and cities 2.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 99.8%.

Forests covered 16% of its land in 2024, up from 13.3% in 1990 (World Bank).
