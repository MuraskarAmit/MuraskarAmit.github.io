Chile: map and facts

Chile is in South America. It has about 19.9 million people (2025), the 65th largest population. It covers 756,556 km², the 37th largest by area. That is about 26 people per km². Its highest point is Nevado Ojos del Salado, at 6,893 m. The longest river on its map is the Gallegos, 300 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 19,859,921 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 756,556 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 736,000 km².
  • Capital: Santiago
  • Highest point: Nevado Ojos del Salado, 6,893 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 24.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Chile, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Gallegos: 300 km
  • Copiapó: 292 km

Also on the map: Bío-Bío and Rio Rapel.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Nevado Ojos del Salado: 6,893 m (its highest point)
  • Volcan Llullaillaco: 6,723 m
  • Cerro Tupungato: 6,635 m
  • Aucanquilcha: 6,176 m
  • Cerro San Valentín: 4,058 m
  • Monte Tronador: 3,491 m
  • Torres del Paine: 2,884 m
  • Volcaan Villarrica: 2,850 m

Mountain ranges: Andes.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Patagonia, Altiplano, Atacama Desert, Valle Longitudinal, Península de Taitao, Desventuradas Islands and Juan Fernandez Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 36.9%, grassland 12.4%, evergreen broadleaf forest 9.4%, savanna 9.1%, mixed forest 7%, evergreen conifer forest 6.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 51.8%, hills 21.1%, high mountains 16.5%, plains 6.2%, plateaus 4.4%.

Forests covered 24.5% of its land in 2024, up from 22.6% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #2 in molybdenum among the countries USGS lists (2025)
