Monaco: map and facts

Monaco is in Western Europe. It has 38,341 people (2025), the 188th largest population. Its highest point is Mont Agel, at 162 m.

Facts

  • Population: 38,341 (2025, World Bank)
  • Capital: Monaco
  • Highest point: Mont Agel, 162 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0% of the land (2023, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

Land
