Saint Kitts and Nevis: map and facts

Saint Kitts and Nevis is in the Caribbean. It has 46,922 people (2025), the 186th largest population. It covers 261 km², the 187th largest by area. That is about 180 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Misery, at 1,156 m.

Facts

  • Population: 46,922 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 261 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Basseterre
  • Highest point: Mount Misery, 1,156 m (Natural Earth)
  • Forest: 42.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Misery: 1,156 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 28.2%, evergreen broadleaf forest 27%, savanna 18.1%, wooded savanna 9.5%, water 8%, wetland 7.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 87.7%, hills 7.1%, plains 5.2%.

Forests covered 42.1% of its land in 2024, down from 42.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Saint Kitts and Nevis has no land borders on this map.
