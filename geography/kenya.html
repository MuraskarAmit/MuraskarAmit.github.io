Kenya: map and facts

Kenya is in Eastern Africa. It has about 57.5 million people (2025), the 26th largest population. It covers 591,970 km², the 46th largest by area. That is about 97 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Kenya, at 5,199 m. The longest river on its map is the Tana, 800 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 57,532,493 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 591,970 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Nairobi
  • Highest point: Mount Kenya, 5,199 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 6.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Kenya, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Tana: 800 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Kenya: 5,199 m (its highest point)
  • Mount Elgon: 4,321 m
  • Lesatima: 4,001 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Great Rift Valley, Chalbi Desert and Serengeti Plains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 73.7%, savanna 6.6%, open shrubland 6.5%, farmland with natural plants 3.3%, bare ground 2.3%, farmland 2.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 38.1%, hills 28.3%, plains 22.5%, mountains 10%, high mountains 1.1%.

Forests covered 6.6% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Kenya shares land borders with Ethiopia, Somalia, South Sudan, Tanzania and Uganda on this map.
