Dominican Republic: map and facts

The Dominican Republic is in the Caribbean. It has about 11.5 million people (2025), the 84th largest population. It covers 48,590 km², the 128th largest by area. That is about 237 people per km². Its highest point is Pico Duarte, at 3,101 m. The longest river on its map is the Yaque del Norte, 296 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 11,520,487 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 48,590 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Santo Domingo
  • Highest point: Pico Duarte, 3,101 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 46.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Its highest peaks:

  • Pico Duarte: 3,101 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: West Indies and Greater Antilles.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 35%, savanna 28.6%, evergreen broadleaf forest 19.1%, grassland 5.4%, farmland 5.1%, farmland with natural plants 2.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 40.6%, hills 35.9%, plains 22.9%.

Forests covered 46.7% of its land in 2024, up from 44.1% in 1990 (World Bank).
