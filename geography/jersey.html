Jersey: map and facts

Jersey is in Northern Europe. It has 103,674 people (2023). It covers 119 km². That is about 871 people per km².

Facts

  • Population: 103,674 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Area: 119 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Saint Helier

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland with natural plants 50%, savanna 21.4%, water 7.6%, wetland 6.2%, farmland 4.3%, evergreen conifer forest 3.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Neighbours

Jersey has no land borders on this map.
