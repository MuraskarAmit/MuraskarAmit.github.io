North Korea: map and facts

North Korea is in Eastern Asia. It has about 26.6 million people (2025), the 56th largest population. It covers 120,540 km², the 98th largest by area. That is about 220 people per km². Its highest point is Paektu-san, at 2,744 m. The longest river on its map is the Yalu, 813 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 26,571,036 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 120,540 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Pyongyang
  • Highest point: Paektu-san, 2,744 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 64.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of North Korea, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Yalu: 813 km
  • Tumen: 549 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Paektu-san: 2,744 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Hamgyong Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Korean Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): deciduous broadleaf forest 43.7%, farmland 24.7%, wooded savanna 10.6%, mixed forest 9%, grassland 5.9%, savanna 2.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 64.5%, hills 30.1%, plains 5.4%.

Forests covered 64.1% of its land in 2024, up from 61.9% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #4 in tungsten among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #8 in graphite among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

North Korea shares land borders with China, Republic of Korea and Russia on this map.
