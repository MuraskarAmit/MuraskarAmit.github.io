Faroe Islands: map and facts

The Faroe Islands is in Northern Europe. It has 54,900 people (2025). It covers 1,393 km². That is about 39 people per km². Its highest point is Slaettaratindur, at 880 m.

Facts

  • Population: 54,900 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,393 km² (UN Demographic Yearbook). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 1,320 km².
  • Capital: Tórshavn
  • Highest point: Slaettaratindur, 880 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Slaettaratindur: 880 m (its highest point)

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 67.7%, hills 32.3%.

Forests covered 0.1% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The Faroe Islands has no land borders on this map.
