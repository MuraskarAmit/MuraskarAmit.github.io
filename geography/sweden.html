Sweden: map and facts

Sweden is in Northern Europe. It has about 10.6 million people (2025), the 92nd largest population. It covers 528,860 km², the 49th largest by area. That is about 20 people per km². Its highest point is Kebnekaise, at 2,097 m. The longest river on its map is the Tornelven, 470 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 10,596,620 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 528,860 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 443,000 km².
  • Capital: Stockholm
  • Highest point: Kebnekaise, 2,097 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 68.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Kebnekaise: 2,097 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Kjølen Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Scandinavia and Lapland.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 35.2%, evergreen conifer forest 32.1%, mixed forest 7.7%, savanna 6.2%, open shrubland 5.8%, grassland 4.8%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 50.8%, plains 36.5%, mountains 8%, plateaus 4.7%.

Forests covered 68.6% of its land in 2024, down from 68.9% in 1990 (World Bank).
