Senegal: map and facts

Senegal is in Western Africa. It has about 18.9 million people (2025), the 68th largest population. It covers 196,710 km², the 86th largest by area. That is about 96 people per km². The longest river on its map is the Gambia, 1,130 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 18,931,966 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 196,710 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Dakar
  • Forest: 45% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

  • Gambia: 1,130 km

Also on the map: Senegal.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 81.9%, farmland 12.1%, savanna 3.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 98.2%, hills 1.8%.

Forests covered 45% of its land in 2024, down from 47.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #6 in titanium among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #6 in zircon among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Senegal shares land borders with Gambia, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Mali and Mauritania on this map.
