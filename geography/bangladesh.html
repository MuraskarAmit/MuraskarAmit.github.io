Bangladesh: map and facts

Bangladesh is in Southern Asia. It has about 176 million people (2025), the 8th largest population. It covers 147,570 km², the 92nd largest by area. That is about 1,191 people per km². Its highest point is Mowdok Mual, at 1,054 m. The longest river on its map is the Brahmaputra, 2,900 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 175,686,899 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 147,570 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 137,000 km².
  • Capital: Dhaka
  • Highest point: Mowdok Mual, 1,054 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 14.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Bangladesh, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Brahmaputra: 2,900 km
  • Ganges: 2,525 km
  • Teesta: 414 km

Also on the map: Barak.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mowdok Mual: 1,054 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Ganges Plain, Ganges Delta and Sundarbans.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 50.8%, farmland with natural plants 14.4%, wetland 10%, savanna 7.7%, wooded savanna 6.2%, water 3.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 91.6%, hills 6.7%, mountains 1.7%.

Forests covered 14.4% of its land in 2024, down from 15.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Bangladesh shares land borders with India and Myanmar on this map.
