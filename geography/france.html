France: map and facts

France is in Western Europe. It has about 68.7 million people (2025), the 23rd largest population. It covers 606,410 km², the 44th largest by area. That is about 113 people per km². Its highest point is Mont Blanc, at 4,806 m. The longest river on its map is the Rhin, 1,233 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 68,720,337 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 606,410 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 636,000 km².
  • Capital: Paris
  • Highest point: Mont Blanc, 4,806 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 32.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of France, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Rhin: 1,233 km
  • Loire: 1,006 km
  • Maas: 950 km
  • Rhone: 812 km
  • Seine: 777 km
  • Garonne: 647 km
  • Mosel: 544 km
  • Marne: 514 km
  • Lot: 485 km
  • Dordogne: 483 km

Also on the map: Doubs, Tarn, Vienne, Durance, Yonne, Ariege, Maroni, Litani, Schelde and Sane.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mont Blanc: 4,806 m (its highest point)
  • Piton des Neiges: 3,070 m
  • Pic de Coma Pedrosa: 2,942 m
  • Monte Cinto: 2,706 m
  • Puy de Sancy: 1,885 m
  • Cret de la Neige: 1,718 m
  • La Soufriere: 1,467 m
  • Montagne Pelée: 1,397 m

Mountain ranges: Alps, Pyrenees, Ardennes, Jura Mountains, Tumuc-Humac Mountains and Vosges Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Archipel des Comores, Lesser Antilles, Mascarene Islands, Bretagne, Massif Central, Normandie and Riviera.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 28.3%, savanna 18.7%, evergreen broadleaf forest 13.6%, wooded savanna 8.8%, mixed forest 7%, grassland 6.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 55%, hills 27.9%, mountains 14.4%, plateaus 2.3%.

Forests covered 32.9% of its land in 2024, up from 26.8% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in silica sand among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #6 in mica among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

France shares land borders with Andorra, Belgium, Brazil, Germany, Italy, Luxembourg, Monaco, Spain, Suriname and Switzerland on this map.
