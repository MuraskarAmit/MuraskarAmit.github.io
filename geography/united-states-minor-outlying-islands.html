United States Minor Outlying Islands: map and facts

The United States Minor Outlying Islands is in the Pacific Ocean. It covers 28 km². Its highest point is Dunning Hill, at 76 m.

Facts

  • Area: 28 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Highest point: Dunning Hill, 76 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Dunning Hill: 76 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Midway Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): water 44.6%, wetland 30.5%, savanna 9.2%, grassland 3.2%, snow and ice 3.2%, bare ground 3.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Neighbours

The United States Minor Outlying Islands has no land borders on this map.
