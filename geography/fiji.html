Fiji: map and facts

Fiji is in Melanesia. It has 933,154 people (2025), the 157th largest population. It covers 18,333 km², the 151st largest by area. That is about 51 people per km². Its highest point is Tomanivi, at 1,324 m.

Facts

  • Population: 933,154 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 18,333 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 19,000 km².
  • Capital: Suva
  • Highest point: Tomanivi, 1,324 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 61.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Tomanivi: 1,324 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Viti Levu, Vanua Levu, Lau Group and Lau Group.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 70.1%, grassland 9.7%, water 7.1%, wetland 3.6%, farmland 3%, savanna 3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 51.1%, mountains 41%, plains 7.9%.

Forests covered 61.7% of its land in 2024, up from 51.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Fiji has no land borders on this map.
