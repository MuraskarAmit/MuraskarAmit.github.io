Jordan: map and facts

Jordan is in Western Asia. It has about 11.5 million people (2025), the 83rd largest population. It covers 89,318 km², the 110th largest by area. That is about 129 people per km². Its highest point is Jabal Umm ad Dami, at 1,854 m. The longest river on its map is the Jordan, 252 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 11,520,684 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 89,318 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Amman
  • Highest point: Jabal Umm ad Dami, 1,854 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Jordan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Jordan: 252 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Jabal Umm ad Dami: 1,854 m (its highest point)
  • Jabal Ramm: 1,734 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Syrian Desert.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 86.8%, open shrubland 7.6%, farmland 2.9%, grassland 1.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 69.3%, hills 18.6%, mountains 11.1%, plains 1%.

Forests covered 0.8% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #5 in phosphate among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #8 in potash among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Jordan shares land borders with Iraq, Israel, Palestine, Saudi Arabia and Syria on this map.
