San Marino: map and facts

San Marino is in Southern Europe. It has 34,109 people (2025), the 190th largest population. It covers 61 km², the 190th largest by area. That is about 559 people per km². Its highest point is Monte Titano, at 756 m.

Facts

  • Population: 34,109 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 61 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: San Marino
  • Highest point: Monte Titano, 756 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 16.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Monte Titano: 756 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 64.6%, farmland 18.2%, grassland 7.1%, towns and cities 6.1%, farmland with natural plants 2%, wooded savanna 2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 66.7%, hills 33.3%.

Forests covered 16.3% of its land in 2024, down from 16.7% in 1990 (World Bank).
