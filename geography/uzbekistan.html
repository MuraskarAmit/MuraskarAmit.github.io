Uzbekistan: map and facts

Uzbekistan is in Central Asia. It has about 37.1 million people (2025), the 42nd largest population. It covers 448,923 km², the 56th largest by area. That is about 83 people per km². Its highest point is Khazret Sultan, at 4,643 m. The longest river on its map is the Amu Darya, 2,620 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 37,053,428 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 448,923 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Tashkent
  • Highest point: Khazret Sultan, 4,643 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 8.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Uzbekistan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Amu Darya: 2,620 km
  • Syr Darya: 2,212 km
  • Naryn: 807 km

Also on the map: Zarafshon.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Khazret Sultan: 4,643 m (its highest point)
  • Gora Manas: 4,482 m
  • Ayrybaba: 3,139 m

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Fergana Valley, Turan Lowland, Karakum Desert, Kyzylkum Desert and Ustyurt Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 42%, grassland 33.8%, farmland 10.2%, open shrubland 7.3%, water 4.1%, towns and cities 2.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 68.8%, hills 11.3%, plateaus 11.3%, mountains 7.2%, high mountains 1.4%.

Forests covered 8.8% of its land in 2024, up from 6.5% in 1995 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #8 in gold among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Uzbekistan shares land borders with Afghanistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan and Turkmenistan on this map.
