Algeria: map and facts

Algeria is in Northern Africa. It has about 47.4 million people (2025), the 33rd largest population. It covers 2,381,740 km², the 10th largest by area. That is about 20 people per km². Its highest point is Tahat, at 2,908 m.

Facts

  • Population: 47,435,312 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 2,381,740 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 2,310,000 km².
  • Capital: Algiers
  • Highest point: Tahat, 2,908 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 0.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Tahat: 2,908 m (its highest point)
  • Djebel Chelia: 2,328 m
  • Garet el Djenoun: 2,327 m
  • Lalla Khedidja: 2,308 m

Mountain ranges: Atlas Mountains, Ahaggar Mountains, Saharan Atlas and Tell Atlas.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Sahara, Erg Chech, Grand Erg Occidental, Grand Erg Oriental, Hamada du Draa, Tassili n'Ajjer, Tassili oua-n-Ahaggar, Erg Iguidi, Hamada de Tinrhert and Tademait Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 91.6%, open shrubland 4.7%, farmland 1.7%, grassland 1.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 60.8%, plains 18.3%, hills 16.8%, mountains 4.1%.

Forests covered 0.7% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #10 in natural gas among the countries EIA lists (2024)

Neighbours

Algeria shares land borders with Libya, Mali, Mauritania, Morocco, Niger and Tunisia on this map.
