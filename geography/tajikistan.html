Tajikistan: map and facts

Tajikistan is in Central Asia. It has about 10.8 million people (2025), the 90th largest population. It covers 141,379 km², the 94th largest by area. That is about 76 people per km². Its highest point is Pik Imeni Ismail Samani, at 7,495 m. The longest river on its map is the Amu Darya, 2,620 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 10,786,734 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 141,379 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Dushanbe
  • Highest point: Pik Imeni Ismail Samani, 7,495 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 3.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Tajikistan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Amu Darya: 2,620 km
  • Syr Darya: 2,212 km
  • Panj: 921 km
  • Naryn: 807 km

Also on the map: Pamir and Zarafshon.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Pik Imeni Ismail Samani: 7,495 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Pamirs.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 43.6%, bare ground 39.1%, snow and ice 6.4%, farmland 5.2%, open shrubland 4.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): high mountains 61.3%, mountains 26.6%, hills 8.7%, plateaus 3.3%.

Forests covered 3.1% of its land in 2024, up from 2.9% in 1995 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #3 in antimony among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Tajikistan shares land borders with Afghanistan, China, Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan on this map.
