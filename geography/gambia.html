Gambia: map and facts

The Gambia is in Western Africa. It has about 2.82 million people (2025), the 139th largest population. It covers 11,295 km², the 159th largest by area. That is about 250 people per km². The longest river on its map is the Gambia, 1,130 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 2,822,093 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 11,295 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 10,500 km².
  • Capital: Banjul
  • Forest: 21.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

  • Gambia: 1,130 km

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 48.9%, farmland 42.7%, wetland 5.2%, water 1.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 21.3% of its land in 2024, down from 41% in 1990 (World Bank).
