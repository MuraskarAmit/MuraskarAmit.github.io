Palau: map and facts

Palau is in Micronesia. It has 17,663 people (2025), the 191st largest population. It covers 460 km², the 179th largest by area. That is about 38 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Ngerchelchuus, at 242 m.

Facts

  • Population: 17,663 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 460 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 493 km².
  • Capital: Ngerulmud
  • Highest point: Mount Ngerchelchuus, 242 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 90.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Ngerchelchuus: 242 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Palau Islands.

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 69.1%, hills 30.9%.

Forests covered 90.8% of its land in 2024, up from 84.5% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Palau has no land borders on this map.
