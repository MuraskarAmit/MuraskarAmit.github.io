Gibraltar: map and facts

Gibraltar is in Southern Europe. It has 40,126 people (2025). It covers 4 km². That is about 10,032 people per km². Its highest point is Rock of Gibraltar, at 426 m.

Facts

  • Population: 40,126 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 4 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Gibraltar
  • Highest point: Rock of Gibraltar, 426 m (Natural Earth)
  • Forest: 0% of the land (2023, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Rock of Gibraltar: 426 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wetland 75%, towns and cities 25%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 100%.

Forests covered 0% of its land in 2023, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).
