US Naval Base Guantanamo Bay: map and facts

The US Naval Base Guantanamo Bay is in the Caribbean. It covers 65 km².

Facts

  • Area: 65 km² (area as drawn on this map)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 38.8%, savanna 30.6%, wetland 12.9%, towns and cities 11.8%, water 3.5%, bare ground 2.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 81.2%, hills 18.8%.

Neighbours

The US Naval Base Guantanamo Bay shares land borders with Cuba on this map.
