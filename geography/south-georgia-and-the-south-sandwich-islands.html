South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands: map and facts

The South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands is in the South Atlantic. It covers 3,904 km². Its highest point is Mount Paget, at 2,934 m.

Facts

  • Area: 3,904 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: King Edward Point
  • Highest point: Mount Paget, 2,934 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Paget: 2,934 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: South Sandwich Islands.

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 94.1%, hills 5.7%.
