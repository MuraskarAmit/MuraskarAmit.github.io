Belgium: map and facts

Belgium is in Western Europe. It has about 11.9 million people (2025), the 81st largest population. It covers 30,689 km², the 136th largest by area. That is about 389 people per km². Its highest point is Signal de Botrange, at 694 m. The longest river on its map is the Meuse, 950 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 11,941,781 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 30,689 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Brussels
  • Highest point: Signal de Botrange, 694 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 22% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Belgium, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Meuse: 950 km
  • Sauer: 173 km
  • Grote Gete: 51 km

Also on the map: Scheldt.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Signal de Botrange: 694 m (its highest point)
  • Kneiff: 560 m
  • Vaalserberg: 322 m

Mountain ranges: Ardennes.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: North European Plain.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): farmland 32.7%, savanna 23%, mixed forest 18.1%, towns and cities 10.5%, grassland 8%, farmland with natural plants 4.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 68.6%, hills 25.6%, plateaus 5.8%.

Forests covered 22% of its land in 2024, the same as in 2000 (World Bank).

Belgium shares land borders with France, Germany, Luxembourg and Netherlands on this map.
