Bahrain: map and facts

Bahrain is in Western Asia. It has about 1.6 million people (2025), the 149th largest population. It covers 800 km², the 172nd largest by area. That is about 2,000 people per km². Its highest point is Jabal ad Dukhan, at 134 m.

Facts

  • Population: 1,600,366 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 800 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 690 km².
  • Capital: Manama
  • Highest point: Jabal ad Dukhan, 134 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 4.9% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Jabal ad Dukhan: 134 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): bare ground 60.9%, towns and cities 33.3%, water 3.2%, open shrubland 1.3%, grassland 1.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 4.9% of its land in 2024, up from 2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Bahrain has no land borders on this map.
