Bosnia and Herzegovina: map and facts

Bosnia and Herzegovina is in Southern Europe. It has about 3.14 million people (2025), the 132nd largest population. It covers 51,210 km², the 125th largest by area. That is about 61 people per km². Its highest point is Maglic, at 2,386 m. The longest river on its map is the Sava, 945 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 3,140,095 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 51,210 km² (2023, World Bank)
  • Capital: Sarajevo
  • Highest point: Maglic, 2,386 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 42.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Bosnia and Herzegovina, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Sava: 945 km
  • Drina: 346 km
  • Bosna: 282 km
  • Neretva: 225 km
  • Lim: 220 km
  • Una: 207 km

Also on the map: Vrbas.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Maglic: 2,386 m (its highest point)
  • Troglav: 1,913 m
  • Dinara: 1,831 m

Mountain ranges: Dinaric Alps.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Balkan Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): deciduous broadleaf forest 32.7%, wooded savanna 21.4%, savanna 11.9%, grassland 10.5%, mixed forest 8.8%, farmland 7.2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 66.4%, hills 26.8%, plains 6.8%.

Forests covered 42.2% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Bosnia and Herzegovina shares land borders with Croatia, Montenegro and Serbia on this map.
