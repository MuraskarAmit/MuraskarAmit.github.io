Cameroon: map and facts

Cameroon is in Middle Africa. It has about 29.9 million people (2025), the 51st largest population. It covers 475,442 km², the 53rd largest by area. That is about 63 people per km². Its highest point is Mont Cameroun, at 4,040 m. The longest river on its map is the Chari, 1,400 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 29,879,337 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 475,442 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Yaoundé
  • Highest point: Mont Cameroun, 4,040 m (CIA World Factbook (archived))
  • Forest: 40.7% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Cameroon, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Chari: 1,400 km
  • Benue: 1,370 km
  • Logone: 1,000 km
  • Sanaga: 918 km
  • Dja: 720 km
  • Mbam: 425 km
  • Lom: 380 km

Also on the map: Kadéi, Aina and Vina.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mont Cameroun: 4,040 m (its highest point)
  • Mount Oku: 3,011 m
  • Mont Ngaoui: 1,410 m

Mountain ranges: Mbang Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Adamawa Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 46.2%, savanna 26%, farmland 12.2%, wooded savanna 7.1%, grassland 3.3%, farmland with natural plants 2%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 52%, hills 28.5%, mountains 11%, plains 8.5%.

Forests covered 40.7% of its land in 2024, down from 47.6% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Cameroon shares land borders with Central African Republic, Chad, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Nigeria and Republic of the Congo on this map.
