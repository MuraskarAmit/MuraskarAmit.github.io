Northern Mariana Islands: map and facts

The Northern Mariana Islands is in Micronesia. It has 43,541 people (2025). It covers 457 km². That is about 95 people per km².

Facts

  • Population: 43,541 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 457 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 581 km².
  • Capital: Saipan
  • Forest: 53.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Mariana Islands.

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 66.2%, mountains 18.6%, plains 15.2%.

Forests covered 53.3% of its land in 2024, up from 44.2% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The Northern Mariana Islands has no land borders on this map.
