Laos: map and facts

Laos is in South-Eastern Asia. It has about 7.87 million people (2025), the 102nd largest population. It covers 236,800 km², the 82nd largest by area. That is about 33 people per km². Its highest point is Phou Bia, at 2,819 m. The longest river on its map is the Mekong, 4,350 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 7,873,046 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 236,800 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 229,000 km².
  • Capital: Vientiane
  • Highest point: Phou Bia, 2,819 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 56.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Laos, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Mekong: 4,350 km

Also on the map: Sekong, Nam Ou, Ca and Nam Ngum.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Phou Bia: 2,819 m (its highest point)
  • Phu Soi Dao: 2,120 m

Mountain ranges: Annamite Range.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Indochina Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 55.5%, wooded savanna 23.5%, grassland 10.6%, savanna 6.3%, farmland 2.1%, deciduous broadleaf forest 1.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 64.8%, hills 23.2%, plains 11.8%.

Forests covered 56.6% of its land in 2024, down from 62.4% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #6 in potash among the countries USGS lists (2025)
  • World #8 in barite among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Laos shares land borders with Cambodia, China, Myanmar, Thailand and Vietnam on this map.
