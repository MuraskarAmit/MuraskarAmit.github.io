Comoros: map and facts

The Comoros is in Eastern Africa. It has 882,847 people (2025), the 158th largest population. It covers 1,870 km², the 169th largest by area. That is about 472 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Karthala, at 2,361 m.

Facts

  • Population: 882,847 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,870 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 1,680 km².
  • Capital: Moroni
  • Highest point: Mount Karthala, 2,361 m (Natural Earth)
  • Forest: 17.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Karthala: 2,361 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Comoro Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 45%, savanna 17.2%, wooded savanna 15.9%, grassland 8.6%, water 8.5%, wetland 2.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 94.4%, hills 5.6%.

Forests covered 17.8% of its land in 2024, down from 25.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The Comoros has no land borders on this map.
