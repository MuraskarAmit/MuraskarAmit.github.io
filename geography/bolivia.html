Bolivia: map and facts

Bolivia is in South America. It has about 12.6 million people (2025), the 78th largest population. It covers 1,098,580 km², the 27th largest by area. That is about 11 people per km². Its highest point is Nevado Sajama, at 6,542 m. The longest river on its map is the Parana, 4,880 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 12,581,843 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,098,580 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: La Paz, Sucre
  • Highest point: Nevado Sajama, 6,542 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 50.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Bolivia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Parana: 4,880 km
  • Mamoré: 1,930 km
  • Madeira: 1,450 km
  • Guaporé: 1,210 km
  • Pilcomayo: 1,100 km
  • Madre de Dios: 1,060 km
  • Bermejo: 1,060 km
  • Grande: 820 km
  • Yapacani: 440 km

Also on the map: Beni, San Miguel and San Martin.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Nevado Sajama: 6,542 m (its highest point)
  • Nevado Illimani: 6,438 m
  • Serrania de Santiago: 1,555 m
  • Cerro San Miguel: 839 m

Mountain ranges: Andes, Cordillera Oriental, Cordillera Real and Yungas.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Amazon Basin, Gran Chaco, Altiplano, Chaco Boreal, Pantanal and Salar de Uyuni.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 30.7%, grassland 16.1%, savanna 14.4%, deciduous broadleaf forest 14.1%, open shrubland 9.6%, wooded savanna 6.3%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 45%, plateaus 21.3%, high mountains 14.7%, mountains 12.2%, hills 6.8%.

Forests covered 50.2% of its land in 2024, down from 57% in 1990 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels
