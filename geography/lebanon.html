Lebanon: map and facts

Lebanon is in Western Asia. It has about 5.85 million people (2025), the 114th largest population. It covers 10,452 km², the 161st largest by area. That is about 560 people per km². Its highest point is Qurnat as Sawda, at 3,088 m. The longest river on its map is the Jordan, 252 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 5,849,421 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 10,452 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 10,000 km².
  • Capital: Beirut
  • Highest point: Qurnat as Sawda, 3,088 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 13.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Lebanon, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Jordan: 252 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Qurnat as Sawda: 3,088 m (its highest point)
  • Jabal ash Shaykh: 2,814 m

Mountain ranges: Lebanon Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Levant Coast.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 33.7%, farmland 26.3%, open shrubland 21.7%, savanna 10.5%, towns and cities 4.5%, evergreen conifer forest 1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 79.2%, hills 19.1%, high mountains 1%.

Forests covered 13.8% of its land in 2024, up from 13.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Lebanon shares land borders with Israel and Syria on this map.
