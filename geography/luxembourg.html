Luxembourg: map and facts

Luxembourg is in Western Europe. It has 686,970 people (2025), the 162nd largest population. It covers 2,590 km², the 167th largest by area. That is about 265 people per km². Its highest point is Kneiff, at 560 m. The longest river on its map is the Moselle, 544 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 686,970 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 2,590 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Luxembourg
  • Highest point: Kneiff, 560 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 34.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Luxembourg, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Moselle: 544 km
  • Sauer: 173 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Kneiff: 560 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 32.3%, mixed forest 23.7%, farmland 12.8%, grassland 10.1%, wooded savanna 7.7%, towns and cities 5.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 73.6%, plateaus 19.4%, plains 7%.

Forests covered 34.5% of its land in 2024, up from 33.7% in 2000 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Luxembourg shares land borders with Belgium, France and Germany on this map.
