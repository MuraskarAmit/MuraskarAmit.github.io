Bermuda: map and facts

Bermuda is in Northern America. It has 64,555 people (2025). It covers 62 km². That is about 1,041 people per km². Its highest point is Town Hill, at 79 m.

Facts

  • Population: 64,555 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 62 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Capital: Hamilton
  • Highest point: Town Hill, 79 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 14.4% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Town Hill: 79 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wetland 81.1%, water 7.8%, grassland 6.7%, savanna 3.3%, bare ground 1.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 14.4% of its land in 2024, the same as in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Bermuda has no land borders on this map.
