Armenia: map and facts

Armenia is in Western Asia. It has about 3.09 million people (2025), the 134th largest population. It covers 29,740 km², the 138th largest by area. That is about 104 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Aragats, at 4,090 m. The longest river on its map is the Aras, 1,072 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 3,086,700 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 29,740 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Yerevan
  • Highest point: Mount Aragats, 4,090 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 11.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Armenia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Aras: 1,072 km
  • Vorotan: 178 km
  • Hrazdan: 141 km
  • Pambak: 86 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Aragats: 4,090 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Lesser Caucasus.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 72.3%, farmland 8.9%, deciduous broadleaf forest 7.1%, water 4.1%, towns and cities 3.3%, savanna 2.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 63.7%, hills 16.3%, high mountains 14.1%, plateaus 5.9%.

Forests covered 11.8% of its land in 2024, up from 11.7% in 1995 (World Bank).

Minerals and fuels

  • World #6 in molybdenum among the countries USGS lists (2025)

Neighbours

Armenia shares land borders with Azerbaijan, Georgia, Iran and Turkey on this map.
