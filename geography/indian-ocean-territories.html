Indian Ocean Territories: map and facts

The Indian Ocean Territories is in the Indian Ocean. It covers 112 km². Its highest point is Murray Hill, at 361 m.

Facts

  • Area: 112 km² (area as drawn on this map)
  • Highest point: Murray Hill, 361 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Murray Hill: 361 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): evergreen broadleaf forest 53.8%, water 36.3%, grassland 5.3%, wetland 2.3%, bare ground 1.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 88.7%, plains 11.3%.

Neighbours

The Indian Ocean Territories has no land borders on this map.
