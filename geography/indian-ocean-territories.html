Indian Ocean Territories: map and facts

The Indian Ocean Territories is in the Indian Ocean. It has 2,285 people (2021). It covers 149 km². That is about 15 people per km². Its highest point is Murray Hill, at 361 m.

Facts

  • Population: 2,285 (2021, Australian Bureau of Statistics)
  • Area: 149 km² (CIA World Factbook (archived)). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 112 km².
  • Highest point: Murray Hill, 361 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Murray Hill: 361 m (its highest point)

Land

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 88.7%, plains 11.3%.

Neighbours

The Indian Ocean Territories has no land borders on this map.
