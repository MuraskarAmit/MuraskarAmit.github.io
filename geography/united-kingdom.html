United Kingdom: map and facts

The United Kingdom is in Northern Europe. It has about 69.5 million people (2025), the 22nd largest population. It covers 243,610 km², the 78th largest by area. That is about 285 people per km². Its highest point is Ben Nevis, at 1,345 m. The longest river on its map is the Severn, 354 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 69,487,000 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 243,610 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: London
  • Highest point: Ben Nevis, 1,345 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 13.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of the United Kingdom, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Severn: 354 km
  • Thames: 334 km
  • Trent: 298 km
  • Great Ouse: 240 km
  • Wye: 215 km
  • Tay: 193 km
  • Tweed: 156 km
  • Blackwater: 137 km
  • Swale: 118 km
  • Ness: 10 km

Also on the map: Oich, Caledonian Canal and Annalee.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Ben Nevis: 1,345 m (its highest point)
  • Snowdon: 1,085 m

Mountain ranges: Cambrian Mountains and Grampian Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: British Isles, Orkney Islands, Outer Hebrides and Shetland Islands.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 44.2%, farmland 19.7%, grassland 15.5%, mixed forest 6.1%, farmland with natural plants 4.3%, towns and cities 4.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 48.3%, plains 38.7%, mountains 13%.

Forests covered 13.5% of its land in 2024, up from 11.5% in 1990 (World Bank).
