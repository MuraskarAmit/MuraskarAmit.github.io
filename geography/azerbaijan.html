Azerbaijan: map and facts

Azerbaijan is in Western Asia. It has about 10.2 million people (2025), the 94th largest population. It covers 86,600 km², the 112th largest by area. That is about 118 people per km². Its highest point is Bazar Dyuzi, at 4,466 m. The longest river on its map is the Kura, 1,360 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 10,246,996 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 86,600 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Baku
  • Highest point: Bazar Dyuzi, 4,466 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 14.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Azerbaijan, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Kura: 1,360 km
  • Aras: 1,072 km
  • Alazani: 351 km
  • Iori: 320 km
  • Samur: 213 km
  • Terter: 184 km
  • Vorotan: 178 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Bazar Dyuzi: 4,466 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Caucasus Mountains and Lesser Caucasus.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Absheron Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 53.6%, farmland 24%, deciduous broadleaf forest 5.7%, savanna 5.2%, open shrubland 3.7%, wooded savanna 2.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 37.6%, plains 31.2%, hills 27.5%, high mountains 3.1%.

Forests covered 14.6% of its land in 2024, up from 11.6% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Azerbaijan shares land borders with Armenia, Georgia, Iran, Russia and Turkey on this map.
