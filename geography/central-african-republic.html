Central African Republic: map and facts

The Central African Republic is in Middle Africa. It has about 5.51 million people (2025), the 118th largest population. It covers 622,980 km², the 44th largest by area. That is about 8.8 people per km². Its highest point is Mont Ngaoui, at 1,410 m. The longest river on its map is the Ubangi, 1,060 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 5,513,282 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 622,980 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Bangui
  • Highest point: Mont Ngaoui, 1,410 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 72.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of the Central African Republic, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Ubangi: 1,060 km
  • Kotto: 882 km
  • Ouham: 676 km
  • Bahr Aouk: 650 km
  • Lobaye: 520 km
  • Chinko: 420 km

Also on the map: Kadéi, Bamingui, Koukourou, Bomu and Aoukalé.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mont Ngaoui: 1,410 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Chaine des Mongos.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Congo Basin.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 44.7%, evergreen broadleaf forest 20.7%, deciduous broadleaf forest 13.8%, mixed forest 11%, grassland 6.2%, wooded savanna 3.5%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 84.1%, hills 15.8%.

Forests covered 72.5% of its land in 2024, down from 74.5% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

The Central African Republic shares land borders with Cameroon, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Republic of the Congo, South Sudan and Sudan on this map.
