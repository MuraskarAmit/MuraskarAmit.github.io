Tuvalu: map and facts

Tuvalu is in Polynesia. It has 9,492 people (2025), the 193rd largest population. It covers 26 km², the 191st largest by area. That is about 365 people per km².

Facts

  • Population: 9,492 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 26 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 23 km².
  • Capital: Funafuti
  • Forest: 39.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): water 63.9%, wetland 36.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.

Forests covered 39.6% of its land in 2024, up from 34.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Tuvalu has no land borders on this map.
