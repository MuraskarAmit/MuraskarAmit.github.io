Guinea-Bissau: map and facts

Guinea-Bissau is in Western Africa. It has about 2.25 million people (2025), the 144th largest population. It covers 36,125 km², the 134th largest by area. That is about 62 people per km². Its highest point is Monte Torin, at 262 m.

Facts

  • Population: 2,249,515 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 36,125 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 33,000 km².
  • Capital: Bissau
  • Highest point: Monte Torin, 262 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 74.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

Rivers on the map: Geba.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Monte Torin: 262 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Arquipélago dos Bijagós.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 78.7%, wetland 7.3%, grassland 5.6%, wooded savanna 3.4%, water 2%, deciduous broadleaf forest 1.6%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 98.4%, hills 1.6%.

Forests covered 74.8% of its land in 2024, down from 80.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Guinea-Bissau shares land borders with Guinea and Senegal on this map.
