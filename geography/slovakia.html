Slovakia: map and facts

Slovakia is in Eastern Europe. It has about 5.41 million people (2025), the 121st largest population. It covers 49,030 km², the 127th largest by area. That is about 110 people per km². Its highest point is Gerlachovsky stit, at 2,655 m. The longest river on its map is the Danube, 2,850 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 5,413,813 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 49,030 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Bratislava
  • Highest point: Gerlachovsky stit, 2,655 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 40.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Slovakia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Danube: 2,850 km
  • Vah: 433 km
  • Morava: 388 km
  • Hron: 298 km
  • Hornad: 286 km
  • Dunajec: 247 km
  • Ipel: 233 km
  • Slana: 229 km
  • Latorica: 182 km

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Gerlachovsky stit: 2,655 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Carpathian Mountains and Tatra Mountains.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): deciduous broadleaf forest 35%, farmland 31.4%, mixed forest 10.4%, grassland 6.5%, wooded savanna 6.1%, savanna 5.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 39.3%, mountains 38.3%, plains 22.3%.

Forests covered 40.3% of its land in 2024, up from 39.5% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Slovakia shares land borders with Austria, Czechia, Hungary, Poland and Ukraine on this map.
