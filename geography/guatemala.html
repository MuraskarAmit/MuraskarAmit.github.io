Guatemala: map and facts

Guatemala is in Central America. It has about 18.7 million people (2025), the 69th largest population. It covers 108,889 km², the 105th largest by area. That is about 172 people per km². Its highest point is Volcan Tajumulco, at 4,203 m. The longest river on its map is the Usumacinta, 560 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 18,687,881 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 108,889 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Guatemala City
  • Highest point: Volcan Tajumulco, 4,203 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 33.1% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Guatemala, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Usumacinta: 560 km
  • Motagua: 486 km
  • Chixoy: 400 km
  • Pasión: 353 km
  • Polochic: 194 km
  • San Pedro: 186 km
  • Hondo: 150 km
  • Paz: 134 km
  • Dulce: 111 km

Also on the map: Cuilco, Rio Salinas and San Gregorio.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Volcan Tajumulco: 4,203 m (its highest point)
  • Volcan Tacana: 4,064 m

Mountain ranges: Sierra Chiapas and Maya Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Central America and Yucatan Peninsula.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 34.7%, evergreen broadleaf forest 34.4%, savanna 18.4%, grassland 2.9%, farmland 2.8%, farmland with natural plants 2.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 43%, plains 30.1%, hills 20.7%, high mountains 4%, plateaus 2.2%.

Forests covered 33.1% of its land in 2024, down from 45.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Guatemala shares land borders with Belize, El Salvador, Honduras and Mexico on this map.
