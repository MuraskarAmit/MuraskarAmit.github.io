Aland: map and facts

Aland is in Northern Europe. It has 30,836 people (2025). It covers 1,583 km². That is about 19 people per km². Its highest point is Orrdalsklint, at 127 m.

Facts

  • Population: 30,836 (2025, Statistics Finland)
  • Area: 1,583 km² (UN Demographic Yearbook). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 937 km².
  • Capital: Mariehamn
  • Highest point: Orrdalsklint, 127 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Orrdalsklint: 127 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): wooded savanna 52.2%, evergreen conifer forest 29.1%, mixed forest 7.3%, water 5.6%, wetland 4%, grassland 1.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 100%.
