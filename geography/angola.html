Angola: map and facts

Angola is in Middle Africa. It has about 39 million people (2025), the 39th largest population. It covers 1,246,700 km², the 22nd largest by area. That is about 31 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Moco, at 2,620 m. The longest river on its map is the Congo, 4,700 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 39,040,039 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 1,246,700 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Luanda
  • Highest point: Mount Moco, 2,620 m (Natural Earth)
  • Forest: 51.2% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Angola, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Congo: 4,700 km
  • Zambezi: 2,574 km
  • Kasai: 2,272 km
  • Kwango: 1,770 km
  • Okavango: 1,600 km
  • Cunene: 1,050 km
  • Cuito: 1,000 km
  • Cuanza: 965 km
  • Kwilu: 965 km
  • Cuando: 731 km

Also on the map: Lungwebungu, Inkisi and Lucala.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Moco: 2,620 m (its highest point)

Mountain ranges: Crystal Mountains.

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Congo Basin, Namib Desert, Bié Plateau and Huíla Plateau.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 35.3%, savanna 32.5%, wooded savanna 18.2%, evergreen broadleaf forest 5.5%, mixed forest 1.9%, deciduous broadleaf forest 1.7%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plateaus 58.5%, hills 33%, plains 4.3%, mountains 4.2%.

Forests covered 51.2% of its land in 2024, down from 62.2% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Angola shares land borders with Democratic Republic of the Congo, Namibia, Republic of the Congo and Zambia on this map.
