Slovenia: map and facts

Slovenia is in Southern Europe. It has about 2.13 million people (2025), the 145th largest population. It covers 20,480 km², the 150th largest by area. That is about 104 people per km². Its highest point is Triglav, at 2,864 m. The longest river on its map is the Sava, 945 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 2,130,986 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 20,480 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Ljubljana
  • Highest point: Triglav, 2,864 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 61.8% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Slovenia, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Sava: 945 km
  • Mur: 434 km
  • Ljubljanica: 41 km

Also on the map: Drava.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Triglav: 2,864 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): deciduous broadleaf forest 33.4%, mixed forest 25%, wooded savanna 17.3%, savanna 9.7%, farmland 5.4%, grassland 3.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): mountains 59.7%, hills 31.2%, plains 8.1%, plateaus 1%.

Forests covered 61.8% of its land in 2024, up from 60.1% in 1995 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Slovenia shares land borders with Austria, Croatia, Hungary and Italy on this map.
