Paraguay: map and facts

Paraguay is in South America. It has about 7.01 million people (2025), the 106th largest population. It covers 406,752 km², the 59th largest by area. That is about 17 people per km². Its highest point is Cerro Tres Kandu, at 842 m. The longest river on its map is the Parana, 2,570 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 7,013,078 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 406,752 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Asunción
  • Highest point: Cerro Tres Kandu, 842 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 36.5% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Paraguay, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Parana: 2,570 km
  • Pilcomayo: 1,500 km

Also on the map: Verde and Jejui Guazu.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Cerro Tres Kandu: 842 m (its highest point)

Plateaus, plains, deserts and other regions: Gran Chaco and Chaco Boreal.

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): savanna 61%, grassland 12.6%, wooded savanna 12.3%, farmland 4.6%, farmland with natural plants 3.9%, evergreen broadleaf forest 2.4%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 88.3%, hills 6.7%, plateaus 5%.

Forests covered 36.5% of its land in 2024, down from 64.6% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Paraguay shares land borders with Argentina, Bolivia and Brazil on this map.
