Vatican: map and facts

The Vatican is in Southern Europe. It has 496 people (2023), the smallest population. Its highest point is Vatican Hill, at 75 m.

Facts

  • Population: 496 (2023, UN World Population Prospects)
  • Capital: Vatican City
  • Highest point: Vatican Hill, 75 m (Wikidata)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:
