Burkina Faso: map and facts

Burkina Faso is in Western Africa. It has about 24.1 million people (2025), the 59th largest population. It covers 274,200 km², the 73rd largest by area. That is about 88 people per km². Its highest point is Téna Kourou, at 749 m. The longest river on its map is the Mouhoun, 1,352 km from source to mouth.

Facts

  • Population: 24,074,580 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 274,200 km² (2024, World Bank)
  • Capital: Ouagadougou
  • Highest point: Téna Kourou, 749 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 12.3% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Rivers

The longest rivers on the map of Burkina Faso, with the length of each whole river (Wikidata):

  • Mouhoun: 1,352 km
  • Komoé: 900 km
  • Oti: 900 km

Also on the map: Nakanbé.

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Téna Kourou: 749 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 94.6%, farmland 4.9%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): plains 52.4%, plateaus 44.5%, hills 3.1%.

Forests covered 12.3% of its land in 2024, down from 30.3% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Burkina Faso shares land borders with Benin, Cote d'Ivoire, Ghana, Mali, Niger and Togo on this map.
