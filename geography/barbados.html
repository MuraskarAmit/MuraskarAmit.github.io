Barbados: map and facts

Barbados is in the Caribbean. It has 282,623 people (2025), the 173rd largest population. It covers 431 km², the 182nd largest by area. That is about 656 people per km². Its highest point is Mount Hillaby, at 340 m.

Facts

  • Population: 282,623 (2025, World Bank)
  • Area: 431 km² (2024, World Bank). This figure covers a different area from the one this map shows: as drawn, about 447 km².
  • Capital: Bridgetown
  • Highest point: Mount Hillaby, 340 m (Wikidata)
  • Forest: 14.6% of the land (2024, World Bank)

Mountains and landscapes

Its highest peaks:

  • Mount Hillaby: 340 m (its highest point)

Land

Land cover seen from satellites (NASA MODIS, 2024): grassland 48.9%, farmland 26.4%, towns and cities 8.2%, savanna 7.5%, water 5.4%, evergreen broadleaf forest 2.1%.

Landforms, worked out from the height of the land (USGS GMTED2010): hills 89.9%, plains 10.1%.

Forests covered 14.6% of its land in 2024, down from 14.7% in 1990 (World Bank).

Neighbours

Barbados has no land borders on this map.
