Squaring numbers close to 100 or 1000

यावदूनं तावदूनीकृत्य वर्गं च योजयेत् Yāvadūnaṃ Tāvadūnīkṛtya Vargañca Yojayet “whatever the deficiency, lessen by it, and set up its square”, a sub-sutra of Vedic maths. The book also calls it the Yāvadūnam sutra, after sutra 10, “by the deficiency”.

A square is a number multiplied by itself, so the base method works for squares too. Vedic maths gives squares a rule of their own: lessen the number by its deficiency from the base, and set the square of the deficiency beside it.

The method

  1. Find how far the number is from the base: 96 is 4 below 100; 107 is 7 above.
  2. Left part: the number, lessened by the deficiency (96 − 4 = 92), or grown by the excess (107 + 7 = 114).
  3. Right part: the square of the difference: 4² = 16, 7² = 49.
  4. The right part has as many digits as the base has zeros. Fill it with a 0 in front, or carry the extra to the left part.

Worked examples

96²

  1. 96 is 4 below 100.96: −4
  2. Left part: take the difference away from the number.96 − 4 = 92
  3. Right part: square the difference.4² = 16

96² = 92 | 16 = 9216

107²

  1. 107 is 7 above 100.107: +7
  2. Left part: add the difference to the number.107 + 7 = 114
  3. Right part: square the difference.7² = 49

107² = 114 | 49 = 11449

89²

  1. 89 is 11 below 100.89: −11
  2. Left part: take the difference away from the number.89 − 11 = 78
  3. Right part: square the difference.11² = 121
  4. The right part can only have 2 digits: carry 1 to the left part.78 | 121 = 79 | 21

89² = 79 | 21 = 7921

988²

  1. 988 is 12 below 1000.988: −12
  2. Left part: take the difference away from the number.988 − 12 = 976
  3. Right part: square the difference.12² = 144

988² = 976 | 144 = 976144

Why it works

Write the number as B + d, where B is the base and d the difference (negative below the base). Then (B + d)² = B² + 2Bd + d² = B × (B + 2d) + d². And B + 2d is the number plus its difference once more: (B + d) + d.

Watch out

  • The right part needs exactly as many digits as the base has zeros: 103² is 106 | 09, not 106 | 9.
  • Far from the base, the square of the difference carries: 89² gives 78 | 121, which is 7921.
  • For numbers far from any base, the duplex works for every number.
