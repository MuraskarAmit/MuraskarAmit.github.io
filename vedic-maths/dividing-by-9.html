Dividing by 9 with running totals

निखिलं नवतश्चरमं दशतः Nikhilaṃ Navataścaramaṃ Daśataḥ “all from 9 and the last from 10”, a sutra of Vedic maths. Dividing by 9 is the simplest case of the Nikhilam way to divide.

Because 9 is one less than 10, dividing by 9 needs no times tables: the answer comes from adding the digits as you go along, and the remainder is the total of all the digits.

The method

  1. The first digit of the answer is the first digit of the number.
  2. Each next digit of the answer is the one before plus the next digit of the number.
  3. Stop one digit before the end. The remainder is the last total plus the last digit.
  4. A total of 10 or more carries 1 to the left.
  5. A remainder of 9 or more holds a 9: add 1 to the answer for each 9, and keep what is left as the remainder.

Worked examples

2312 ÷ 9

  1. The answer starts with the first digit.2
  2. Add the next digit each time, and stop one digit before the end.2 + 3 = 5, 5 + 1 = 6
  3. The remainder: the last total plus the last digit.6 + 2 = 8

2312 ÷ 9 = 256 remainder 8

1235 ÷ 9

  1. The answer starts with the first digit.1
  2. Add the next digit each time, and stop one digit before the end.1 + 2 = 3, 3 + 3 = 6
  3. The remainder: the last total plus the last digit.6 + 5 = 11
  4. The remainder holds a 9, so add 1 to the answer.11 = 9 + 2 → 136 + 1 = 137

1235 ÷ 9 = 137 remainder 2

4567 ÷ 9

  1. The answer starts with the first digit.4
  2. Add the next digit each time, and stop one digit before the end.4 + 5 = 9, 9 + 6 = 15
  3. The remainder: the last total plus the last digit.15 + 7 = 22
  4. A total over 9 carries to the left.4 | 9 | 15: 15: keep 5, carry 1; 9 + 1 = 10: keep 0, carry 1; 4 + 1 = 5 → 505
  5. The remainder holds 2 nines, so add 2 to the answer.22 = 2 × 9 + 4 → 505 + 2 = 507

4567 ÷ 9 = 507 remainder 4

Why it works

10 = 9 + 1, 100 = 99 + 1 and 1000 = 999 + 1. So 2312 = 2 × 999 + 3 × 99 + 1 × 9 + (2 + 3 + 1 + 2). Divided by 9, that is 2 × 111 + 3 × 11 + 1 × 1 = 222 + 33 + 1 = 256, remainder 2 + 3 + 1 + 2 = 8.

Add 222 + 33 + 1 place by place and you get 2, 2 + 3 and 2 + 3 + 1: the running totals.

Watch out

  • This shortcut is for 9 only. Dividing by 8 or 98 uses the general Nikhilam division, which works with the complement (2 for 8) and takes longer.
  • Don’t forget the last step: a remainder of 9 or more isn’t finished.
