# Make Me Smarter

Quick daily practice for mental maths, speed tricks, memory techniques, reasoning and general knowledge. A few minutes a day makes you faster and more accurate at what you practise.

- Six tracks and 83 levels: from 3 + 4 to long multiplication, memory palaces and knights and knaves
- Short lessons, hints when you're stuck, and spaced review of your mistakes
- Works offline, with no account: your progress stays on your phone

Coming soon to Google Play.
