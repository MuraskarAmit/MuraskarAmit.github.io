# Delete your data

Make Me Smarter has no accounts, and we keep no copy of your data. Your progress and notes are on your device, and in your own Google Drive if you turned on Google Drive sync. You can delete all of it yourself.

**In the Android app or on the website**, under **Settings → Your data**:
- **Reset progress** deletes your stars, XP and history.
- **Delete all notes** deletes your Retention Notes, with their photos and voice recordings.
- **Stop syncing**, with **Also delete the copy in Google Drive** ticked, deletes the copy in your Google Drive.

**Everything at once:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android's settings. On the website, clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

**The copy in your Google Drive, from Google's side:** open Google Drive's **Settings** (the gear) → **Manage apps** → Make Me Smarter → **Options** → **Delete hidden app data**. To take back the app's access to your Drive on all your devices, remove it at [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

**Ads:** information collected by Google's ads software is held by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and turn off personalised ads at [google.com/settings/ads](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

**Need help?** We don't hold your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side, but we're happy to help with the steps above: [muraskaramit@gmail.com](mailto:muraskaramit@gmail.com).
