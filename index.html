How it works

A few minutes a level

Short levels, a lesson before each new method, and a second look at what you missed.

  1. 1

    Pick a game

    Maths, tricks, memory, reasoning or general knowledge: start with whichever you like.

  2. 2

    Learn the method

    Tricks and techniques start with a short lesson: try an example, then see the method and why it works.

  3. 3

    Practise a level

    Each level is a short set of questions to answer from memory, and the levels open one after another.

Misses come back

A miss shows the right answer, and the questions you miss come back later for review.

Retention Notes

Write down anything you want to remember, with a photo or a voice clip, and it comes back for review on day 1, 3, 7 and later.

Your practice adds up

Streaks, XP, achievements and stats, day after day.

No account

Your progress stays in your browser. If you like, keep a copy in your own Google Drive and pick up where you left off on another device.

Squares ending in 5

65² = ?

  1. Take the 6 and multiply it by the next number up: 6 × 7 = 42.
  2. Write 25 after it.

65² = 4225

Try one: 85²

8 × 9 = 72, then 25: 7225. Why it works: squares ending in 5.
