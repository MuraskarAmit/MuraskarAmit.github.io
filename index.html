How it works

  • A few minutes a level. Each level is a short set of questions to answer from memory, and the levels open one after another.
  • The method first. Tricks and techniques start with a short lesson: try an example, then see the method and why it works.
  • Misses come back. A miss shows the right answer, and the questions you miss come back later for review.
  • Retention Notes. Write down anything you want to remember, with a photo or a voice clip, and it comes back for review on day 1, 3, 7 and later.
  • Your practice adds up in streaks, XP, achievements and stats.
  • No account. Your progress stays in your browser. If you like, keep a copy in your own Google Drive and pick up where you left off on another device.
