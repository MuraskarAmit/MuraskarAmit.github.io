Map quiz: countries, capitals, rivers and peaks

Countries on the world map, region by region: tap the country a question names, name the one that is highlighted, and match capitals, neighbours, rivers and highest peaks, with a level on the rivers and peaks of Southern Asia. Each answer shows the right place on the map, and the levels open one after another. India’s boundaries are drawn according to India’s official position, simplified for small-scale maps (data: Natural Earth, India view).
