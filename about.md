# About Make Me Smarter

Make Me Smarter is daily practice for mental maths, speed tricks, memory techniques, reasoning and general knowledge: short sets of questions to answer from memory. It is made by makemesmarter.

- **Six games, 83 levels:** [Maths](/games/maths/), [Reasoning](/games/reasoning/), [Memory](/games/memory/), [Everyday Maths](/games/everyday-maths/), [General Knowledge](/games/general-knowledge/) and [Speed Tricks](/games/speed-tricks/).
- **Free, with no sign-up:** [play in your browser](/play/), on a phone or a computer. Your progress stays on your device. The web version shows a few ads from Google AdSense, never while you play.
- **English and Hindi:** choose the language in Settings.
- **Android app:** coming soon to Google Play.

How the practice is designed, and what it can and can't do: [How it helps](/how-it-helps/).
